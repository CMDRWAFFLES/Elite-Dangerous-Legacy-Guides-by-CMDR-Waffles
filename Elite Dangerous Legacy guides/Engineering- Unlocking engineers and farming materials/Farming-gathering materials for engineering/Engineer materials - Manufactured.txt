ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
ENGINEER MATERIALS — MANUFACTURED
HIGH GRADE EMISSIONS FARMING
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
PURPOSE OF THIS GUIDE
===============================================================================

This guide explains how to find and repeatedly farm Grade 5 Manufactured
engineering materials from High Grade Emissions signal sources.

It is written specifically for:

- Xbox Legacy
- PlayStation Legacy
- Horizons 3.8 / pre-Update 14 mechanics

This is not a Live/Odyssey HGE guide.

===============================================================================
IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY
===============================================================================

HGE
- High Grade Emissions.
- A temporary signal source found in supercruise.
- HGEs contain Manufactured engineering materials.

MINOR FACTION
- A local faction operating inside a star system.
- A system can contain several minor factions at the same time.
- Each faction can have its own allegiance and state.

FACTION ALLEGIANCE
- Empire
- Federation
- Alliance
- Independent

FACTION STATE
Examples include:
- Boom
- War
- Civil War
- Civil Unrest
- Outbreak

POWERPLAY POWER
Examples include:
- Aisling Duval
- Edmund Mahon
- Li Yong-Rui
- Archon Delaine

Powerplay is a separate system.

The Powerplay ruler or exploiter does not determine HGE materials.

SYSTEM GOVERNMENT
Examples include:
- Democracy
- Dictatorship
- Corporate
- Anarchy

Government does not determine the HGE material family.

SYSTEM ECONOMY
Examples include:
- Industrial
- High Tech
- Refinery
- Extraction

Economy does not determine the HGE material family.

===============================================================================
THE MOST IMPORTANT HGE RULE
===============================================================================

The material inside an HGE is determined by the MINOR FACTION attached to that
specific signal source.

Do not rely only on:

- The controlling faction
- The system allegiance shown in the Galaxy Map
- The main system state
- The system government
- The system economy
- The Powerplay ruler
- A current Live-PC website

When you target an HGE, the information panel should show details such as:

- Faction
- State
- Time remaining

That specific faction and state are what matter.

A system can contain several HGEs attached to different factions, so two HGEs
inside the same system can contain completely different material families.

===============================================================================
WHAT EACH REQUIREMENT MEANS
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPERIAL SHIELDING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE MUST BELONG TO AN EMPIRE-ALIGNED MINOR FACTION.

The entire system does not have to be shown as Empire if an Empire faction
inside that system owns the HGE.

Cleanest search:

- Look for an Empire-aligned faction.
- Prefer one without Boom, War, Civil War, Civil Unrest or Outbreak.
- This reduces competition from state-based HGE families.

Possible companion or competing result:

- Other faction-state materials can appear when that Empire faction is also in
  a relevant special state.

Powerplay clarification:

- It does not matter whether the system is controlled or exploited by Aisling
  Duval, Edmund Mahon or another Power.
- The local minor faction allegiance is what matters.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE DYNAMICS COMPOSITES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE MUST BELONG TO A FEDERATION-ALIGNED MINOR FACTION.

Cleanest search:

- Look for a Federation-aligned faction.
- Prefer one without a competing Boom, War, Civil War, Civil Unrest or Outbreak
  state.

Common companion material:

- Proprietary Composites, Grade 4

Possible complication:

- A Federal faction in Boom can produce the Federal material family or the
  Boom Proto family.
- A Federal faction in War can compete with the Military family.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTO RADIOLIC ALLOYS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN BOOM.

Faction allegiance:

- Independent is valid.
- Alliance is valid.
- Federation is valid.
- Empire is valid.

Independent or Alliance is preferred because those allegiances do not add the
Federal or Imperial allegiance-specific HGE families.

Possible results from a suitable Boom HGE:

- Proto Radiolic Alloys
- Proto Heat Radiators
- Proto Light Alloys

You cannot force a Boom HGE to contain only Proto Radiolic Alloys.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTO HEAT RADIATORS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN BOOM.

The same rules apply as for Proto Radiolic Alloys.

Preferred search:

- Independent faction in Boom
- Alliance faction in Boom

The Powerplay ruler is irrelevant.

Example:

A system can be:

- Independent
- Democracy
- Industrial / High Tech
- Exploited by Aisling Duval
- In Boom

This is a valid Proto-material search system.

The only final check is whether the specific HGE belongs to a faction whose
state is Boom.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MILITARY GRADE ALLOYS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN WAR OR CIVIL WAR.

Faction allegiance is not required.

Preferred search:

- Independent or Alliance faction in War/Civil War
- This avoids adding Federal or Imperial allegiance materials to the possible
  pool.

Possible competing result:

- Military Supercapacitors

You may need to inspect several suitable HGEs before finding the one you need.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MILITARY SUPERCAPACITORS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN WAR OR CIVIL WAR.

Faction allegiance is not required.

Possible competing result:

- Military Grade Alloys

The system can contain an active human Conflict Zone, but entering a Conflict
Zone is not part of HGE farming.

The HGE remains a separate signal source found in supercruise.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPROVISED COMPONENTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN CIVIL UNREST.

Faction allegiance is not required.

Preferred search:

- Independent or Alliance faction in Civil Unrest

Civil Unrest is relatively uncommon, which can make this material awkward to
find directly in the separate Legacy BGS.

When the correct state is unavailable, farming an easier G5 material and
cross-trading may be faster.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHARMACEUTICAL ISOLATORS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED:

    THE HGE FACTION MUST BE IN OUTBREAK.

Faction allegiance is not required.

Preferred search:

- Independent or Alliance faction in Outbreak

Outbreak is relatively uncommon and may exist on a secondary faction rather
than the controlling faction.

Target every HGE and read its attached faction and state before deciding
whether to enter it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIOTECH CONDUCTORS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE:

    MISSION REWARDS

Biotech Conductors are not a normal HGE material.

Look for them as a selectable mission reward after building reputation with
local factions.

Traditional Legacy method:

    Robigo Mines -> Sirius Atmospherics passenger missions

Other high-reputation mission boards can also offer them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXQUISITE FOCUS CRYSTALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE:

    MISSION REWARDS

Exquisite Focus Crystals are not a normal HGE material.

They can appear on the same kinds of high-reputation mission boards used for
Biotech Conductors.

Robigo passenger missions are a traditional Legacy source.

===============================================================================
REQUIRED VERSUS PREFERRED
===============================================================================

This distinction is important.

EXAMPLE 1:

Material wanted:
- Proto Heat Radiators

Required:
- The HGE faction is in Boom.

Preferred:
- The HGE faction is Independent or Alliance.

Independent is not the actual requirement.

Boom is the requirement.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLE 2:

Material wanted:
- Imperial Shielding

Required:
- The HGE belongs to an Empire-aligned minor faction.

Preferred:
- That faction has no competing state such as Boom or War.

The system's Powerplay controller is irrelevant.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLE 3:

Material wanted:
- Pharmaceutical Isolators

Required:
- The HGE faction is in Outbreak.

Preferred:
- Independent or Alliance allegiance, to reduce unrelated allegiance-based
  HGE results.

===============================================================================
WHAT DOES NOT MATTER
===============================================================================

POWERPLAY

Ignore:

- Aisling Duval
- Edmund Mahon
- Zachary Hudson
- Felicia Winters
- Li Yong-Rui
- Archon Delaine
- Other Powerplay control or exploitation

Powerplay does not decide HGE contents.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GOVERNMENT

Examples such as Democracy, Corporate, Dictatorship or Anarchy do not decide
the HGE material family.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ECONOMY

Industrial, High Tech, Extraction and other economy types do not decide the
HGE material family.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

POPULATION

Population is not a required material condition.

A system with three million inhabitants can be a valid HGE system.

A much larger population does not guarantee:

- More HGEs
- A longer timer
- The desired material
- A faster respawn

Do not reject a suitable faction merely because the population is below an
arbitrary number quoted by another guide.

===============================================================================
HOW TO SEARCH FOR A SUITABLE SYSTEM
===============================================================================

Because Legacy has its own separate BGS, current Live-PC faction-state websites
can be wrong for Xbox and PlayStation.

Use the in-game Legacy Galaxy Map as the starting point.

1. Decide which G5 material you need.

2. Identify its required allegiance or faction state from the table.

3. Use the Galaxy Map to look for a promising system.

4. Enter the system.

5. Scan the Navigation Beacon, or use the FSS, to reveal signal sources.

6. Enter supercruise.

7. Find and target a High Grade Emissions signal.

8. Read the signal information.

9. Confirm:
   - Faction
   - State
   - Time remaining

10. Enter only when the signal matches the material requirement.

Important:

The Galaxy Map usually helps you choose a promising system, but the individual
HGE is the final authority.

===============================================================================
EXAMPLE: AN INDEPENDENT BOOM SYSTEM
===============================================================================

Suppose a system is shown as:

- Allegiance: Independent
- Population: 3 million
- Government: Democracy
- State: Boom
- Economy: Industrial / High Tech
- Powerplay: Aisling Duval or Edmund Mahon territory

This is a valid system to search for:

- Proto Heat Radiators
- Proto Radiolic Alloys
- Proto Light Alloys

Why:

- Boom is the relevant material condition.
- Independent avoids Empire and Federation allegiance competition.
- Population is acceptable but not the deciding requirement.
- Democracy is irrelevant.
- Industrial / High Tech is irrelevant.
- Powerplay is irrelevant.

Still target the HGE itself and confirm its faction is in Boom.

===============================================================================
HGE TIMER AND DISTANCE
===============================================================================

Every HGE has a time remaining value.

The signal disappears when the timer expires.

The timer continues while you:

- Travel toward it
- Collect materials
- Close and restart the game

There is no guaranteed fixed respawn schedule for a replacement HGE.

New signal sources are generated dynamically.

Do not sit beside an expired location expecting the same HGE to reappear after
a known number of minutes.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO SKIP AN HGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Skip it when:

- The signal is several thousand light-seconds away
- Only a few minutes remain
- You cannot arrive before expiration
- There is not enough time for a console restart loop

Example:

    DISTANCE: 5,000 ls
    TIME REMAINING: 3 minutes

This is usually not worth travelling to unless the supercruise arrival estimate
clearly shows that you can enter before the timer reaches zero.

Even if you reach it, there is unlikely to be enough time for repeated full
application restarts.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TIMER TARGETS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bare minimum for one collection:
- Enough time to arrive and collect

Reasonable for farming:
- Approximately 15 minutes or more

Preferred:
- Approximately 20 minutes or more

Excellent:
- 25–35 minutes remaining

The more console restart cycles you intend to perform, the more time you need.

===============================================================================
LEGACY-CONSOLE HGE FARMING LOOP
===============================================================================

FIT A SHIP WITH:

[ ] Collector Limpet Controller
[ ] Cargo Rack
[ ] Approximately 30–50 limpets
[ ] Fuel Scoop
[ ] Good supercruise handling
[ ] Supercruise Assist, useful but optional

A small or medium ship is convenient, but any ship that carries the required
limpets and controller can be used.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST COLLECTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Enter a suitable Legacy system.

2. Scan the Navigation Beacon or use the FSS.

3. Find an HGE with plenty of time remaining.

4. Target it.

5. Verify the attached faction and state.

6. Drop into the HGE.

7. Open the Cargo Hatch.

8. Release collector limpets.

9. Collect every Manufactured material fragment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONSOLE RESTART LOOP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Return to the Xbox or PlayStation dashboard.

2. Fully close Elite Dangerous.

3. Restart Elite Dangerous.

4. Enter the same game mode.

5. Immediately enter supercruise with the throttle at zero.

6. The previous HGE should be close behind the ship as an unidentified signal.

7. Turn around.

8. Target the signal.

9. Drop back into it.

10. Collect the respawned materials.

11. Repeat until:
    - The timer expires
    - Your material storage reaches 100
    - You run out of limpets

Important:

Exiting only to Elite's main menu is not the same as fully closing the console
application for this farming method.

===============================================================================
WHY LIVE-PC HGE VIDEOS MAY LOOK DIFFERENT
===============================================================================

Later Live-PC engineering changes increased the quantity available from HGEs.

Legacy consoles retain the older, smaller HGE quantity.

Ignore videos in which one modern Live HGE immediately fills an entire G5 bin.

On Xbox and PlayStation Legacy, the full application-restart loop remains
useful.

===============================================================================
HGE CONTENTS AND COMPANION MATERIALS
===============================================================================

Some HGEs contain more than one material in the same family.

Common examples include:

- Core Dynamics Composites with Proprietary Composites
- Proto Radiolic Alloys with Proto Light Alloys
- Proto Heat Radiators with other Proto-family material
- Military Grade Alloys or Military Supercapacitors

Do not assume every fragment in an HGE is the same grade or exact material.

Check the Contacts panel when necessary.

===============================================================================
WHAT TO DO WHEN THE CORRECT HGE WILL NOT APPEAR
===============================================================================

1. Check every HGE in the system.

2. Confirm that you are reading the faction attached to the signal.

3. Check whether another faction in the system has the required state.

4. Jump to another suitable Legacy system.

5. Scan its Navigation Beacon.

6. Continue searching.

7. Consider farming an easier G5 donor and using a Manufactured Material
   Trader.

Do not wait indefinitely in one system for a guaranteed respawn.

===============================================================================
MATERIAL TRADING
===============================================================================

A Manufactured Material Trader can exchange one Manufactured material for
another.

Most efficient use:

- Farm a direct G5 source.
- Trade downward within the same material family.
- Cross-trade only when the direct source is too difficult or unavailable.

Cross-category G5 trading has a poor exchange rate.

Imperial Shielding is often used as a universal donor because Empire-aligned
HGEs are comparatively easy to locate.

Trading is especially useful for:

- Pharmaceutical Isolators
- Improvised Components
- A missing Military material
- A missing Proto material

===============================================================================
THE TWO MISSION-ONLY G5 MATERIALS
===============================================================================

BIOTECH CONDUCTORS

EXQUISITE FOCUS CRYSTALS

These are most efficiently collected as mission rewards.

Traditional Legacy route:

    Robigo Mines -> Sirius Atmospherics passenger missions

Once allied with the Robigo factions:

1. Inspect each passenger mission.

2. Check every reward option.

3. Select Biotech Conductors or Exquisite Focus Crystals when offered.

Robigo can also offer Modified Embedded Firmware.

Modified Embedded Firmware is Encoded Data, not a Manufactured material.

Other high-reputation mission boards can also offer these materials, so Robigo
is useful rather than mandatory.

===============================================================================
WHERE DAV'S HOPE FITS
===============================================================================

Dav's Hope is a permanent planetary relog location for Manufactured materials.

System:
- Hyades Sector DR-V c2-23

Body:
- A 5

Coordinates:
- +44.8180, -31.3893

REQUIRED:

[ ] Planetary Vehicle Hangar
[ ] SRV
[ ] Planetary landing capability
[ ] Fuel Scoop recommended

PROCEDURE:

1. Land at Dav's Hope.

2. Deploy the SRV.

3. Drive the circuit around the abandoned settlement.

4. Scoop the Manufactured materials.

5. Return to the main menu.

6. Reload.

7. Repeat the circuit.

Dav's Hope mainly produces a mixture of Grade 2–Grade 4 materials.

It is excellent for:

- Early engineering
- General mid-grade stocking
- New commanders without an HGE farming ship

It is less efficient than repeatedly farming Grade 5 HGEs and trading downward
when maximum efficiency is the goal.

===============================================================================
BEGINNER TROUBLESHOOTING
===============================================================================

NO HGES APPEAR

Check:

[ ] Navigation Beacon scanned or FSS used
[ ] You are in supercruise
[ ] Navigation-panel signal-source filters are enabled
[ ] The system contains a faction with a suitable condition
[ ] You are using Legacy in-game information, not current Live states

Remember:

A suitable faction creates the possibility of an HGE.

It does not guarantee that one will be present immediately.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SYSTEM LOOKS CORRECT BUT THE HGE IS WRONG

The HGE may belong to a different faction.

Target it and read:

- Faction
- State

Do not rely only on the system description.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE HGE EXPIRED BEFORE I ARRIVED

Move to another HGE or another suitable system.

There is no guaranteed wait time for the same HGE to respawn.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MY LIMPETS WILL NOT COLLECT

Check:

[ ] Cargo Hatch is open
[ ] Collector Limpet Controller is powered
[ ] Limpets are in the cargo hold
[ ] Materials are not manually ignored in the Contacts panel
[ ] You are within controller range

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE G5 STORAGE BIN IS FULL

Grade 5 Manufactured storage maximum:

    100

Visit a Manufactured Material Trader before returning to the same farm.

Trade away enough of the farmed material to create space.
