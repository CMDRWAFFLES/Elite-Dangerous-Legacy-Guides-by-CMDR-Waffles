ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
MOST EFFICIENT COMPLETE ENGINEERING-MATERIAL ROUTINE
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
PURPOSE
===============================================================================

This route minimizes cross-category trading while avoiding inefficient RNG
hunts.

It is strictly for Xbox/PlayStation Legacy Horizons 3.8.

===============================================================================
1. ENCODED DATA
===============================================================================

MAIN UNIVERSAL FARM:

JAMESON CRASH SITE

LOCATION:

System:      HIP 12099
Body:        1 B
Coordinates: −54.3667, −50.3631

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FARMING PROCEDURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Scan the four comms beacons with the SRV Data Link Scanner.
2. Return to the main menu.
3. Reload.
4. Repeat.


TIP:

You can park your SRV inbetween each of the four comms so you dont have to move
at all, just go into turret mode, rotate and scan each, go to main menu and go
back in and repeat.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATERIALS RECEIVED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MATERIAL                       GRADE   CATEGORY
-----------------------------  ------  -------------------
Modified Consumer Firmware     G2      Encoded Firmware

Cracked Industrial Firmware    G3      Encoded Firmware

Atypical Encryption Archives   G4      Encryption Files

Adaptive Encryptors Capture    G5      Encryption Files


This is the fastest general encoded-data farm because the four stationary
beacons can be scanned every relog.

You can farm for the other Encoded data directly but the best laziest way to
get them is just to go to a material trader - encoded and trade the G4 and G5
to the other G4 and G5 encoded data, then just going back to Jameson Crash Site
and repeat.

===============================================================================
2. RAW MATERIALS
===============================================================================

Raw materials top out at Grade 4.

Legacy’s concentrated crystalline-shard biological POIs allow six of the seven
G4 materials to be farmed directly and predictably.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIRECT G4 SHARD CIRCUIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

G4 MATERIAL   LEGACY LOCATION
------------  ------------------------------------------------------
Polonium      HIP 36601 C 1 A — Biological sites

Ruthenium     HIP 36601 C 1 D — Biological sites

Tellurium     HIP 36601 C 3 B — Biological sites

Technetium    HIP 36601 C 5 A — Biological sites

Yttrium       Outotz  LS-K d8-3 B 5 A — Biological sites

Antimony      Outotz LS-K d8-3 B 5 C — Biological sites

Selenium      Brain-tree sites, rather than crystalline shards


Map each body with the DSS and select Biological, not Geological, signals.

Drive through the shard forest, shoot the Crystalline Clusters and scoop the G4
drops.

Legacy retains the old discrete biological POIs that disappeared from the
later 4.0 planet system.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EFFICIENT RAW ROUTINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Travel to HIP 36601 and fill Polonium, Ruthenium, Tellurium and Technetium.
2. Travel to Outotz LS-K d8-3 and fill Yttrium and Antimony.
3. Farm Selenium at a known brain-tree site.
4. Trade downward within each column for its corresponding G1–G3 materials.


Direct Selenium collection is less clean than the six shard materials.

For maximum speed rather than maximum direct collection, filling the six shard
materials and cross-trading some of them into Selenium can be reasonable.

===============================================================================
3. MANUFACTURED MATERIALS
===============================================================================

The main Legacy manufactured farm is High Grade Emissions.

Unlike Jameson, HGEs are temporary signal sources governed by faction
allegiance and state.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIRECT G5 MANUFACTURED SOURCES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

G5 MATERIAL                    LEGACY SOURCE
-----------------------------  --------------------------------
Imperial Shielding             Empire-aligned HGE

Core Dynamics Composites       Federation-aligned HGE

Proto Heat Radiators           Boom HGE

Proto Radiolic Alloys          Boom HGE

Military Grade Alloys          War or Civil War HGE

Military Supercapacitors       War or Civil War HGE

Pharmaceutical Isolators       Outbreak HGE

Improvised Components          Civil Unrest HGE

Biotech Conductors             Mission rewards

Exquisite Focus Crystals       Mission rewards


The signal’s associated faction matters—not merely the system’s headline state.

Scan the Navigation Beacon or use the FSS, then inspect each HGE’s faction and
timer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EFFICIENT MANUFACTURED ROUTINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Farm Imperial Shielding whenever possible; it is generally the cleanest
   universal manufactured donor.

2. Farm Core Dynamics Composites in Federation space.

3. Collect the two Boom materials whenever useful Boom HGEs appear.

4. Directly hunt War, Civil War, Outbreak and Civil Unrest HGEs when those
   Legacy states are available.

5. Select Biotech Conductors and Exquisite Focus Crystals from mission rewards;
   Robigo passenger missions can offer both.

6. Cross-trade Imperial Shielding for particularly troublesome materials when
   searching for the required state takes longer than another Imperial
   Shielding run.


Dav’s Hope is useful for broad stocks of mid-grade manufactured materials, but
it is not competitive with G5 HGEs plus downward trading when maximum
efficiency is the goal.

The commonly recommended Horizons strategy is therefore to farm high-grade
sources and use the trader rather than individually searching for every lower
grade.

===============================================================================
BEST COMBINED ORDER
===============================================================================

For a major engineering-stockpile run:

1. Jameson Crash Site:
   Fill AEC and AEA.

2. Raw shard expedition:
   Fill the six crystalline-shard G4 bins; gather Selenium or trade for it.

3. Imperial space:
   Fill Imperial Shielding from HGEs.

4. Federation space:
   Collect Core Dynamics Composites if needed.

5. State-specific HGEs:
   Target Boom, War, Outbreak and Civil Unrest only where useful Legacy states
   currently exist.

6. Robigo Mines:
   Collect Biotech Conductors, Exquisite Focus Crystals and Modified Embedded
   Firmware from passenger rewards.

7. Optional Distribution Centre:
   Directly stock wake data.

8. Finish at the three Material Trader types and trade downward or across only
   where required.
