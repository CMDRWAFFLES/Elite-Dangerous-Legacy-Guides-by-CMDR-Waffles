===============================================================================
ENGINEERING MATERIALS — DATA
===============================================================================

Jameson Crash Site → Adaptive Encryptors Capture → Material Trader

Jameson Crash Site is the best concentrated, reloggable encoded-material farm
available in Horizons 3.8.

Its main prize is Adaptive Encryptors Capture, a Grade-5 encoded material that
you can cross-trade into every other encoded category.

You also receive Atypical Encryption Archives and lower-grade firmware data.

===============================================================================
BEFORE LEAVING
===============================================================================

Visit an Encoded Material Trader and trade away most of your:

[ ] Adaptive Encryptors Capture
[ ] Atypical Encryption Archives
[ ] Cracked Industrial Firmware
[ ] Modified Consumer Firmware

This creates room for the materials Jameson provides.

===============================================================================
MANUAL NAVIGATION
===============================================================================

Navigation is manual.

Get close-ish to the cords from orbit. Then drop (glide) down to fly more
accuratly when you're close.

Your current latitude and longitude appear on the HUD while flying close to the
surface.

Heading 180° south lowers latitude toward −54.3667.

Heading 0° north raises latitude.

Heading 90° east raises longitude.

Heading 270° west lowers longitude toward −50.3631.

===============================================================================
LEGACY-CONSOLE FARMING LOOP
===============================================================================

1. Land at the crash site.
2. Deploy the SRV.
3. Park centrally between the four Comms Control beacons.
4. Enter turret mode and scan all four with the Data Link Scanner.
5. Exit to the main menu.
6. Log back in.
7. Scan all four again.
8. Repeat until Adaptive Encryptors Capture or Atypical Encryption Archives is
   full.

LEGACY SITE NOTE
===============================================================================

Legacy has the original four-beacon farming arrangement.

Ignore modern Live-PC guides showing nine upgraded scan points; that change was
introduced much later and is not part of console Legacy. 
I have included a map of the Jameson Crash site which is in the same folder 
as this file. I recommend taking a look at it.

===============================================================================
TRADING STRATEGY
===============================================================================

Return to an Encoded Material Trader—Ray Gateway in Diaguandri is the
traditional nearby choice—and convert the Jameson materials into everything
else.

Then return to HIP 12099 and repeat.

Prioritize trades like this:

1. Use Adaptive Encryptors Capture for other Grade-5 data.

2. Use Atypical Encryption Archives for Grade 4 and lower.

3. Use the Grade-3 and Grade-2 firmware drops for lower-grade shortages.

4. Avoid spending Grade-5 materials on Grade-1 data unless you need an
   immediate large quantity.

Cross-trading into another Grade-5 family has a poor exchange rate, but Jameson
remains faster overall because the source is stationary, safe, concentrated
and resettable.

Direct wake scanning, ship scanning and mission rewards involve travel,
spawning or mission RNG.

They are useful passively, but generally do not beat Jameson when you are
deliberately filling encoded storage.

===============================================================================
BOTTOM LINE
===============================================================================

Empty the Jameson materials at a trader, farm the four Legacy beacons until
full, trade everything into your shopping list, and repeat.

That is the fastest general encoded-data strategy on Xbox and PlayStation
Legacy.
