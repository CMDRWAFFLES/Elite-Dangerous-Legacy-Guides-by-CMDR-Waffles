RAW MATERIALS: DIRECT G4 FARMING CIRCUIT
===============================================================================

Raw materials max out at Grade 4, with a storage cap of 150 each.

The six crystalline-shard locations produce almost entirely one specific G4
material.

Selenium is the exception and is best gathered from brain trees.

===============================================================================
CRYSTALLINE-SHARD PROCEDURE
===============================================================================

BRING:

[ ] An SRV
[ ] Detailed Surface Scanner
[ ] Fuel scoop
[ ] Preferably a small or medium ship with reasonable jump range


AT EACH SITE:

1. Land at the coordinates.
2. Deploy the SRV.
3. Target the small Crystalline Clusters growing from the tall crystalline
   shards.
4. Shoot each cluster with the SRV repeater.
5. Scoop the released raw material.
6. Continue until that G4 reaches 150.


A sufficiently dense Legacy shard forest can normally fill the relevant G4 bin
at one site.

Shard growths are not as reliably Jameson-reloggable as data beacons, so when a
site is exhausted, move to another Biological site on the same body rather than
depending on an immediate menu relog.

===============================================================================
SELENIUM
===============================================================================

For Selenium, drive through the brain-tree grove, shoot the harvestable growths
and scoop the Selenium drops.

It is not as pure or tidy as a crystalline-shard forest, but HR 3230 3 A A is
the established Legacy solution for directly filling Selenium.

===============================================================================
BEST RAW-MATERIAL STRATEGY
===============================================================================

Directly fill all seven G4 materials, then use a Raw Material Trader only to
trade downward within each material family for G1–G3 materials.

That avoids the expensive cross-category exchanges and gives you access to
every ordinary raw engineering material.

So for Raw materials, your preferred approach is entirely practical:

    DIRECTLY FARM EVERY MAXIMUM-GRADE MATERIAL;
    TRADE DOWNWARD ONLY WHEN LOWER GRADES ARE NEEDED.
