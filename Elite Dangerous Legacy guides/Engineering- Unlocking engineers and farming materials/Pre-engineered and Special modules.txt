PRE-ENGINEERED / SPECIAL MODULES — LEGACY 3.8
==============================================

HUMAN PRE-ENGINEERED MODULES
============================

1. Engineered Frame Shift Drive V1
----------------------------------
Class   : 5A
Source  : Human Technology Broker
Effect  : Increased Range + Faster Boot. An experimental effect can be added
          afterwards; Mass Manager is the common choice.
Cost    : 18 Datamined Wake Exceptions; 26 Tellurium; 26 Electrochemical Arrays; 28
          Chemical Processors
Acquire : Each payment gives one module. Pay again for additional copies.

2. Engineered Detailed Surface Scanner V1
-----------------------------------------
Class   : 1I
Source  : Human Technology Broker
Effect  : Double-engineered Expanded Probe Scanning Radius.
Cost    : 24 Niobium; 28 Mechanical Components; 22 Germanium; 26 Mechanical Scrap
Acquire : Each payment gives one module. Pay again for additional copies.

3. Engineered Seeker Missile Rack V1
------------------------------------
Class   : 2B Fixed
Source  : Human Technology Broker
Effect  : Lightweight + High Capacity + Thermal Cascade.
Cost    : 16 Proto Radiolic Alloys; 24 Conductive Ceramics; 26 Hybrid Capacitors; 28
          Phosphorus; 10 Osmium
Acquire : Each payment gives one module. It cannot be further engineered.

4. Modified Mining Laser
------------------------
Class   : 1D Fixed
Source  : Human Technology Broker at Zetian's Recall, LTT 198
Permit  : LTT 198 permit required.
Effect  : Long Range + Reduced Distributor Draw + Incendiary Rounds. About 2,500 m
          maximum range with roughly half the normal Class-1 power/distributor draw.
Cost    : 20 Arsenic; 24 Rhenium; 28 Phosphorus; 16 Osmium
Acquire : Each payment gives one laser. Pay again for additional copies.
Note also obtainable from:
Pride of Bitterwood — EGM 559
Champion of Commerce — Laguz
The House Ascendant — 21 Eridani
