UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

Felicity Farseer → Juri Ishmaak → The Sarge

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
20. THE SARGE
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Beta-3 Tucani
- Base: The Beach

Discovered from:
- Juri Ishmaak at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Federal Navy rank Midshipman or higher

Unlock donation:
- 50 Aberrant Shield Pattern Analysis

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Cannon
- Grade 5 Collector, Fuel Transfer, Hatch Breaker and Prospector Limpet
  Controllers

Fast material method:
- Use Jameson Crash Site data and an Encoded Material Trader.
- Trade into Aberrant Shield Pattern Analysis.

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Federal Navy rank Midshipman.
[ ] Hold 50 Aberrant Shield Pattern Analysis.
[ ] Travel to The Beach.
[ ] Donate the data.
[ ] Rank The Sarge as desired.
