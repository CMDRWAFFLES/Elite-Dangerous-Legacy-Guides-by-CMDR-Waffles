===============================================================================
SCOPE
===============================================================================

This guide covers all 21 non-Colonia ship engineers available in Elite
Dangerous Legacy on Xbox and PlayStation.

Included:
- The five starting engineers
- Every engineer reached through their referral chains
- Professor Palin and Chloe Sedesi
- Permit-locked engineers in Sirius, Alioth, Sol, Achenar and Shinrarta Dezhra

Excluded:
- Odyssey suit and handheld-weapon engineers
- Colonia engineers
- Live-PC-only mechanics and later engineering additions

Chloe Sedesi is outside the central Bubble in the Witch Head area, but she is
included because she is a Horizons ship engineer and is not a Colonia engineer.

IMPORTANT ENGINEERING RULES
===============================================================================

1. REFERRAL PROGRESS

Most referral engineers become known when the preceding engineer reaches
approximately Grade 3 plus one-third of the progress toward Grade 4.

For simplicity, take each engineer to Grade 4 or to their maximum grade before
moving on. This ensures the referrals trigger and makes future engineering more
material-efficient.

2. RANKING AN ENGINEER

Engineer reputation is increased by performing modifications in person at the
engineer's workshop. Some engineers also accept exploration data or another
activity at their base.

Remote Workshop modifications do not increase reputation with the engineer.

3. EXPERIMENTAL EFFECTS

Experimental effects must be applied in person at an engineer who handles that
type of module. A pinned blueprint lets you perform ordinary engineering
remotely, but not apply an experimental effect.

4. TRIGGER DELAYS

If a referral, invitation or permit does not appear after meeting the
requirements:

- Return to the main menu and reload.
- Check the Engineer section of the right-hand panel.
- Check the mission board at a station controlled by the required faction.
- Confirm that the preceding engineer is beyond Grade 3.
- Confirm that you are playing Legacy rather than using Live-galaxy information.

5. LEGACY BGS AND MARKETS

Legacy has a separate Background Simulation from Live PC. Faction states,
station services, commodity supplies and mission boards can differ.

Use the in-game Legacy Galaxy Map, System Map and station services for current
conditions. Current Live-only database listings are not reliable for dynamic
Legacy information.

START THESE LONG-TERM REQUIREMENTS IMMEDIATELY
===============================================================================

[ ] TRADE AT 50 DIFFERENT COMMODITY MARKETS

Lei Cheung requires transactions at more than 50 separate markets.

At every new station:
1. Buy at least one tonne of an ordinary commodity.
2. Sell it at another station.
3. Continue using new markets until the requirement completes.

The requirement counts distinct commodity markets, not fifty transactions at
the same market.

[ ] SAVE MODULAR TERMINALS

Marco Qwent requires 25 tonnes of Modular Terminals.

Modular Terminals are mission-reward commodities. Check mission reward choices
and store them in your ship until you have 25. They occupy cargo space and may
attract pirates.

[ ] PLAN A 5,000-LY EXPEDITION

Professor Palin and Chloe Sedesi both require a maximum distance of at least
5,000 light years from the system where your commander began their career.

Before leaving:
- Fit a fuel scoop and Detailed Surface Scanner.
- Collect enough Sensor Fragments for both engineers if possible.
- Make sure the journey exceeds 5,000 LY from your career-start system, not
  merely 5,000 LY from your current position.

Scan systems during the trip. The exploration data can be used to gain
reputation with Sirius Corporation, Alioth Independents and Eurybia Blue Mafia,
and can rank engineers who accept exploration data.

[ ] WORK ON SUPERPOWER RANK

Federal requirements:
- Midshipman for The Sarge
- Petty Officer for the Sol permit needed by Colonel Bris Dekker
- Friendly overall Federation reputation for Bris Dekker

Imperial requirements:
- Outsider for Hera Tani
- Squire for the Achenar permit needed by Tiana Fortune
- Friendly overall Empire reputation for Tiana Fortune

[ ] BEGIN MINING PROGRESS

Selene Jean counts all ore that you personally mine and refine.

While completing the 500-tonne requirement, retain:
- 10 tonnes of Painite for Selene Jean
- 50 tonnes of Bromellite for Bill Turner

Do not sell or discard those quantities before making the donations.

1. FELICITY FARSEER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Deciat
- Base: Farseer Inc

How she becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Exploration rank Scout or higher

Unlock donation:
- 1 Meta-Alloy

Reliable Legacy source:
- Darnielle's Progress in Maia normally sells Meta-Alloys.
- Carry at least one tonne of cargo space.

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Frame Shift Drive
- Grade 3 Thrusters
- Grade 3 Sensors and Detailed Surface Scanner

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Scout exploration rank.
[ ] Acquire one Meta-Alloy.
[ ] Travel to Farseer Inc in Deciat.
[ ] Donate the Meta-Alloy.
[ ] Engineer a Frame Shift Drive and rank Felicity to at least Grade 3 plus
    progress toward Grade 4.
[ ] Confirm that Juri Ishmaak has been discovered.

Efficiency note:
A Grade 5 Increased Range FSD makes every later unlock substantially faster.
Deciat is a well-known player-traffic system; Solo is the safest mode.

3. THE DWELLER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Wyrd
- Base: Black Hide

How he becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Sell goods at five different black markets

Unlock donation:
- 500,000 credits

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Power Distributor
- Laser engineering up to Grade 4

Fast method:
1. Obtain at least five units of illegal or stolen cargo.
2. Find five separate stations that have an active Black Market.
3. Confirm that your cargo is illegal at each station.
4. Sell at least one unit at each of the five different Black Markets.

Important:
- Five sales at one station do not count.
- A legal commodity will not be accepted by the Black Market merely because
  the station has one.
- Black Market availability can vary with the Legacy BGS.

Procedure:
[ ] Complete sales at five distinct Black Markets.
[ ] Travel to Black Hide.
[ ] Pay 500,000 credits.
[ ] Rank The Dweller beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Lei Cheung.

RARE-GOOD RULES
===============================================================================

Rare commodities have limited allocations. The exact quantity can vary with
Legacy BGS conditions and your local reputation.

Important:
- Carrying your current allocation normally prevents another allocation from
  appearing.
- Deliver the goods to the engineer before returning for more.
- Do not rely on a modern Live-PC market quantity.
- Fleet Carriers may sell rare unlock goods, but stock and location are
  player-dependent; this guide uses permanent source stations.

FASTEST OVERALL CAMPAIGN SUMMARY
===============================================================================

1. Unlock Felicity and engineer an Increased Range FSD.
2. Unlock Elvira, Dweller, Tod and Liz.
3. Begin buying and selling one commodity at every unique market.
4. Save Modular Terminals from missions.
5. Collect 50 Sensor Fragments.
6. Make a 5,000-LY expedition and gather exploration data.
7. Use that data to help reach Allied with Sirius Corporation and Alioth
   Independents, and Friendly with Eurybia Blue Mafia.
8. Unlock Qwent, then Palin and Chloe.
9. Complete the 50-market requirement and unlock Lei Cheung, then Ram Tah.
10. Mine 500 tonnes while saving ten Painite and fifty Bromellite.
11. Unlock Selene Jean, Bill Turner and Didi Vatermann.
12. Complete Imperial rank to at least Squire.
13. Unlock Hera Tani, Broo Tarquin and Tiana Fortune.
14. Complete Federal rank to at least Petty Officer.
15. Earn the necessary Federal combat bonds.
16. Unlock Juri Ishmaak, The Sarge and Colonel Bris Dekker.
17. Unlock Zacariah Nemo whenever Party of Yoru reputation is convenient.
18. Unlock Lori Jameson after reaching Dangerous combat rank and obtaining the
    Shinrarta Dezhra permit.
