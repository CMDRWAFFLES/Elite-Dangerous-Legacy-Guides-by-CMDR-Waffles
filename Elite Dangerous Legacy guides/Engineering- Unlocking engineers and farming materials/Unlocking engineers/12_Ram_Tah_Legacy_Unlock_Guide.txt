12. RAM TAH
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Meene
- Base: Phoenix Base

Discovered from:
- Lei Cheung at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Exploration rank Surveyor or higher

Unlock donation:
- 50 Classified Scan Databanks

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Heat Sink Launcher
- Grade 5 Point Defence
- Grade 5 Chaff Launcher
- Grade 5 Electronic Countermeasure
- Limpet-controller engineering

Fast material method:
- Use the Jameson Crash Site farm and an Encoded Material Trader.
- Trade into Classified Scan Databanks if direct scanning has not supplied 50.

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Surveyor exploration rank.
[ ] Hold 50 Classified Scan Databanks.
[ ] Travel to Phoenix Base.
[ ] Donate the data.
[ ] Rank Ram Tah as desired.
