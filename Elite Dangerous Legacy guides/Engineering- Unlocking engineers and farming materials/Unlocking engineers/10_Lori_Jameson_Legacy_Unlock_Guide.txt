===============================================================================
10. LORI JAMESON
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Shinrarta Dezhra — permit required
- Base: Jameson Base

Discovered from:
- Marco Qwent at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirements:
- Combat rank Dangerous or higher
- Access to Shinrarta Dezhra by reaching Elite in at least one main career rank

Unlock donation:
- 25 Kongga Ale

Rare-good source:
- System: Kongga
- Station: Laplace Ring

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Sensors
- Grade 5 Detailed Surface Scanner
- Grade 4 Life Support, Fuel Scoop, AFMU and Refinery

Procedure:
[ ] Reach combat rank Dangerous.
[ ] Reach Elite in at least one qualifying career rank for the Shinrarta permit.
[ ] Buy Kongga Ale at Laplace Ring.
[ ] Deliver 25 total to Jameson Base.
[ ] Rank Lori as desired.

This is normally one of the last engineers unlocked because of the Dangerous
combat-rank gate and Shinrarta permit.
