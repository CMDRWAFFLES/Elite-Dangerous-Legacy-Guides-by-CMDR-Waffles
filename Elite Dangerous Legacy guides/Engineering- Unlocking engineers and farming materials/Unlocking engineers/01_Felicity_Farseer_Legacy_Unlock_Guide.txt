===============================================================================
1. FELICITY FARSEER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Deciat
- Base: Farseer Inc

How she becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Exploration rank Scout or higher

Unlock donation:
- 1 Meta-Alloy

Reliable Legacy source:
- Darnielle's Progress in Maia normally sells Meta-Alloys.
- Carry at least one tonne of cargo space.

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Frame Shift Drive
- Grade 3 Thrusters
- Grade 3 Sensors and Detailed Surface Scanner

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Scout exploration rank.
[ ] Acquire one Meta-Alloy.
[ ] Travel to Farseer Inc in Deciat.
[ ] Donate the Meta-Alloy.
[ ] Engineer a Frame Shift Drive and rank Felicity to at least Grade 3 plus
    progress toward Grade 4.
[ ] Confirm that Juri Ishmaak has been discovered.

Efficiency note:
A Grade 5 Increased Range FSD makes every later unlock substantially faster.
Deciat is a well-known player-traffic system; Solo is the safest mode.
