===============================================================================
2. ELVIRA MARTUUK
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Khun
- Base: Long Sight Base

How she becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Travel at least 300 LY from your career-start system

Unlock donation:
- 3 Soontill Relics

Rare-good source:
- System: Ngurii
- Station: Cheranovsky City

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Frame Shift Drive
- Grade 3 Shield Generator

Procedure:
[ ] Travel at least 300 LY from your starting system.
[ ] Buy three Soontill Relics at Cheranovsky City.
[ ] Travel to Long Sight Base.
[ ] Donate all three relics.
[ ] Rank Elvira beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Marco Qwent and Zacariah Nemo.
