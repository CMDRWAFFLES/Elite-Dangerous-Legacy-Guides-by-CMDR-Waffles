UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

The Dweller → Lei Cheung

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
11. LEI CHEUNG
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Laksak
- Base: Trader's Rest

Discovered from:
- The Dweller at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Trade at more than 50 distinct commodity markets

Unlock donation:
- 200 tonnes of Gold

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Shield Generator
- Grade 5 Sensors
- Grade 5 Detailed Surface Scanner

Procedure:
[ ] Complete commodity transactions at more than 50 separate markets.
[ ] Source Gold from Legacy markets, preferably near Laksak.
[ ] Deliver 200 tonnes total to Trader's Rest.
[ ] Rank Lei beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Ram Tah.

Cargo tip:
A large ship can deliver the Gold in one trip if Trader's Rest accepts its
landing-pad size; otherwise use a suitable medium ship and make multiple trips.
