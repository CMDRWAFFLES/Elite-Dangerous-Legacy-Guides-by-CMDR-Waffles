===============================================================================
21. COLONEL BRIS DEKKER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Sol — permit required
- Base: Dekker's Yard

Discovered from:
- Juri Ishmaak at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirements:
- Friendly with the Federation
- Federal Navy rank Petty Officer for the Sol permit

Unlock donation:
- Federal combat bonds worth the amount shown in your Engineer panel

Legacy warning:
The displayed donation has historically appeared as either 1,000,000 or
10,000,000 credits under different conditions. Retain enough valid Federal
combat bonds to meet the exact amount displayed in your Legacy Engineer panel.

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 4 Frame Shift Drive Interdictor
- Grade 3 Frame Shift Drive

Procedure:
[ ] Become Friendly with the Federation.
[ ] Reach Federal Navy rank Petty Officer.
[ ] Confirm that the Sol permit has been awarded.
[ ] Earn and retain the required value of Federal combat bonds.
[ ] Travel to Dekker's Yard in Sol.
[ ] Donate the bonds.
[ ] Rank Bris Dekker as desired.
