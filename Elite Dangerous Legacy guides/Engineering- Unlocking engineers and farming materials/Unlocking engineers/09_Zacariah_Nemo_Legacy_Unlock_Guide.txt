UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

Elvira Martuuk → Zacariah Nemo

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
9. ZACARIAH NEMO
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Yoru
- Base: Nemo Cyber Party Base

Discovered from:
- Elvira Martuuk at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Become Friendly with Party of Yoru
- Complete their engineer invitation mission

Unlock donation:
- 25 Xihe Biomorphic Companions

Rare-good source:
- System: Xihe
- Station: Zhen Dock

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Fragment Cannon

Procedure:
[ ] Become Friendly with Party of Yoru through missions, trade, bounty
    redemption or exploration data where available.
[ ] Check Party of Yoru-controlled mission boards for the invitation mission.
[ ] Buy Xihe Biomorphic Companions at Zhen Dock.
[ ] Deliver 25 total to Nemo Cyber Party Base.
[ ] Rank Nemo as desired.

Rare-good note:
The station may not sell all 25 in one allocation. Deliver your current cargo
to Nemo, return to Xihe and repeat.
