===============================================================================
8. CHLOE SEDESI
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Shenve
- Base: Cinder Dock

Discovered from:
- Marco Qwent at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Travel at least 5,000 LY from your career-start system

Unlock donation:
- 25 Sensor Fragments

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Thrusters
- Alternative to Professor Palin, particularly for commanders operating near
  the Witch Head region

Procedure:
[ ] Confirm the 5,000-LY career-distance requirement.
[ ] Hold 25 additional Sensor Fragments.
[ ] Travel to Cinder Dock in Shenve.
[ ] Donate the fragments.
[ ] Rank Chloe to Grade 5.

Note:
Chloe is not located in the central Bubble, but is included in this complete
non-Colonia Horizons engineer guide.
