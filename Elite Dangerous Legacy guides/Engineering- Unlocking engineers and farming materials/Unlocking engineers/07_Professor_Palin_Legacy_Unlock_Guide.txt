===============================================================================
7. PROFESSOR PALIN
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Arque
- Base: Abel Laboratory

Discovered from:
- Marco Qwent at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Travel at least 5,000 LY from your career-start system

Unlock donation:
- 25 Sensor Fragments

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Thrusters

Sensor Fragments:
- Obtain them by destroying Thargoid Sensors found at appropriate Thargoid
  wreck sites.
- Sensor Fragments are engineering materials and do not require cargo space.
- Collect at least 50 total if you intend to unlock both Palin and Chloe.

Procedure:
[ ] Confirm that your maximum career distance exceeds 5,000 LY.
[ ] Hold at least 25 Sensor Fragments.
[ ] Travel to Abel Laboratory in Arque.
[ ] Donate 25 Sensor Fragments.
[ ] Rank Palin to Grade 5.
