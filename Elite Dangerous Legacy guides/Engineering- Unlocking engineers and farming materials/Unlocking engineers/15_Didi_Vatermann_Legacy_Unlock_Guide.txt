===============================================================================
15. DIDI VATERMANN
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Leesti
- Base: Vatermann LLC

Discovered from:
- Selene Jean at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Trade rank Merchant or higher

Unlock donation:
- 50 Lavian Brandy

Rare-good source:
- System: Lave
- Station: Lave Station

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Shield Booster

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Merchant trade rank.
[ ] Buy Lavian Brandy at Lave Station.
[ ] Deliver it to Vatermann LLC.
[ ] Repeat until 50 total have been donated.
[ ] Rank Didi to Grade 5.

Rare-good note:
The available allocation may vary. You cannot accumulate a second allocation
while still carrying the first; deliver or otherwise remove the current cargo
before returning.
