===============================================================================
5. LIZ RYDER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Eurybia
- Base: Demolition Unlimited

How she becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Become Cordial or Friendly with Eurybia Blue Mafia
- Complete their engineer invitation mission when offered

Unlock donation:
- 200 tonnes of Landmines

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Missile Rack
- Grade 5 Seeker Missile Rack
- Grade 5 Torpedo Pylon

Efficient reputation method:
- Sell exploration data at a station controlled by Eurybia Blue Mafia.
- Alternatively complete missions for the faction.

Landmines:
- Landmines are normal cargo, commonly available from appropriate military or
  industrial markets.
- Use a large cargo ship to reduce the number of trips.
- Verify supply using the Legacy in-game market.

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Cordial or Friendly with Eurybia Blue Mafia.
[ ] Complete the engineer invitation mission.
[ ] Source and deliver 200 tonnes of Landmines.
[ ] Rank Liz beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Hera Tani.
