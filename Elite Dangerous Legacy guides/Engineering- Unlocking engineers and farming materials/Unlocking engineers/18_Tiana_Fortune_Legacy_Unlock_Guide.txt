18. TIANA FORTUNE
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Achenar — permit required
- Base: Fortune's Loss

Discovered from:
- Hera Tani at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirements:
- Friendly with the Empire
- Imperial Navy rank Squire for the Achenar permit

Unlock donation:
- 50 Decoded Emission Data

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Sensors
- Grade 5 Frame Shift Wake Scanner
- Grade 5 Kill Warrant Scanner
- Grade 5 Manifest Scanner
- Grade 5 limpet controllers

Fast material method:
- Use Jameson Crash Site data and an Encoded Material Trader.
- Trade into Decoded Emission Data if required.

Procedure:
[ ] Become Friendly with the Empire.
[ ] Reach Imperial Navy rank Squire and obtain the Achenar permit.
[ ] Hold 50 Decoded Emission Data.
[ ] Travel to Fortune's Loss.
[ ] Donate the data.
[ ] Rank Tiana as desired.
