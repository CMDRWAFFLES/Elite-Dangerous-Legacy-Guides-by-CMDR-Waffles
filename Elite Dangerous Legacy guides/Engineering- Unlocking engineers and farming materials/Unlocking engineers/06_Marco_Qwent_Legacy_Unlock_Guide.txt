MARCO QWENT — STANDALONE ENGINEER UNLOCK GUIDE
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

Elvira Martuuk → Marco Qwent

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
6. MARCO QWENT
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Sirius — permit required
- Base: Qwent Research Base

Discovered from:
- Elvira Martuuk at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirements:
- Become Allied with Sirius Corporation
- Obtain the Sirius system permit
- Complete the Sirius Corporation engineer invitation mission

Unlock donation:
- 25 tonnes of Modular Terminals

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 4 Power Plant
- Required referral for Professor Palin, Chloe Sedesi and Lori Jameson

Efficient Sirius reputation:
- Sell exploration data at a Sirius Corporation-controlled station outside
  Sirius.
- Complete missions for Sirius Corporation.
- Once Allied, look for the Sirius permit mission.
- After entering Sirius, look for the Marco Qwent invitation mission.

Modular Terminals:
- Mission-reward commodity
- Requires cargo space
- Keep selecting missions that offer them until you hold 25 tonnes

Procedure:
[ ] Raise Elvira beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Become Allied with Sirius Corporation.
[ ] Complete the Sirius permit mission.
[ ] Enter Sirius and complete the Qwent invitation mission.
[ ] Deliver 25 Modular Terminals to Qwent Research Base.
[ ] Rank Qwent beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Professor Palin, Chloe Sedesi and Lori Jameson.
