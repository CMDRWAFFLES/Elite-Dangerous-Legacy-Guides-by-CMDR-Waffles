===============================================================================
4. TOD "THE BLASTER" McQUINN
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Wolf 397
- Base: Trophy Camp

How he becomes known:
- Publicly known starting engineer

Meeting requirement:
- Earn 15 or more bounty vouchers

Unlock donation:
- At least 100,000 credits in bounty vouchers

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Multi-cannon
- Grade 5 Rail Gun

Fast method:
- Hunt wanted ships at a Nav Beacon or Resource Extraction Site.
- Claim enough separate bounty vouchers to satisfy the count.
- Retain at least 100,000 credits of vouchers for Trophy Camp.

Procedure:
[ ] Accumulate more than 15 bounty vouchers.
[ ] Hold at least 100,000 credits of bounty vouchers.
[ ] Travel to Trophy Camp.
[ ] Redeem the required vouchers through the engineer unlock.
[ ] Rank Tod beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Selene Jean.
