===============================================================================

Felicity Farseer → Juri Ishmaak

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
19. JURI ISHMAAK
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Giryak
- Base: Pater's Memorial

Discovered from:
- Felicity Farseer at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Claim more than 50 Federal combat bonds

Unlock donation:
- Federal combat bonds worth the amount shown in your Engineer panel

Legacy warning:
The displayed donation has historically appeared as either 100,000 or
1,000,000 credits under different conditions. Carry at least 1,000,000 credits
in valid Federal combat bonds and follow the exact amount shown in your
Legacy Engineer panel.

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Sensors
- Grade 5 Detailed Surface Scanner
- Grade 5 Mine Launcher

Efficient method:
1. Find a Legacy system in War or Civil War.
2. Enter a Conflict Zone.
3. Use the Communications panel to side with a Federation-aligned faction.
4. Destroy and claim bonds for more than 50 enemy ships.
5. Retain enough bonds for Juri's donation.

Procedure:
[ ] Claim more than 50 qualifying combat bonds.
[ ] Carry sufficient Federal combat bonds.
[ ] Travel to Pater's Memorial.
[ ] Donate the required bonds.
[ ] Rank Juri beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of The Sarge and Colonel Bris Dekker.
