===============================================================================
14. BILL TURNER
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Alioth — permit required
- Base: Turner Metallics Inc

Discovered from:
- Selene Jean at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirements:
- Friendly with the Alliance
- Allied with Alioth Independents
- Obtain the Alioth system permit

Unlock donation:
- 50 tonnes of Bromellite

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Plasma Accelerator
- Grade 5 Sensors
- Grade 5 Detailed Surface Scanner

Efficient permit method:
- Sell exploration data to Alioth Independents-controlled stations.
- Complete missions for Alioth Independents.
- When Allied, accept the Alioth permit mission.

Procedure:
[ ] Become Friendly with the Alliance.
[ ] Become Allied with Alioth Independents.
[ ] Complete the Alioth permit mission.
[ ] Carry the retained 50 tonnes of Bromellite into Alioth.
[ ] Donate it at Turner Metallics Inc.
[ ] Rank Bill as desired.
