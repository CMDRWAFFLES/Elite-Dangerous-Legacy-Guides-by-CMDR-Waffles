===============================================================================
13. SELENE JEAN
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Kuk
- Base: Prospector's Rest

Discovered from:
- Tod McQuinn at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Mine and refine at least 500 tonnes of ore

Unlock donation:
- 10 tonnes of mined Painite

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Armour
- Grade 5 Hull Reinforcement Package

Efficient method:
1. Mine in a metallic ring until you have ten tonnes of Painite.
2. Continue mining until the career total reaches 500 tonnes.
3. Mine in an icy ring as necessary and retain 50 tonnes of Bromellite for
   Bill Turner.
4. Do not sell the ten Painite before visiting Selene.

Important:
The Painite must have been mined. Bought Painite does not satisfy the intended
unlock route.

Procedure:
[ ] Mine and refine 500 tonnes total.
[ ] Keep ten tonnes of mined Painite.
[ ] Keep fifty tonnes of Bromellite.
[ ] Deliver the Painite to Prospector's Rest.
[ ] Rank Selene beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Bill Turner and Didi Vatermann.
