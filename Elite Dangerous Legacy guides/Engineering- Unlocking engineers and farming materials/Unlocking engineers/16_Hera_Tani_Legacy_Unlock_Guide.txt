UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

Liz Ryder → Hera Tani

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
16. HERA TANI
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Kuwemaki
- Base: The Jet's Hole

Discovered from:
- Liz Ryder at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Imperial Navy rank Outsider or higher

Unlock donation:
- 50 Kamitra Cigars

Rare-good source:
- System: Kamitra
- Station: Hammel Terminal

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Power Plant
- Grade 5 Detailed Surface Scanner

Procedure:
[ ] Reach Imperial Navy rank Outsider.
[ ] Buy Kamitra Cigars at Hammel Terminal.
[ ] Deliver them to The Jet's Hole.
[ ] Repeat until 50 total have been donated.
[ ] Rank Hera beyond Grade 3.
[ ] Confirm discovery of Broo Tarquin and Tiana Fortune.
