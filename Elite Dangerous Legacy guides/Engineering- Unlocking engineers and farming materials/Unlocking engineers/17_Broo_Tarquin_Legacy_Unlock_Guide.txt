UNLOCK PATH
===============================================================================

Liz Ryder → Hera Tani → Broo Tarquin

Referral engineers normally become known after ranking the preceding
engineer beyond Grade 3 and making some progress toward Grade 4.

Dynamic mission availability, faction states, commodity supplies and
rare-good allocations must be checked in the separate Legacy galaxy.

===============================================================================
17. BROO TARQUIN
===============================================================================

Location:
- System: Muang
- Base: Broo's Legacy

Discovered from:
- Hera Tani at approximately Grade 3 to Grade 4

Meeting requirement:
- Combat rank Competent or higher

Unlock donation:
- 50 Fujin Tea

Rare-good source:
- System: Fujin
- Station: Futen Spaceport

Best reason to unlock:
- Grade 5 Beam Laser
- Grade 5 Burst Laser
- Grade 5 Pulse Laser

Procedure:
[ ] Reach combat rank Competent.
[ ] Buy Fujin Tea at Futen Spaceport.
[ ] Deliver it to Broo's Legacy.
[ ] Repeat until 50 total have been donated.
[ ] Rank Broo to Grade 5.
