ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
HOW PINNED ENGINEERING BLUEPRINTS WORK
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
PURPOSE
===============================================================================

Pinning an engineering blueprint allows you to apply that regular modification
through a Remote Workshop without repeatedly travelling back to the engineer.

A pinned blueprint is not:

- A saved copy of a completed module
- A free modification
- An Experimental Effect
- A way to skip Grades 1 through 4
- A way to increase reputation with an engineer
- A Technology Broker unlock

Pinning simply makes one normal engineering blueprint from that engineer
available remotely.

===============================================================================
THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE
===============================================================================

You may pin ONE blueprint from EACH engineer.

This means:

- Felicity Farseer can provide one pinned blueprint.
- Elvira Martuuk can provide a different pinned blueprint.
- The Dweller can provide another.
- Every other unlocked ship engineer can also provide one.

The limit is not one blueprint for your whole commander. It is one blueprint
per engineer.

If all 21 non-Colonia Horizons ship engineers covered by the Legacy guide are
unlocked, you can maintain as many as 21 separate pinned blueprints.

Pinning a second blueprint at the same engineer replaces that engineer's
previous pin.

===============================================================================
WHAT EXACTLY IS PINNED?
===============================================================================

The pin applies to the blueprint type, not to one specific module, ship, size
or engineering grade.

Example:

If you pin:

    FRAME SHIFT DRIVE — INCREASED RANGE

you can use that blueprint remotely on compatible Frame Shift Drives fitted to
any of your ships.

The pin is not tied to:

- The Frame Shift Drive fitted when the blueprint was pinned
- One particular ship
- One module class or size
- One specific engineering grade

The highest grade available remotely is limited by:

1. The maximum grade that the engineer offers for that blueprint.
2. The grade access you have personally unlocked with that engineer.

Example:

If an engineer offers Increased Range Frame Shift Drive to Grade 5 but you have
only earned Grade 3 access with that engineer, the Remote Workshop will stop at
Grade 3.

===============================================================================
HOW TO PIN A BLUEPRINT
===============================================================================

You must visit the engineer in person to select or change their pinned
blueprint.

General procedure:

1. Unlock the engineer.
2. Travel to the engineer's workshop.
3. Open Engineer's Workshop.
4. Select a compatible module.
5. Select the normal modification you want available remotely.
6. Choose PIN BLUEPRINT.
7. Confirm that the blueprint is marked as pinned.

Recommended procedure before leaving:

1. Rank the engineer as high as required.
2. Unlock the maximum grade you intend to use.
3. Pin the chosen blueprint.
4. Apply any desired Experimental Effect to modules already aboard.
5. Check the Engineer section of the right-hand panel to confirm the pin.

Pinning itself does not consume engineering materials.

===============================================================================
HOW TO USE A PINNED BLUEPRINT REMOTELY
===============================================================================

At a station that offers Remote Workshop services:

1. Fit the module you want to modify to your current ship.
2. Open Starport Services.
3. Open Remote Workshop.
4. Select the fitted module.
5. Select the available pinned blueprint.
6. Spend the required materials to engineer the module.
7. Continue through the grades as normal.

Remote engineering uses the normal Legacy material recipes and engineering
rolls. It does not make the modification cheaper.

The Remote Workshop only shows pinned blueprints that are compatible with
modules currently fitted to your ship.

Example:

If you have Overcharged Multi-cannon pinned but your current ship has no
Multi-cannon fitted, that blueprint will not appear as a usable option for the
ship.

===============================================================================
YOU STILL HAVE TO PROGRESS THROUGH THE GRADES
===============================================================================

A Grade 5 pin does not let a new module jump directly to Grade 5.

Every newly engineered module starts at Grade 1.

Typical progression:

    Grade 1
       ↓
    Grade 2
       ↓
    Grade 3
       ↓
    Grade 4
       ↓
    Grade 5

Apply enough rolls at the current grade to unlock the next grade, then move up.

The pin gives remote access to the grades that you have unlocked. It does not
remove normal grade progression from the individual module.

===============================================================================
REMOTE ENGINEERING DOES NOT RANK THE ENGINEER
===============================================================================

Engineering performed through a Remote Workshop does not increase your
reputation or access level with the engineer.

This is one of the most important restrictions.

Example:

- You have Grade 3 access with Felicity Farseer.
- You pin Increased Range Frame Shift Drive.
- You engineer several modules remotely.

Those remote rolls will not raise Felicity to Grade 4 or Grade 5.

To unlock higher grades and referrals, perform engineering in person at the
engineer's workshop or use another reputation activity that the engineer
accepts.

Best practice:

Rank an engineer to their desired maximum grade before relying on their pinned
blueprint.

===============================================================================
EXPERIMENTAL EFFECTS CANNOT BE PINNED
===============================================================================

A pinned blueprint covers only the main modification.

Example:

Main blueprint:

    Frame Shift Drive — Increased Range

Possible Experimental Effects:

    Mass Manager
    Deep Charge
    Stripped Down
    Double Braced
    Thermal Spread

The Increased Range blueprint can be pinned.

Mass Manager and the other Experimental Effects cannot be pinned or applied
through a Remote Workshop.

To add or change an Experimental Effect:

1. Engineer the module normally, either remotely or in person.
2. Fit the module to your ship.
3. Visit an engineer who handles that module category.
4. Apply the Experimental Effect in person.

Useful travel-saving rule:

The engineer applying the Experimental Effect does not have to be the same
engineer who supplied the pinned blueprint.

An engineer with lower-grade access to that module category can apply an
Experimental Effect to a module whose main modification was completed to a
higher grade elsewhere.

Example:

1. Complete a Grade 5 modification remotely using a pinned blueprint.
2. Visit a nearby engineer who handles that type of module.
3. Apply the desired Experimental Effect there.

===============================================================================
CHANGING A PINNED BLUEPRINT
===============================================================================

To replace an engineer's pinned blueprint:

1. Visit that engineer in person.
2. Open Engineer's Workshop.
3. Select the new blueprint.
4. Choose PIN BLUEPRINT.
5. Confirm the change.

The new pin replaces the previous pin from that engineer.

Example:

Felicity currently has:

    Frame Shift Drive — Increased Range

You visit Felicity and pin:

    Thrusters — Dirty Drive Tuning

Felicity's Increased Range FSD pin is removed and Dirty Drive Tuning becomes
her active pin.

Pins from every other engineer remain unchanged.

===============================================================================
DUPLICATE PINS
===============================================================================

Different engineers may offer the same blueprint.

You can pin the same blueprint at two engineers, but this usually wastes one of
your available pins.

Example:

Both Felicity Farseer and Elvira Martuuk can provide high-grade Increased Range
Frame Shift Drive engineering.

A more useful arrangement is normally:

    Felicity Farseer:
    Frame Shift Drive — Increased Range

    Elvira Martuuk:
    Shield Generator — Thermal Resistant

This gives access to two useful remote blueprints instead of duplicating one.

Duplicate pins can still be reasonable when:

- You are temporarily ranking one engineer.
- One engineer has not yet reached the required grade.
- You intend to change the pins later.
- Your personal ship-building preferences make the duplicate convenient.

===============================================================================
COMPLETE EXAMPLE — FRAME SHIFT DRIVE
===============================================================================

Goal:

Engineer Frame Shift Drives for several ships without repeatedly travelling to
Deciat.

At Felicity Farseer:

1. Unlock Felicity.
2. Rank her to Grade 5.
3. Select Frame Shift Drive — Increased Range.
4. Pin the blueprint.
5. Apply Mass Manager or another Experimental Effect to the FSD currently
   aboard, if desired.

Later, at another station:

1. Fit an unengineered Frame Shift Drive to your current ship.
2. Open Remote Workshop.
3. Select Increased Range.
4. Engineer the module from Grade 1 upward.
5. Continue until the desired Grade 5 result is complete.

To add Mass Manager:

1. Visit an engineer capable of working on Frame Shift Drives.
2. Apply Mass Manager in person.

You do not need to return specifically to Felicity unless she is the engineer
you choose for the Experimental Effect or you want to change her pin.

===============================================================================
COMPLETE EXAMPLE — POWER DISTRIBUTOR
===============================================================================

At The Dweller:

1. Rank The Dweller to Grade 5.
2. Pin Power Distributor — Charge Enhanced.

At any suitable Remote Workshop:

1. Fit a Power Distributor.
2. Open Remote Workshop.
3. Select Charge Enhanced.
4. Engineer it through the grades.

For the Experimental Effect:

- Visit an engineer who handles Power Distributors.
- Apply Super Conduits, Cluster Capacitors or another desired effect in person.

===============================================================================
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A MODULE ALREADY HAS ENGINEERING?
===============================================================================

A pinned blueprint can continue work on a module that already uses that same
main modification.

Example:

A Frame Shift Drive already has Grade 3 Increased Range.

With Increased Range pinned, a Remote Workshop can continue that module toward
Grades 4 and 5, provided those grades are unlocked with the pinning engineer.

A different main blueprint is not added on top of the existing one. A module
can only have one normal engineering modification at a time.

Before replacing one main modification with another, check the module carefully
and make sure you intend to rebuild it under the new blueprint.

===============================================================================
LEGACY MODULE WARNING
===============================================================================

Very old modules modified under the pre-Engineering-3.0 system may be marked as
Legacy modifications.

These grandfathered modules are a special case. They may need to be converted
to the current Grade 1–5 engineering system at an engineer before normal pinned
blueprint progression can be used.

Conversion can remove the old unique roll.

Do not convert a valuable grandfathered module unless you are certain that the
new engineering result is preferable.

===============================================================================
WHAT PINNING DOES NOT PROVIDE
===============================================================================

Pinning does not provide remote access to:

- Experimental Effects
- Engineer reputation gains
- Higher grades not yet unlocked
- Another blueprint from the same engineer
- Guardian Technology Broker unlocks
- Human Technology Broker unlocks
- Synthesis recipes
- Module purchases
- Special pre-engineered modules
- Guardian weapons or modules
- A free copy of the engineered module

===============================================================================
BEST PRACTICE WHEN UNLOCKING AN ENGINEER
===============================================================================

Use this routine at each engineer:

1. Arrive with enough materials to rank the engineer.
2. Engineer modules in person until the desired maximum grade is unlocked.
3. Trigger any referrals that depend on engineer reputation.
4. Choose the blueprint that will save the most future travel.
5. Pin that blueprint.
6. Apply Experimental Effects to any modules already aboard.
7. Leave only after confirming the correct pin.

Do not leave an engineer at Grade 2 or Grade 3 and assume remote engineering
will finish ranking them later. It will not.

===============================================================================
HOW TO CHOOSE A GOOD PIN
===============================================================================

A good pinned blueprint is usually:

- Used on many ships
- Needed at Grade 5
- Offered by an inconvenient or distant engineer
- A modification you regularly apply to new modules
- A modification whose materials you commonly keep stocked

Common examples include:

- Frame Shift Drive — Increased Range
- Thrusters — Dirty Drive Tuning
- Power Distributor — Charge Enhanced
- Power Plant — Armoured or Overcharged
- Shield Generator — Thermal Resistant or Reinforced
- Shield Booster — Heavy Duty or Resistance Augmented
- Multi-cannon — Overcharged
- Armour — Heavy Duty
- Hull Reinforcement Package — Heavy Duty
- Plasma Accelerator — Efficient
- Laser weapons — a frequently used combat blueprint

There is no single perfect pin list for every commander. Exploration, trading,
mining, PvE combat, PvP and Anti-Xeno builds use different modifications.

===============================================================================
COMMON MISTAKES
===============================================================================

MISTAKE 1:
Assuming there is only one pin in total.

CORRECTION:
You receive one pin per engineer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 2:
Pinning before ranking the engineer.

CORRECTION:
Remote engineering cannot raise engineer reputation. Rank the engineer in
person first.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 3:
Expecting to jump directly to Grade 5.

CORRECTION:
Every new module must progress from Grade 1 upward.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 4:
Expecting the Experimental Effect to be included.

CORRECTION:
Experimentals require an in-person engineer visit.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 5:
Pinning the wrong blueprint with a similar name.

CORRECTION:
Read the blueprint name carefully before confirming. For example, Increased
Range, Faster Boot Sequence and Shielded are different FSD blueprints.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 6:
The pin does not appear in Remote Workshop.

CHECK:

[ ] Is a compatible module fitted to the current ship?
[ ] Does the station offer Remote Workshop?
[ ] Is the correct blueprint actually pinned?
[ ] Has the required grade been unlocked with the pinning engineer?
[ ] Is the module an old grandfathered Legacy modification?
[ ] Did another blueprint recently replace the pin?

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 7:
Using two engineers to pin the same common blueprint unintentionally.

CORRECTION:
Spread pins across different useful modifications where possible.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MISTAKE 8:
Leaving the engineer without confirming the pin.

CORRECTION:
Check the Engineer panel before making the return journey.

===============================================================================
QUICK REFERENCE
===============================================================================

PIN LIMIT:
- One blueprint per engineer

PIN APPLIES TO:
- One regular blueprint type
- Compatible modules of any appropriate size
- Any of your ships

REMOTE WORKSHOP CAN:
- Apply the pinned main modification
- Progress a module through unlocked engineering grades
- Continue the same modification on a partially engineered module

REMOTE WORKSHOP CANNOT:
- Increase engineer reputation
- Unlock higher engineering grades
- Add or change Experimental Effects
- Apply a blueprint that is not pinned
- Skip directly to Grade 5

TO CHANGE A PIN:
- Visit the engineer in person
- Pin a different blueprint
- The engineer's previous pin is replaced

BEST TIME TO PIN:
- After ranking the engineer to the highest grade you require
