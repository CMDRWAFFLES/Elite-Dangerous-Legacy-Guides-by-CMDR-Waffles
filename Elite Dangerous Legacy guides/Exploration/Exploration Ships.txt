 4. ESSENTIAL MODULES
===============================================================================

For serious exploration, these are the three modules that matter most.


A-RATED FRAME SHIFT DRIVE
-------------------------

The FSD is the single most important module on an exploration ship.

Always fit the largest A-rated normal FSD the ship can use.

+----------------------+----------+---------------+
| Ship                 | FSD      | Module Price  |
+----------------------+----------+---------------+
| Hauler               | 2A FSD   | 160,224 Cr    |
| Dolphin              | 4A FSD   | 1,610,080 Cr  |
| Diamondback Explorer | 5A FSD   | 5,103,953 Cr  |
| Asp Explorer         | 5A FSD   | 5,103,953 Cr  |
| Krait Phantom        | 5A FSD   | 5,103,953 Cr  |
| Anaconda             | 6A FSD   | 16,179,530 Cr |
+----------------------+----------+---------------+


FUEL SCOOP
----------

A Fuel Scoop is essential for long-range exploration.

Fuel Scoops have ZERO mass.

This means:
  A bigger / better scoop does NOT reduce your jump range.

Higher-rated scoops simply refuel faster.

Recommended choices:

+----------------------+----------------+---------------+
| Ship                 | Recommended    | Price         |
+----------------------+----------------+---------------+
| Hauler               | 3A Scoop       | 902,950 Cr    |
| Dolphin              | 4A Scoop*      | 2,862,360 Cr  |
| Diamondback Explorer | 4A Scoop       | 2,862,360 Cr  |
| Asp Explorer         | 6B Scoop       | 7,190,900 Cr  |
| Asp Explorer         | 6A Scoop       | 28,763,610 Cr |
| Krait Phantom        | 6B Scoop       | 7,190,900 Cr  |
| Krait Phantom        | 6A Scoop       | 28,763,610 Cr |
| Anaconda             | 7B Scoop       | 22,795,160 Cr |
| Anaconda             | 7A Scoop       | 91,180,640 Cr |
+----------------------+----------------+---------------+

* Dolphin:
  A Class 5 Scoop fits, but for a long-range build it is often better to put
  a Class 5 Guardian FSD Booster in that slot and use a Class 4 Fuel Scoop.

B-rated scoop:
  Much cheaper.

A-rated scoop:
  Fastest refuelling.

Because Fuel Scoops have no mass, buying B instead of A is mainly a question
of money and refuelling speed.


DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER - DSS
------------------------------

Price:
  250,000 Cr

Purpose:
  Maps planets and moons with probes.

For an explorer:
  Buy one.

DSS mapping is where a large part of exploration income comes from.

 8. FSD EXPERIMENTAL EFFECT
===============================================================================

For Class 5 and larger exploration FSDs:

  RECOMMENDED:
    MASS MANAGER


Mass Manager gives:
  +4% Optimised Mass

Material cost:

  5 x Atypical Disrupted Wake Echoes
  3 x Galvanising Alloys
  1 x Eccentric Hyperspace Trajectory


USE MASS MANAGER ON:

  Diamondback Explorer - Class 5
  Asp Explorer         - Class 5
  Krait Phantom        - Class 5
  Anaconda             - Class 6


CLASS 4 AND SMALLER
-------------------

Deep Charge can produce slightly more maximum single-jump range on smaller
FSDs.

However:

  It increases fuel used per jump.

For a beginner long-range ship:

  Mass Manager is simpler if available.

For absolute maximum range on Class 4 and below:

  Deep Charge may give the higher single-jump figure.


===============================================================================
 9. PRE-ENGINEERED 5A FSD V1
===============================================================================

This is extremely useful in Legacy.

It is a special pre-engineered Class 5A Frame Shift Drive.

It has BOTH:

  Increased Range
  Faster Boot Sequence

It can jump roughly 2-3 LY farther than a normal fully-engineered 5A FSD
with Mass Manager, depending on the ship/build.


IT WORKS ON:

  Diamondback Explorer
  Asp Explorer
  Krait Phantom

and other ships that use a Class 5 FSD.


MATERIAL COST FOR EACH COPY
---------------------------

 18 x Datamined Wake Exceptions
 26 x Tellurium
 26 x Electrochemical Arrays
 28 x Chemical Processors


IMPORTANT:

You pay these materials EACH TIME you want another V1 FSD.

Do NOT apply a normal engineering blueprint over the V1 drive.

Doing that removes its special double-engineered benefit.

You MAY safely add an Experimental Effect.

Recommended:

  Add MASS MANAGER.

 12. MODULES RECOMMENDED FOR LONG TRIPS

For a trip lasting thousands or tens of thousands of light-years:


FUEL SCOOP
----------

DSS

GUARDIAN FSD BOOSTER

Strongly recommended.


SHIELD GENERATOR
----------------
Recommended.

Use:
  A small/light shield.

Purpose:
  Protects you from:
    Bad landings
    Bumps
    Accidental terrain impacts


AFMU
----

AFMU =
  Auto Field-Maintenance Unit

Purpose:
  Repairs damaged internal modules.

Can repair:
  FSD
  Thrusters
  DSS
  Sensors
  Most other modules

Cannot repair:
  Hull
  Power Plant
  Itself

Two AFMUs can repair each other.

AFMUs have no mass, so if you have spare internal slots there is little
jump-range penalty for carrying another one.


Example AFMU prices:

+------+---------------+
| AFMU | Price         |
+------+---------------+
| 2D   | 54,000 Cr     |
| 2A   | 1,458,000 Cr  |
| 3D   | 97,200 Cr     |
| 3A   | 2,624,400 Cr  |
| 4D   | 174,960 Cr    |
| 4A   | 4,723,920 Cr  |
+------+---------------+

A-rated:
  More expensive, more repair capability.

Cheap D/E-rated:
  Still useful.

  Carry at least one.

===============================================================================
 13. WHAT NOT TO OVERDO
===============================================================================

WEAPONS
-------

Usually unnecessary in deep-space exploration.

Removing them:
  Reduces mass.


GIANT SHIELDS
-------------

Usually unnecessary.

A small shield is normally enough to protect against landing mistakes.


EXTRA FUEL TANKS
----------------

Usually unnecessary.

More fuel means more carried mass.

Keep the ship's normal fuel tank unless you have a specific reason to change it.


ULTRA-LIGHT THRUSTERS
---------------------

Be careful.

Do not reduce your thrusters or Power Distributor so far that the ship becomes
unpleasant or cannot boost when you need it.

A few tenths of a light-year are not worth making the ship miserable to fly.

===============================================================================
 15. NEUTRON HIGHWAY - WHAT IS IT?
===============================================================================

A Neutron Star can supercharge your FSD.

Legacy neutron boost:

  Normal jump range x4

This is often described as:

  +300% bonus

Example:

  Normal jump:
    60 LY

  Neutron-supercharged jump:
    Up to about 240 LY


WHITE DWARF:

  Normal jump range x1.5

Only:
  +50%

White Dwarfs are much more dangerous to use because their exclusion zones are
large relative to their jet cones.

For normal neutron-highway travel:

  USE NEUTRON STARS.
  AVOID WHITE DWARFS.


===============================================================================
 16. WHAT YOU NEED FOR NEUTRON TRAVEL
===============================================================================

ABSOLUTELY TAKE:

  Fuel Scoop
  AFMU


STRONGLY RECOMMENDED:

  Second AFMU
  Heat Sink Launcher
  Enough normal fuel for several jumps
  Materials for AFMU synthesis


WHY THE AFMU IS REQUIRED
------------------------

Every neutron supercharge damages your FSD.

Each neutron supercharge damages the Frame Shift Drive.

Below:

  80% FSD health

the FSD can begin malfunctioning.

For sustained neutron-highway travel, carry an AFMU and repair before reaching
that threshold.


SAFE BEGINNER RULE:

  Repair the FSD around 85-90%.

Do not wait for it to begin malfunctioning.


===============================================================================
 17. HOW TO USE A NEUTRON STAR SAFELY
===============================================================================

1. Make sure you have a Fuel Scoop fitted.

2. Stay in Supercruise.

3. Identify the neutron star's jet cone.

4. NEVER fly toward the neutron star itself.

5. Enter near the OUTER END of a jet cone.

6. Point approximately AWAY from the neutron star.

7. Allow the cone to move the ship around.

8. Wait for:

     "FRAME SHIFT DRIVE SUPERCHARGED"

9. Fly OUT of the cone.

10. Select your next destination.

11. Make the boosted jump.


NEVER:

  Drop from Supercruise while inside the neutron jet.

This can be extremely dangerous.


===============================================================================
 18. NEUTRON HIGHWAY FSD REPAIRS
===============================================================================

After repeated boosts:

  Check:
    Right Panel -> Modules -> Frame Shift Drive

If FSD health approaches:

  85-90%

stop and repair.


SAFE REPAIR METHOD
------------------

1. Leave the neutron cone.

2. Move to a safe location.

3. Drop from Supercruise.

4. Stop the ship.

5. Activate the AFMU.

6. Select the Frame Shift Drive.

7. Choose Repair.

8. Wait for repair to finish.

9. Re-enable the FSD.

10. Continue travelling.

  -> Neutron
  -> Neutron

  Watch your fuel gauge on EVERY neutron jump.


Make regular refuelling stops at:

  O
  B
  A
  F
  G
  K
  M

  KGB FOAM

===============================================================================
 25. FINAL BEGINNER RULES
===============================================================================

1. Buy the best FSD before buying fancy optional equipment.

2. Engineer the FSD before worrying about minor engineering.

3. A Fuel Scoop is mandatory for serious deep-space travel.

4. A DSS is mandatory if you want proper exploration mapping payouts.

5. Guardian FSD Booster is one of the best exploration upgrades in Legacy.

6. For Class 5 ships, the pre-engineered 5A FSD V1 is excellent.

7. Carry an AFMU if you use neutron stars.

8. Repair the FSD BEFORE it falls below 80%.

9. Do not use White Dwarfs just because the route planner offers one.

10. Do not forget to refuel while chaining neutron stars.

11. A small shield is worth losing a tiny amount of jump range.

12. Do not sacrifice so much weight that the ship becomes horrible to fly.

13. Long expeditions are about reliability, not just the biggest LY number.

14. Keep enough credits for your rebuy.

15. Use equipment and engineering instructions specifically written for
    Legacy / Horizons 3.8.
