===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 EXPLORATION TERMINOLOGY GLOSSARY - ORGANIZED BY TOPIC
===============================================================================

This glossary is specifically for:

  Elite Dangerous: Legacy / Console
  Horizons 3.8

It groups exploration terms by subject so beginners can quickly find what they
need.


===============================================================================
 1. STELLAR BODIES
===============================================================================

STAR
----

A stellar body.

Explorers scan stars automatically / through normal system exploration and sell
their data to Universal Cartographics.


BH
--

Black Hole.


BLACK HOLE
----------

A stellar remnant.

Black Holes are classic exploration targets and are generally worth more than
ordinary stars when scanned.


NS
--

Neutron Star.


NEUTRON STAR
------------

A compact stellar remnant with powerful jet cones.

Useful for:

  - Exploration
  - Codex discoveries
  - FSD supercharging


A Neutron Star can boost your current FSD jump range to:

  x4

for one jump.


WD
--

White Dwarf.


WHITE DWARF
-----------

A compact stellar remnant with jet cones.

A White Dwarf can boost your current FSD jump range to:

  x1.5

for one jump.

They are generally more dangerous for beginners than Neutron Stars because of
their exclusion-zone / jet-cone geometry.


BROWN DWARF
-----------

Very low-mass stellar objects.

Common classes:

  L
  T
  Y

They are NOT fuel-scoopable.


SCOOPABLE STAR
--------------

A star from which a Fuel Scoop can collect hydrogen fuel.

Main scoopable classes:

  O
  B
  A
  F
  G
  K
  M


KGB FOAM
--------

Player mnemonic for the scoopable main star classes:

  K
  G
  B
  F
  O
  A
  M

The order is only for remembering them.

The scoopable classes are:

  O B A F G K M


SAG A*
------

Short for:

  Sagittarius A*

The Supermassive Black Hole at the centre of the Milky Way.


SAGITTARIUS A*
--------------

See:

  Sag A*


GALACTIC CORE
-------------

The dense central part of the galaxy.

Sagittarius A* is located there.


THE CORE
--------

Player nickname for:

  The Galactic Core


===============================================================================
 2. PLANETS, MOONS & PLANETOID TYPES
===============================================================================

ELW
---

Earth-like World.


EARTH-LIKE WORLD
----------------

One of the most valuable normal exploration planet types.

Usually worth:

  FSS scanning
  DSS mapping


WW
--

Water World.


WATER WORLD
-----------

A valuable planet type.

Always check whether it is also:

  Terraformable


TWW
---

Terraformable Water World.

A Water World marked as:

  "a candidate for terraforming"

Very valuable for exploration.


AW
--

Ammonia World.


AMMONIA WORLD
-------------

A rare and valuable planet type.

Usually worth:

  FSS scanning
  DSS mapping


HMC
---

High Metal Content World.


HIGH METAL CONTENT WORLD
------------------------

A common rocky planet type.

Normal HMCs are moderately valuable.

Terraformable HMCs are much more valuable.


THMC
----

Terraformable High Metal Content World.

One of the more valuable exploration planet types.


MRB
---

Metal-Rich Body.


METAL-RICH BODY
---------------

A rocky planet class that generally pays more than a normal HMC.


ROCKY BODY
----------

A common low-value planet type.

Normally worth little for exploration credits.

If terraformable, however, its value can increase dramatically.


ICY BODY
--------

A common low-value planet / moon type.

Usually not worth a long DSS detour purely for credits.


ROCKY-ICE BODY
--------------

A mixed rocky / icy planet or moon type.

Usually low-value for exploration credits.


GG
--

Gas Giant.


GAS GIANT
---------

Large gaseous planet.

Common categories include:

  Class I
  Class II
  Class III
  Class IV
  Class V

Class II is generally the most valuable normal Gas Giant type for exploration.


TERRAFORMABLE / TF
------------------

A planet whose System Map description says it is:

  "a candidate for terraforming"

Terraformability can greatly increase exploration value.


TF
--

Short for:

  Terraformable


HIGH-G WORLD
------------

A landable planet with unusually high surface gravity.

Interesting to visit, but potentially dangerous to land on.


RINGED WORLD
------------

A planet, moon or other body surrounded by planetary rings.

Rings may be:

  Icy
  Rocky
  Metallic
  Metal Rich


===============================================================================
 3. SCANNING & MAPPING
===============================================================================

HONK
----

Player nickname for using the Discovery Scanner.

The scanner makes a distinctive sound when charged.

Basic beginner rule:

  Honk every new system.


DISCOVERY SCANNER
-----------------

Built-in scanner used to perform the system:

  Honk

It detects the system's astronomical signals.


FSS
---

Full Spectrum System Scanner.

Built-in scanner used to identify:

  - Planets
  - Moons
  - Other system signals

Basic exploration flow:

  Honk
      ->
  FSS
      ->
  DSS valuable bodies


FULL SPECTRUM SYSTEM SCANNER
----------------------------

See:

  FSS


DSS
---

Detailed Surface Scanner.

Optional internal module used to fire probes at:

  - Planets
  - Moons
  - Rings

Used to:

  - Map planets
  - Increase exploration payout
  - Earn First Mapped tags
  - Reveal Legacy Geological Sites
  - Reveal Legacy Biological Sites
  - Reveal ring resource hotspots


DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER
------------------------

See:

  DSS


DSS EFFICIENCY BONUS
--------------------

Extra mapping payout earned when a body is mapped using no more probes than the
displayed:

  Efficiency Target


PROBE EFFICIENCY
----------------

See:

  DSS Efficiency Bonus


PRE-ENGINEERED DSS
------------------

Special Technology Broker Detailed Surface Scanner with improved probe coverage.

Valid Legacy equipment.


COMPOSITION SCANNER
-------------------

Short for:

  Short Range Composition Scanner

Built into ships and SRVs.

Used in Analysis Mode to scan:

  - Biological structures
  - Geological structures
  - Objects inside Notable Stellar Phenomena


SHORT RANGE COMPOSITION SCANNER
-------------------------------

See:

  Composition Scanner


===============================================================================
 4. EXPLORATION MODULES
===============================================================================

FSD
---

Frame Shift Drive.

Used for:

  - Hyperspace jumps between star systems
  - Supercruise inside a system


FUEL SCOOP
----------

Module used to refuel directly from scoopable stars.

Essential for serious exploration.


AFMU
----

Auto Field-Maintenance Unit.

Repairs damaged internal modules while away from stations.

Useful for:

  - FSD repairs
  - Neutron Highway travel
  - Long expeditions

AFMUs cannot normally repair:

  - Hull
  - Power Plant
  - Themselves

Two AFMUs can repair each other.


GUARDIAN FSD BOOSTER
--------------------

Guardian optional internal module that adds a fixed amount of jump range.

Largest version:

  5H

Adds:

  +10.5 LY

Very popular on exploration ships.


HEAT SINK
---------

Heat Sink Launcher.

Utility module used to rapidly reduce ship heat.

Useful for:

  - Accidental overheating
  - Bad star approaches
  - Emergency heat management


REPAIR LIMPET CONTROLLER
------------------------

Optional module used to repair ship hull damage.

Requires:

  Cargo Rack
  Limpets


PLANETARY VEHICLE HANGAR
------------------------

Optional internal module used to carry an SRV.


SRV
---

Surface Recon Vehicle.

In Legacy the standard exploration SRV is the:

  Scarab

Used for:

  - Surface driving
  - Material collection
  - Guardian sites
  - Geological Sites
  - Biological Sites


SUPERCRUISE ASSIST
------------------

Optional convenience module that can automate parts of Supercruise travel.


SC ASSIST
---------

Short for:

  Supercruise Assist


DOCKING COMPUTER
----------------

Optional module used to automate docking.

Not required for exploration.


===============================================================================
 5. EXPLORATION SHIPS & SHIP NICKNAMES
===============================================================================

DBX
---

Diamondback Explorer.

Popular cheap exploration ship.

Known for:

  - Excellent jump range
  - Low cost
  - Small landing footprint
  - Class 4 maximum Fuel Scoop


ASPX
----

Asp Explorer.

One of the classic exploration ships.

Known for:

  - Good jump range
  - Excellent cockpit view
  - Class 6 Fuel Scoop
  - Medium pad size


KRAIT PHANTOM
-------------

Popular medium long-range exploration ship.

Known for:

  - Strong jump range
  - Good internal space
  - Class 6 Fuel Scoop
  - Medium pad size


DOLPHIN
-------

Small exploration-capable ship famous for running very cool.

Useful for:

  - Budget exploration
  - Comfortable fuel scooping
  - Small landing footprint


ANACONDA
--------

Large ship with extremely high potential jump range.

Often used for:

  - Long expeditions
  - Neutron Highway travel
  - Maximum-range builds


JUMPCONDA
---------

Player nickname for an Anaconda built primarily for:

  Maximum or very high jump range

Usually includes:

  - Lightweight modules
  - Engineered 6A FSD
  - Guardian FSD Booster
  - Large Fuel Scoop


JUMP RANGE BUILD
----------------

Ship build designed mainly to reduce mass and maximize hyperspace jump range.


===============================================================================
 6. ROUTING, DISTANCE & TRAVEL
===============================================================================

LY
--

Light-Year.

Standard unit for:

  - Distance between star systems
  - Ship jump range


JUMP RANGE
----------

Maximum hyperspace distance your ship can currently travel in one jump.

Affected by:

  - Ship mass
  - Fuel
  - Cargo
  - FSD
  - Engineering
  - Guardian FSD Booster


SC
--

Supercruise.


SUPERCRUISE
-----------

Faster-than-light travel inside a star system.

Used to travel between:

  - Stars
  - Planets
  - Moons
  - Stations
  - Signal sources


FASTEST ROUTE
-------------

Galaxy Map route mode that uses longer jumps.

Best for:

  Travelling somewhere quickly.


ECONOMICAL ROUTE
----------------

Galaxy Map route mode that uses many shorter jumps.

Best for:

  - Visiting many systems
  - Searching for undiscovered systems
  - Detailed regional exploration


NEUTRON HIGHWAY
---------------

Player term for a long-distance route that chains Neutron Stars together.

Each Neutron Star can give:

  x4 current jump range

for one jump.


NEUTRON ROUTE
-------------

A route deliberately planned through multiple Neutron Stars.


NEUTRON SCOOPING
----------------

Player term for flying through a Neutron Star jet cone to supercharge the FSD.

Requires:

  Fuel Scoop

Repeated use damages the FSD.


JET-CONE BOOST
--------------

Galaxy Map routing option that allows routes to use:

  Neutron Stars
  White Dwarfs


SUPERCHARGE
-----------

Usually means temporarily increasing FSD jump range using:

  Neutron Star jet
  or
  White Dwarf jet


FSD BOOST
---------

Can refer to:

  FSD Injection / Jumponium

or:

  Neutron / White Dwarf supercharging

Check the context.


JUMONIUM / JUMPONIUM
--------------------

Community nickname for:

  FSD Injection

A synthesized one-jump range boost.

Correct common spelling:

  Jumponium


FSD INJECTION
-------------

Synthesized one-jump FSD range boost.

Legacy levels:

  Basic:
      +25%

  Standard:
      +50%

  Premium:
      +100%


GALACTIC PLANE
--------------

The broad flat disc of the galaxy.

Explorers often travel above or below it to escape heavily travelled routes.


GALACTIC RIM
------------

Sparse outer edge of the galaxy.

High jump range and Jumponium become increasingly useful there.


THE RIM
-------

Player nickname for:

  Galactic Rim


BEAGLE POINT
------------

Famous remote exploration destination near the far side of the galaxy.


COLONIA
-------

Major inhabited region roughly 22,000 LY from Sol.

One of the classic long-distance destinations.


JACKSON'S LIGHTHOUSE
--------------------

Well-known Neutron Star relatively close to the Bubble.

Often used for practicing neutron supercharging.

 7. ENGINEERING, MATERIALS & SYNTHESIS
===============================================================================

MATS
----

Short for:

  Materials


RAW MATS
--------

Raw Materials.

Examples:

  Carbon
  Vanadium
  Germanium
  Arsenic
  Niobium
  Yttrium
  Polonium


ENGINEERED FSD
--------------

Frame Shift Drive modified by an Engineer.

For explorers, the most important blueprint is usually:

  Increased Range


MASS MANAGER
------------

Frame Shift Drive experimental effect.

Commonly used on larger exploration FSDs to increase jump range.


PRE-ENGINEERED FSD / FSD V1
----------------------------

Special Technology Broker Frame Shift Drive with engineering already applied.

The famous exploration version is:

  5A FSD V1

Useful on ships such as:

  - Diamondback Explorer
  - Asp Explorer
  - Krait Phantom

Valid Legacy equipment.


JUMPONIUM
---------

See:

  FSD Injection


BASIC FSD INJECTION
-------------------

One-jump boost:

  +25%

Legacy materials:

  1 Carbon
  1 Vanadium
  1 Germanium


STANDARD FSD INJECTION
----------------------

One-jump boost:

  +50%

Legacy materials:

  1 Carbon
  1 Vanadium
  1 Germanium
  1 Cadmium
  1 Niobium


PREMIUM FSD INJECTION
---------------------

One-jump boost:

  +100%

Legacy materials:

  1 Carbon
  1 Germanium
  1 Arsenic
  1 Niobium
  1 Yttrium
  1 Polonium


===============================================================================
 8. SURFACE EXPLORATION & POIs
===============================================================================

POI
---

Point of Interest.

General term for a location worth visiting.


GEO SITE
--------

Short for:

  Geological Site

In Legacy, DSS mapping suitable bodies can reveal numbered targets such as:

  Geological Site 1
  Geological Site 2


These can contain:

  - Geysers
  - Fumaroles
  - Gas Vents
  - Lava Spouts
  - Material-bearing crystals


BIO SITE
--------

Short for:

  Biological Site

In Legacy, DSS mapping suitable bodies can reveal numbered targets such as:

  Biological Site 1
  Biological Site 2


These can contain:

  - Crystalline Shards
  - Brain Trees
  - Bark Mounds
  - Sinuous Tubers
  - Other Legacy biological structures


CRYSTALLINE SHARDS
------------------

Legacy biological structures used for high-grade raw-material farming.

Classic systems include:

  HIP 36601
  Outotz LS-K d8-3


BRAIN TREES
-----------

Legacy surface biological structures commonly associated with regions of
Guardian activity.


BARK MOUNDS
-----------

Legacy fungal-like surface biological structures.


SINUOUS TUBERS
--------------

Legacy colourful tubular biological structures found on suitable airless
volcanic worlds.


NSP
---

Notable Stellar Phenomena.


NOTABLE STELLAR PHENOMENA
-------------------------

Persistent FSS signal sources containing unusual deep-space objects.

Can contain:

  - Lagrange Clouds
  - Crystals
  - Molluscs
  - Pods
  - Tree-like organisms
  - Anomalies


LAGRANGE CLOUD
--------------

A cloud-like space phenomenon found inside some Notable Stellar Phenomena.

May contain other scannable objects.


RING HOTSPOT
------------

Mining-resource area revealed by firing a DSS probe into a planetary ring.

Examples:

  - Platinum
  - Painite
  - Low Temperature Diamonds

Hotspots are mining information, not an exploration payout multiplier.


===============================================================================
 9. DISCOVERY TAGS, CODEX & DATA
===============================================================================

UC
--

Universal Cartographics.


UNIVERSAL CARTOGRAPHICS
-----------------------

Service used to sell exploration data.


EXPLORATION DATA
----------------

Data collected by scanning stars, planets and moons.

Unsold exploration data is lost if your ship is destroyed.


FD
--

First Discovery / First Discovered.


FIRST DISCOVERED / FIRST DISCOVERY
----------------------------------

Permanent commander tag given if you are the first commander to discover a
body and successfully sell the data.


FM
--

First Mapped.


FIRST MAPPED
------------

Permanent commander tag given if you are the first commander to DSS-map a body
and successfully sell the data.


TAG
---

Player term for a commander name attached to a body.

Examples:

  First Discovered By
  First Mapped By


TAGGING
-------

Trying to obtain First Discovery and First Mapped commander tags.


UNTAGGED
--------

Player term for a body or system with no visible First Discovered / First
Mapped commander tag.


VIRGIN SYSTEM
-------------

Player nickname for a system that appears never to have been previously
discovered and sold.


CODEX
-----

In-game exploration record system.

The galaxy is divided into:

  42 Codex regions


The Codex tracks categories such as:

  - Stellar bodies
  - Biological entities
  - Geological entities
  - Other exploration discoveries


CODEX - RUMOURED
----------------

The game suggests that a discovery type may exist in that galactic region.


CODEX - REPORTED
----------------

Another Legacy commander has reported that type of discovery in that region.


CODEX - CONFIRMED / DISCOVERED BY YOU
-------------------------------------

You personally found and scanned that type of discovery in the region.


===============================================================================
 10. FLEET CARRIERS & RESCUE
===============================================================================

FC
--

Fleet Carrier.


FLEET CARRIER
-------------

Player-owned mobile megaship.

Can provide useful deep-space services.

Legacy Fleet Carrier positions and markets are separate server-dependent data.

Use Legacy / Console filters on third-party tools where available.


FLEET CARRIER UNIVERSAL CARTOGRAPHICS
-------------------------------------

Allows exploration data to be sold aboard a Fleet Carrier.

Commander payout:

  75%

Normal station payout:

  100%

Carrier penalty:

  -25%


DSSA
----

Deep Space Support Array.

Community project involving Fleet Carriers positioned in remote areas.

IMPORTANT:

Current carrier positions are server-dependent.

Do not assume current PC DSSA positions are identical in Legacy.


FUEL RAT
--------

Member of:

  The Fuel Rats

A player rescue organisation specializing in commanders stranded without fuel.


===============================================================================
 11. COMMON EXPLORER NICKNAMES & SLANG
===============================================================================

BUBBLE
------

Player nickname for the main human-inhabited region around Sol and nearby
populated systems.


THE BLACK
---------

Common explorer nickname for:

  Deep space


DEEP BLACK
----------

Informal term for very remote deep space.


HEADING INTO THE BLACK
----------------------

Means:

  Leaving inhabited space to explore.


BUCKYBALLING
------------

Player term for travelling as fast as possible.

Usually involves:

  - Minimal stopping
  - High jump range
  - Fast routing
  - Sometimes Neutron Highway use


SPACE MADNESS
-------------

Joking term for boredom or strange behaviour caused by very long repetitive
exploration trips.


FLYING PAPER
------------

Informal description of an extremely lightweight exploration ship with very
little protection.

Usually sacrifices:

  - Shields
  - Hull strength
  - Thruster performance
  - Other safety margins

for:

  Maximum jump range


JUMPING ON THE HIGHWAY
----------------------

Usually means:

  Starting a Neutron Highway route.


GOING TO SAG A*
---------------

Travelling to:

  Sagittarius A*
