===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 LONG EXPEDITION SURVIVAL & REPAIR GUIDE
===============================================================================

The goal of a long expedition is not just to get far away.

It is to:

  GET HOME ALIVE WITH THE DATA.


Unsold exploration data is lost if your ship is destroyed.

===============================================================================
 2. WHAT THE AFMU REPAIRS
===============================================================================

AFMU:

  Auto Field-Maintenance Unit


It can repair most internal modules.

Examples:

  - Frame Shift Drive
  - Thrusters
  - Sensors
  - Life Support
  - Detailed Surface Scanner
  - Power Distributor
  - Fuel Scoop


It can also repair an UNBROKEN cockpit canopy.


===============================================================================
 3. WHAT THE AFMU CANNOT REPAIR
===============================================================================

An AFMU cannot normally repair:

  - The ship's HULL
  - The POWER PLANT
  - ITSELF
  - A canopy that has already completely shattered


This is why:

  TWO AFMUs are useful.

AFMU #1 repairs AFMU #2.

AFMU #2 repairs AFMU #1.


===============================================================================
 4. HOW TO USE THE AFMU SAFELY
===============================================================================

Do not repair important flight modules while in Supercruise.


SAFE METHOD:

  1. Move away from stars / hazards.

  2. Drop into normal space.

  3. Stop the ship.

  4. Open:
       Right Panel -> Modules

  5. Switch on the AFMU if it is normally powered off.

  6. Select the damaged module.

  7. Choose:
       Repair

  8. Wait.

  9. Re-enable the repaired module.

 10. Switch the AFMU off again if desired.


The module being repaired is OFFLINE while the AFMU works.


IMPORTANT:

Repairing:

  THRUSTERS
  or
  FRAME SHIFT DRIVE

while in Supercruise can force you out of Supercruise.


===============================================================================
 5. NEUTRON DAMAGE
===============================================================================

Repeated neutron supercharging damages the FSD.

Once the FSD falls below:

  80%

it can begin malfunctioning.


Safe neutron-travel habit:

  Repair around:
      85-90%


This keeps a safety margin.


===============================================================================
 6. AFMU REFILL SYNTHESIS
===============================================================================

AFMU ammunition can be synthesized in deep space.


BASIC AFMU REFILL:

  2 Nickel
  2 Zinc
  2 Chromium
  3 Vanadium


This is why long-range explorers should leave with useful raw materials already
stocked.


===============================================================================
 7. HULL DAMAGE
===============================================================================

AFMUs do NOT repair hull damage.


To repair your own hull in deep space, fit:

  REPAIR LIMPET CONTROLLER
  +
  CARGO RACK


Then launch a Repair Limpet with no external target selected.


===============================================================================
 8. SYNTHESIZING LIMPETS
===============================================================================

You do not need to carry dozens of limpets from the Bubble.

Legacy synthesis:

  10 Iron
  10 Nickel

creates:

  4 Limpets


This is excellent for explorers because cargo has mass.


A good strategy:

  Carry:
      0-4 limpets

  Keep:
      Iron
      Nickel

  Synthesize more only if needed.


===============================================================================
 9. CANOPY DAMAGE
===============================================================================

There are two different situations.


DAMAGED BUT STILL INTACT CANOPY
-------------------------------

An AFMU can repair it.


FULLY BROKEN CANOPY
-------------------

The cockpit depressurizes.

Your Life Support emergency oxygen begins counting down.

An AFMU cannot restore an already shattered canopy.


Your priorities become:

  1. Do not panic.
  2. Find somewhere to dock.
  3. Use Life Support synthesis if needed.


===============================================================================
 10. LIFE SUPPORT SYNTHESIS
===============================================================================

Legacy Life Support refill:

  2 Iron
  1 Nickel


This refills the emergency oxygen supply.


If the canopy is broken:

  Keep these materials available.


Do not wait until only seconds remain before synthesizing.


===============================================================================
 11. POWER PLANT DAMAGE
===============================================================================

The Power Plant is the explorer's major repair limitation.

An AFMU cannot repair it normally.


Therefore:

  Avoid severe overheating.
  Avoid crashing.
  Avoid unnecessary combat.


If the Power Plant becomes badly damaged:

  Your safest plan is to reach a station or suitable repair facility.


===============================================================================
 12. REBOOT / REPAIR
===============================================================================

There is an emergency function:

  REBOOT / REPAIR


Location:

  Right Panel -> Ship / Functions area


Purpose:

  It can cannibalize integrity from healthier modules to bring broken modules
  back online.


Use it if something critical is at:

  0%


Examples:

  Thrusters
  Sensors
  FSD


IMPORTANT:

Reboot / Repair:

  - Is an emergency procedure
  - Turns the ship systems off temporarily
  - Does NOT repair hull
  - Does NOT repair a failed canopy


Use an AFMU first when possible.


===============================================================================
 13. HEAT EMERGENCIES
===============================================================================

Common causes:

  - Flying too close to a star
  - Charging FSD while still deep in the star's heat
  - Dropping too close to a stellar object
  - Bad neutron / white-dwarf handling


IF HEAT IS RISING:

  1. Point away from the heat source.

  2. Stop fuel scooping if necessary.

  3. Move away.

  4. Cancel unnecessary heat-producing actions.

  5. Use a Heat Sink if needed.


Repeated or severe overheating can damage modules and eventually the hull.


===============================================================================
 14. HIGH-GRAVITY PLANET LANDINGS
===============================================================================

High gravity can destroy an exploration ship faster than almost anything else.


BEFORE DESCENDING:

  CHECK THE PLANET'S GRAVITY.


The important Legacy flight-model detail is:

  The ship's VENTRAL / UP thrusters
  and
  the ship's REAR / MAIN forward engines

receive special high-gravity assistance from Flight Assist.

This is why even a lightly-built exploration ship can land on and leave very
high-G worlds if flown correctly.


DO NOT THINK OF THE VERTICAL THRUSTERS AS "TOO SMALL"
------------------------------------------------------

Although the manoeuvring thrusters look much smaller than the main engines,
Elite Dangerous deliberately boosts the upward / ventral thrust available in
high gravity.

With the ship kept LEVEL and its BELLY facing the planet:

  UPWARD VERTICAL THRUST can hold the ship against gravity.

This is the safest basic high-G technique.


SAFE CONTROLLED DESCENT
-----------------------

For a normal controlled high-G landing:

  1. Exit glide with plenty of altitude.

  2. Keep the ship almost LEVEL.

  3. Keep the BELLY facing the planet.

  4. Avoid rolling or flying inverted.

  5. Keep your descent rate LOW.

  6. Use the UP / VERTICAL thrusters to arrest the descent.

  7. Make small, patient reductions in altitude.

  8. Do not allow a large downward velocity to build up.


The important danger is MOMENTUM.

Even though the ventral thrusters can oppose the gravity, they still need time
to remove a fast downward velocity.


===============================================================================
 HIGH-RATE DESCENT: 90-DEGREE MAIN-THRUSTER BRAKING
===============================================================================

If you are already descending too quickly AND still have enough altitude, you
can deliberately rotate the ship so the MAIN ENGINES point directly away from
the planet.

In other words:

  NOSE:
      90 DEGREES STRAIGHT UP

  MAIN / REAR THRUSTERS:
      POINTING DOWN TOWARD THE PLANET

Then apply:

  FORWARD THROTTLE

and, if your ship can boost and the situation requires it:

  BOOST


This turns the powerful rear/main thrust into upward acceleration and can help
arrest a dangerous descent.


IMPORTANT:

  Do this while you still have ALTITUDE.

  Do NOT wait until you are only a few hundred metres above the ground.


WHY THE 90-DEGREE ANGLE MATTERS
-------------------------------

The useful high-G thrust directions are:

  BELLY-DOWN / VENTRAL UP THRUST

and:

  NOSE-UP / REAR MAIN THRUST


Do NOT:

  - Roll onto your side
  - Go inverted
  - Pitch beyond 90 degrees so your back faces the planet


The lateral and topside thrust directions do NOT receive the same useful
gravity compensation.

A bad roll on a very high-G world can make the ship start falling extremely
quickly.


THE TRANSITION IS THE DANGEROUS PART
------------------------------------

When changing from:

  BELLY-DOWN VERTICAL THRUST

to:

  90-DEGREE MAIN-ENGINE THRUST

the rear thrusters may need a short moment to build their high-G thrust.

During that transition:

  THE SHIP CAN CONTINUE FALLING.


Therefore:

  - Start the manoeuvre high enough
  - Do not pitch up too late
  - Watch vertical speed / altitude
  - Be ready to return to a level belly-down attitude if necessary


BEGINNER RECOMMENDATION
-----------------------

For a normal landing:

  KEEP THE SHIP LEVEL
  +
  USE VERTICAL UP THRUST


If you have built up too much downward speed and still have plenty of altitude:

  ROTATE NOSE-UP TO 90 DEGREES
  +
  USE THE MAIN ENGINES TO BRAKE THE DESCENT


If the situation is desperate and your ship can boost:

  90-DEGREE NOSE-UP
  +
  FULL MAIN THRUST
  +
  BOOST

can provide an emergency recovery attempt.


SHIELDS
-------

Use a shield when attempting high-G landings.

A small shield can turn a minor mistake into:

  SHIELD DAMAGE

instead of:

  LOST SHIP
  LOST EXPLORATION DATA


HIGH-G BEGINNER RULES
---------------------

  CHECK GRAVITY BEFORE LANDING.

  KEEP THE BELLY TOWARD THE PLANET.

  KEEP DESCENT SPEED LOW.

  DO NOT ROLL.

  DO NOT GO INVERTED.

  USE VERTICAL UP THRUST FOR CONTROLLED DESCENT.

  USE 90-DEGREE NOSE-UP MAIN-THRUSTER BRAKING IF YOU NEED TO ARREST A
  FASTER DESCENT AND STILL HAVE ENOUGH ALTITUDE.

  NEVER WAIT UNTIL THE LAST MOMENT.

 15. FUEL EMERGENCY - USE JUMPONIUM BEFORE GIVING UP
===============================================================================

If your fuel is dangerously low:

  STOP MAKING RANDOM JUMPS.


A very important emergency option is:

  FSD INJECTION / "JUMPONIUM"


Jumponium does NOT add fuel to your tank.

Instead, it increases the Frame Shift Drive's jump range for ONE jump.

WHY THIS CAN SAVE YOU
---------------------

When you have only a small amount of fuel left, the nearest scoopable star may
be outside your normal remaining jump capability.

An FSD Injection increases the distance your FSD can cover on that next jump.

In practical terms, this can let the small amount of fuel you still have carry
you far enough to reach a scoopable star that was otherwise unreachable.


EXAMPLE:

  Maximum jump you can currently make:
      12 LY

  Nearest scoopable star:
      14 LY away


Without injection:

      YOU CANNOT REACH IT.


Basic Jumponium:

      12 LY x 1.25
          =
      15 LY


The 14 LY fuel star may now be reachable.


IMPORTANT:

  Jumponium does NOT create fuel.

  It only increases the FSD's available range for that one jump.

  You still need enough fuel remaining for the boosted jump itself.


EMERGENCY PROCEDURE
-------------------

  1. STOP jumping.

  2. Open the Galaxy Map.

  3. Look for the nearest scoopable star:

       O
       B
       A
       F
       G
       K
       M

  4. Check your current available jump range.

  5. If the star is just outside normal range:

       Try BASIC first:
           +25%

  6. If Basic is not enough:

       Try STANDARD:
           +50%

  7. If Standard is not enough:

       Try PREMIUM:
           +100%

  8. Synthesize the smallest injection that reaches the fuel star.

  9. Target the scoopable star directly.

 10. Make the boosted jump.

 11. FUEL SCOOP IMMEDIATELY after arrival.


HOW TO SYNTHESIZE IT
--------------------

Open:

  RIGHT PANEL
      ->
  INVENTORY
      ->
  SYNTHESIS
      ->
  FSD INJECTION


Choose:

  BASIC
  STANDARD
  or
  PREMIUM


Then make the boosted hyperspace jump.


WHY USE THE SMALLEST INJECTION?
-------------------------------

Premium Jumponium uses rare materials such as:

  Yttrium
  Polonium


Do not waste a Premium injection if:

  Basic
  or
  Standard

will reach the star.


ALSO CHECK FOR A SCOOPABLE SECONDARY STAR
-----------------------------------------

The star you arrive beside is normally the system's primary arrival star.

A system can still contain another scoopable star farther away.

Before spending Jumponium materials:

  Open the System Map.

If the same system contains a reachable:

  O / B / A / F / G / K / M star

you may be able to fly to it in Supercruise and refuel without making another
hyperspace jump.


FUEL EMERGENCY ORDER
--------------------

When low on fuel:

  1. Check the CURRENT SYSTEM for a scoopable secondary star.

  2. Check nearby scoopable systems in the Galaxy Map.

  3. Use Basic / Standard / Premium Jumponium if it makes one reachable.

  4. Do NOT waste fuel on experimental jumps.

  5. If you still cannot reach fuel:
       STOP.

  6. Contact the Fuel Rats.


===============================================================================
 16. FUEL RATS
===============================================================================

The Fuel Rats are a player rescue organization specialising in fuel emergencies.

They support multiple game platforms, including Legacy-related rescue cases.


If you are stranded:

  DO NOT keep jumping around.

  DO NOT self-destruct.

  Contact:
      Fuel Rats


When requesting rescue, be ready to provide:

  - Commander name
  - Platform
  - Game branch / Legacy
  - Current system
  - Oxygen status if canopy is broken


If oxygen is already counting down:

  Tell them immediately.


===============================================================================
 17. UNSOLD EXPLORATION DATA
===============================================================================

Remember:

  UNSOLD DATA DIES WITH THE SHIP.


If you have been away for weeks or months:

  Treat the return trip seriously.


When you reach inhabited space:

  - Avoid unnecessary combat
  - Do not fly recklessly
  - Do not attempt a silly high-G landing
  - Dock carefully
  - Sell the data


===============================================================================
 18. FLEET CARRIER DATA SALE
===============================================================================

A Fleet Carrier can be useful as a safety checkpoint.

But Universal Cartographics on a carrier pays the commander:

  75%

of the normal payout.


Normal Universal Cartographics:

  100%


Carrier sale:

  75%


Penalty:

  -25%


Use a carrier when:

  Safety is worth more to you than the lost credits.


Use a normal station when:

  You want maximum payout.


Legacy warning:

  Current carrier locations on modern third-party tools are server-dependent.

  Use Legacy / Console data where available.

 20. WHAT AN SRV ADDS TO SURVIVAL
===============================================================================

An SRV lets you gather raw materials while far from civilisation.

This can replenish materials for:

  - FSD injections
  - AFMU refills
  - Limpet synthesis
  - Life Support synthesis
  - SRV repairs / fuel / ammunition


For a serious long expedition:

  Bringing an SRV is recommended.
