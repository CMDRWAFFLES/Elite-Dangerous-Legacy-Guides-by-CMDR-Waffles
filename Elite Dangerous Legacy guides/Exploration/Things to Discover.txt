===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 THINGS TO DISCOVER WHILE EXPLORING
===============================================================================

Exploration is not only about credits.

Legacy contains a huge number of unusual stellar, planetary, geological and
biological discoveries.

This guide gives beginners a list of things worth watching for.


===============================================================================
 1. HIGH-VALUE PLANETS
===============================================================================

The classic valuable discoveries are:

  EARTH-LIKE WORLD

  WATER WORLD

  AMMONIA WORLD

  TERRAFORMABLE HIGH METAL CONTENT WORLD

  TERRAFORMABLE ROCKY BODY


These are worth checking and usually worth DSS mapping.


===============================================================================
 2. TERRAFORMABLE WORLDS
===============================================================================

Terraformability can massively increase exploration value.


After FSS scanning:

  Open the System Map.

Look for:

  "This body is a candidate for terraforming."


Check especially:

  - Water Worlds
  - High Metal Content Worlds
  - Rocky Bodies


A seemingly ordinary body can become a very valuable discovery if it is a
terraforming candidate.


===============================================================================
 3. RARE OR INTERESTING PLANET CLASSES
===============================================================================

Other interesting finds include:

  Metal-Rich Bodies

  Gas Giants with Water-Based Life

  Gas Giants with Ammonia-Based Life

  Water Giants

  Helium-Rich Gas Giants

  Helium Gas Giants

  Ringed worlds

  Extremely hot worlds

  Extremely cold worlds

  Very high-gravity landable worlds


Not all of these pay well.

They are interesting because they are uncommon or visually unusual.


===============================================================================
 4. INTERESTING STARS
===============================================================================

Keep an eye out for:

  O-class stars

  Giant stars

  Supergiants

  Carbon stars

  Wolf-Rayet stars

  White Dwarfs

  Neutron Stars

  Black Holes


Some are rare.

Some are visually spectacular.

Some are useful for travel.


===============================================================================
 5. BLACK HOLES
===============================================================================

Black Holes are among the classic deep-space discoveries.

Interesting targets include:

  - Stellar-mass Black Holes
  - Multiple-Black-Hole systems
  - Black Holes with nearby stars
  - Planetary systems orbiting Black Holes
  - Sagittarius A*


Their gravitational lensing makes them visually distinctive.


===============================================================================
 6. NEUTRON STARS
===============================================================================

Neutron Stars are interesting for two reasons:

  1. Exploration / Codex interest
  2. FSD supercharging


A neutron supercharge gives:

  NORMAL RANGE x4


If you plan to use them repeatedly:

  Carry an AFMU.


===============================================================================
 7. MULTIPLE-STAR SYSTEMS
===============================================================================

Do not ignore ordinary stars just because they pay little.

Unusual system architecture can be more memorable than credits.


Look for:

  Binary stars

  Triple-star systems

  Four or more stars

  Very close stellar pairs

  Planets orbiting multiple stars

  Moons with dramatic nearby parent planets

  Bodies orbiting distant secondary stars


===============================================================================
 8. RINGS
===============================================================================

Interesting ring discoveries include:

  Huge ring systems

  Extremely narrow rings

  Multiple ring bands

  Moons orbiting close to rings

  Ringed landable bodies

  Ringed high-value worlds

  Ring systems with unusual lighting


Ring composition may be:

  Icy

  Rocky

  Metallic

  Metal Rich


DSS probe a ring if you also want to reveal mining hotspots.


===============================================================================
 9. HIGH-GRAVITY WORLDS
===============================================================================

Some landable worlds have extremely high gravity.

These are interesting exploration targets.

They are also dangerous.


Before landing:

  CHECK GRAVITY.


A high-G landing can destroy an exploration ship carrying weeks of data.

Visit them only if you are comfortable with planetary flight.


===============================================================================
 10. GEOLOGICAL SITES - LEGACY
===============================================================================

Legacy can contain surface geology such as:

  Fumaroles

  Ice Fumaroles

  Gas Vents

  Geysers

  Ice Geysers

  Lava Spouts


After DSS mapping a suitable body, these appear as numbered navigation targets:

  Geological Site 1
  Geological Site 2
  etc.


You can:

  Select the site
  Land
  Deploy the SRV
  Explore it directly


Many geological formations also have material-bearing crystals nearby.


===============================================================================
 11. LEGACY BIOLOGICAL SITES
===============================================================================

Legacy contains pre-split Horizons biological discoveries.

Examples include:

  Amphora Plants

  Anemones

  Bark Mounds

  Brain Trees

  Crystalline Shards

  Sinuous Tubers


These are NOT the modern on-foot biological exploration loop.


Legacy method:

  1. DSS map the body.
  2. Select the numbered Biological Site.
  3. Land.
  4. Use ship / SRV exploration tools.


===============================================================================
 12. SHORT RANGE COMPOSITION SCANNER
===============================================================================

Every ship and SRV has an integrated:

  SHORT RANGE COMPOSITION SCANNER


It was added with Beyond 3.3.


Use it in:

  ANALYSIS MODE


It can scan:

  - Geological formations
  - Biological structures
  - Objects inside Notable Stellar Phenomena


Successful scans can add discoveries to the:

  CODEX


You do not need to buy this scanner.

It is built into the ship / SRV.


===============================================================================
 13. NOTABLE STELLAR PHENOMENA
===============================================================================

One of the most interesting Legacy discoveries is:

  NOTABLE STELLAR PHENOMENA


These were added in Beyond 3.3.


They appear as persistent signal sources.

You can find them with the:

  FSS


They may contain:

  Lagrange Clouds

  Strange mineral formations

  Spaceborne organic structures

  Mollusc-like life

  Pods

  Tree-like structures

  Anomalies


When one appears in the Navigation Panel:

  GO LOOK.


===============================================================================
 14. LAGRANGE CLOUDS
===============================================================================

Lagrange Clouds can appear at stable points in star systems.

They may be:

  Different colours

  Filled with crystals

  Filled with organic structures

  Electrically active


Some storm-cloud variants can be hazardous.

Approach unusual phenomena carefully.

PODS

TREE-LIKE ORGANISMS

If the FSS shows a Notable Stellar Phenomena:

  Investigate it.


===============================================================================
 16. CRYSTALLINE SHARDS
===============================================================================

Crystalline Shards are both:

  Interesting biological discoveries

and:

  Extremely useful raw-material farming sites


Classic Legacy examples exist around:

  HIP 36601
  Outotz LS-K d8-3


Legacy workflow:

  DSS map body
      ->
  Select numbered Biological Site
      ->
  Land
      ->
  SRV


These sites are particularly useful for stocking high-grade raw materials
before long expeditions.


===============================================================================
 17. BRAIN TREES
===============================================================================

Brain Trees are surface biological structures.

They are commonly associated with areas of space connected with Guardian
activity.

If DSS mapping reveals Biological Sites in those regions:

  They are worth investigating.


They can also contain material-bearing growths.


===============================================================================
 18. BARK MOUNDS
===============================================================================

Bark Mounds are fungal-like surface structures.

They have historically been found near nebulae and on suitable volcanic,
airless worlds.


They are a classic Horizons biological discovery.


===============================================================================
 19. SINUOUS TUBERS
===============================================================================

Sinuous Tubers are colourful tubular surface organisms.

They occur on suitable airless volcanic bodies and are particularly associated
with some areas toward the denser inner galaxy.


They are another classic Legacy Codex discovery.


===============================================================================
 20. ANEMONES
===============================================================================

Anemones are old Horizons biological structures found on suitable airless
worlds.

Some occur on:

  Hot planets
  High-gravity planets


Use:

  Ship or SRV Composition Scanner


Do not risk a dangerous landing just to scan one.


===============================================================================
 21. THE CODEX
===============================================================================

YES:

  THE CODEX STILL WORKS IN LEGACY.


It was added long before the Legacy split and remains part of the Legacy /
Console exploration system.


The Codex divides the galaxy into:

  42 REGIONS


It records categories such as:

  Stellar bodies

  Biological entities

  Geological entities

  Civilisation / xenological discoveries


===============================================================================
 SHARED CODEX INFORMATION VS YOUR PERSONAL CODEX
===============================================================================

The Codex is BOTH:

  SHARED
  and
  PERSONAL


The important states are:


RUMOURED
--------

The game suggests that a certain type of object may exist somewhere in that
galactic region.

Nobody has necessarily confirmed it yet.


REPORTED
--------

Another commander has found and scanned that type of discovery in the region.

This is SHARED regional Codex information.

If another LEGACY commander finds and reports something new for that region,
the shared Legacy Codex can be updated.


CONFIRMED / DISCOVERED BY YOU
-----------------------------

You personally found and scanned that type of object.

That becomes part of YOUR own Codex record.


Simple example:

  Another Legacy commander finds a rare biological object.

  Their discovery can make that object:

      REPORTED

  for the region.


  You later find and scan the same type yourself.

  Your personal Codex then records that YOU have confirmed / discovered it.


===============================================================================
 LEGACY SERVER SEPARATION
===============================================================================

Legacy and the current PC game are separate server environments.

Therefore:

  A new discovery made in the current PC game should NOT be assumed to update
  the Legacy Codex.

For this guide:

  Treat Legacy Codex reports as part of the Legacy / Console game state.


===============================================================================
 22. CODEX VS FIRST DISCOVERED / FIRST MAPPED
===============================================================================

These are DIFFERENT systems.


FIRST DISCOVERED BY
-------------------

Applies to astronomical bodies such as:

  Stars
  Planets
  Moons


To receive the tag:

  1. Discover the body.
  2. Survive.
  3. Sell the exploration data.


FIRST MAPPED BY
---------------

Applies to DSS mapping.

To receive the tag:

  1. DSS-map the body.
  2. Survive.
  3. Sell the exploration data.


CODEX
-----

The Codex does NOT simply list every planet or star discovered by every
commander.

Instead, it tracks:

  TYPES of discoveries
  and
  REGIONAL examples / records.


Examples:

  A certain star class in a region

  A particular biological structure

  A geological phenomenon

  A Notable Stellar Phenomena object


The shared Codex can tell you that another Legacy commander has REPORTED a
type of discovery in that region.

Your personal Codex separately records whether YOU have confirmed it.


===============================================================================
 SIMPLE WAY TO REMEMBER IT
===============================================================================

FIRST DISCOVERED / FIRST MAPPED:

  "Was I the first commander to discover or map THIS BODY?"


CODEX - REPORTED:

  "Has any Legacy commander reported THIS TYPE OF THING in this region?"


CODEX - PERSONAL CONFIRMATION:

  "Have I personally found and scanned THIS TYPE OF THING in this region?"


===============================================================================
 WHY THE CODEX IS USEFUL
===============================================================================

Use the Codex when you want an exploration goal other than:

  "Fly randomly."


Example goals:

  Find something only listed as RUMOURED.

  Personally confirm something already REPORTED.

  Fill out your own Codex entries for a galactic region.

  Search for biological, geological or stellar discoveries you have never
  personally scanned before.


===============================================================================
 23. SEARCHING FOR UNDISCOVERED SYSTEMS
===============================================================================

Avoid obvious highways.

Highly travelled:

  Sol -> Colonia

  Sol -> Sagittarius A*

  Colonia -> Sagittarius A*

  Famous nebula routes


Better method:

  1. Travel several thousand LY from inhabited space.

  2. Move sideways / above / below the obvious route.

  3. Watch system maps for missing commander tags.

  4. Once you find virgin systems:
       Slow down.


===============================================================================
 24. WHAT MAKES A SYSTEM MEMORABLE?
===============================================================================

Credits are only one reason to explore.

A memorable system may have:

  - Earth-like World
  - Black Hole
  - Multiple Neutron Stars
  - Close binary planets
  - Huge rings
  - High-G landable world
  - Biological Sites
  - Geological Sites
  - Notable Stellar Phenomena
  - Strange moon arrangements
  - Fantastic lighting
  - A view of a nebula


Take screenshots.

Bookmark unusual systems.


===============================================================================
 25. BEGINNER "GO LOOK AT THIS" LIST
===============================================================================

If you see:

  Earth-like World
      -> DSS it.

  Ammonia World
      -> DSS it.

  Water World
      -> Check terraformability and DSS it.

  Black Hole
      -> Scan and look around.

  Neutron Star
      -> Scan; supercharge only if prepared.

  Biological Sites
      -> Consider landing.

  Geological Sites
      -> Consider landing.

  Notable Stellar Phenomena
      -> Investigate.

  Huge / unusual rings
      -> Look closer.

  Very high gravity
      -> Interesting, but land carefully.

  Untagged system
      -> You may have found virgin space.
