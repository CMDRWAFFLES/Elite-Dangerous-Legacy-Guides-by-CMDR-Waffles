===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 GALAXY MAP & ROUTE PLANNING GUIDE
===============================================================================

This guide explains how to plan safe exploration routes in Legacy.


===============================================================================
 1. THE TWO NORMAL ROUTE MODES
===============================================================================

The Galaxy Map gives you two main route styles:

  FASTEST ROUTES
  ECONOMICAL ROUTES


+-------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Route Type        | Plotting Limit        | What It Does                      |
+-------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Fastest Routes    | Up to ~20,000 LY      | Uses longer jumps                 |
| Economical Routes | Up to ~1,000 LY       | Uses many short, fuel-cheap jumps |
+-------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------------------+


FASTEST ROUTES
--------------

Use this when:

  - Travelling to a destination
  - Going to Colonia
  - Going toward Sagittarius A*
  - Leaving the Bubble quickly
  - You have a Fuel Scoop

Advantages:

  - Far fewer jumps
  - Much faster travel

Disadvantages:

  - Long jumps consume much more fuel than short jumps


ECONOMICAL ROUTES
-----------------

Use this when:

  - You deliberately want to visit many systems
  - You are searching for undiscovered systems
  - You want maximum exploration density
  - You are exploring a small region in detail

Advantages:

  - Visits many more stars
  - Much lower fuel use per distance travelled

Disadvantages:

  - Extremely slow for long-distance travel


BEGINNER RULE:

  TRAVELLING somewhere:
      Use FASTEST.

  EXPLORING a local patch thoroughly:
      Use ECONOMICAL.


===============================================================================
 2. FUEL-STAR FILTER
===============================================================================

Fuel-scoopable main star classes are:

  O
  B
  A
  F
  G
  K
  M


Mnemonic:

  KGB FOAM


In the Galaxy Map:

  1. Open the star-class filter.
  2. Select:
       O B A F G K M
  3. Enable:
       APPLY FILTER TO ROUTE


This forces the route planner to favour scoopable main stars.


WHY THIS MATTERS
----------------

Without the filter, a route can pass through long runs of unscoopable stars.

That becomes particularly dangerous around regions containing many:

  - L dwarfs
  - T dwarfs
  - Y dwarfs


There is also a broad brown-dwarf-heavy layer around parts of the galactic
plane.

If your route suddenly becomes full of unscoopable stars:

  Move higher or lower on the galactic Z-axis and replot.


===============================================================================
 3. DO NOT FILTER TOO AGGRESSIVELY IN SPARSE SPACE
===============================================================================

The KGB FOAM filter is excellent where star density is high.

Near the galactic rim or other sparse regions:

  A strict scoopable-only route may FAIL even though a route exists.


If plotting fails:

  1. Remove the strict star-class filter.
  2. Plot through whatever stars are reachable.
  3. Manually ensure that you periodically reach a scoopable star.


Sparse-space exploration requires more manual planning.


===============================================================================
 4. FASTEST ROUTE + FUEL SCOOP = NORMAL EXPLORER SETUP
===============================================================================

For normal exploration travel:

  Route Mode:
      FASTEST

  Star Filter:
      O B A F G K M

  Apply Filter To Route:
      YES

  Fuel Scoop:
      FITTED


This is the simplest safe setup for beginners.


===============================================================================
 5. JET-CONE BOOST SETTING
===============================================================================

The Galaxy Map also has an option to use:

  JET-CONE BOOST


This allows the route planner to include jumps using:

  - Neutron Stars
  - White Dwarfs


For a beginner who is NOT deliberately using neutron travel:

  LEAVE IT OFF.


Turn it on only after you understand:

  - How to supercharge safely
  - FSD damage
  - AFMU repairs
  - Fuel management


For serious neutron-highway travel:

  Use the separate Legacy Jumpconda Neutron Highway guide.


===============================================================================
 6. FSD BOOST / INJECTION SETTING
===============================================================================

The Galaxy Map can also display boosted jump capability for:

  Basic FSD Injection:
      +25%

  Standard:
      +50%

  Premium:
      +100%


This setting does NOT magically create the boost.

You must have the correct synthesis materials and actually synthesize the
injection.

For normal travelling:

  Leave FSD Boost disabled.

Use it when manually planning a difficult jump in sparse space.


===============================================================================
 7. YOUR JUMP RANGE CHANGES
===============================================================================

Your actual jump range changes with ship mass.

Things that reduce range include:

  - Fuel
  - Cargo
  - Limpets
  - Heavy modules


Example:

A ship might display:

  Maximum / low-fuel range:
      82 LY

but with:

  Full fuel:
      79 LY

and with cargo:
      even less


IMPORTANT:

If you load cargo or limpets after plotting a route:

  REPLOT THE ROUTE.

Otherwise the Galaxy Map may have planned a jump that the heavier ship can no
longer make.


===============================================================================
 8. HOW TO FIND UNDISCOVERED SPACE
===============================================================================

The obvious routes are heavily travelled.

Examples:

  Sol -> Colonia
  Sol -> Sagittarius A*
  Colonia -> Sagittarius A*
  Routes directly toward famous nebulae
  Routes directly toward famous tourist systems


If your goal is FIRST DISCOVERIES:

  Do not simply follow the most obvious straight line.


A good method:

  1. Leave the Bubble using Fastest Routes.

  2. Travel a few thousand light-years away from populated space.

  3. Move away from the obvious travel corridor.

  4. Add a vertical or sideways detour.

  5. Once you start finding untagged systems, slow down and explore.


A useful detour might be:

  Several hundred to roughly 1,500 LY above or below the route corridor.

This is NOT a magic number.

The purpose is simply to leave the line that thousands of other commanders
have already followed.


===============================================================================
 9. GALACTIC Z-AXIS
===============================================================================

Think of the galaxy as a disc.

You can travel:

  Toward / away from the core
  Left / right across the disc
  ABOVE the galactic plane
  BELOW the galactic plane


Beginners often think only in two dimensions.

Moving vertically is one of the easiest ways to escape heavily travelled
routes.


BUT:

As you move very far above or below the disc:

  Star density drops.

Eventually:

  Route plotting becomes difficult or impossible.


===============================================================================
 10. WHEN THE ROUTE PLOTTER FAILS
===============================================================================

Possible reasons:

  1. Your destination is outside the plotting limit.

  2. Your ship does not have enough jump range.

  3. Star density is too low.

  4. Your star filter removed the only usable stars.

  5. A permit-locked region blocks the direct route.

  6. Your current cargo/fuel mass is greater than expected.

  7. The route expects an FSD boost you have not actually applied.


TRY THIS:

  [ ] Check Fastest vs Economical.

  [ ] Remove star filters temporarily.

  [ ] Check current jump range.

  [ ] Replot after changing cargo.

  [ ] Move sideways / above / below the blockage.

  [ ] Plot to an intermediate system.

  [ ] In sparse space, select individual stars manually.


===============================================================================
 11. ROUTING AROUND PERMIT-LOCKED REGIONS
===============================================================================

Some large areas contain systems that cannot be entered without permits.

This can make the route planner appear broken.

If a route repeatedly fails in a region:

  - Move several hundred LY sideways
  - Move above or below the blocked region
  - Plot an intermediate destination
  - Replot from there


Do not assume that a failed route means your ship lacks jump range.


===============================================================================
 12. SPANSH
===============================================================================

Spansh is extremely useful for long-distance exploration planning.

Useful functions include:

  - Neutron routing
  - Long-distance galaxy plotting


PHYSICAL DATA RULE
------------------

Star positions are physical Stellar Forge galaxy data.

Therefore neutron-star positions and physical route geometry are useful for
Legacy.


DO NOT TREAT SPANSH AS LEGACY MARKET TRUTH FOR:

  - Current Fleet Carrier locations
  - Current carrier services
  - Current commodity markets
  - Supply / demand

 13. SIMPLE ROUTE SETUPS
===============================================================================

NORMAL LONG-DISTANCE EXPLORATION
--------------------------------

  Fastest:
      ON

  OBAFGKM filter:
      ON

  Apply filter to route:
      ON

  Jet-Cone Boost:
      OFF unless deliberately using it

  FSD Injection:
      OFF unless deliberately using it


HUNTING FIRST DISCOVERIES
-------------------------

  First:
      Fastest route away from populated space

  Then:
      Move off the obvious corridor

  Finally:
      Economical routing through the chosen search area


NEUTRON HIGHWAY
---------------

  Use:
      Spansh

  Ship:
      Follow the separate Jumpconda guide


GALACTIC RIM
------------

  Expect:
      Manual jumps
      Sparse stars
      FSD injections
      Careful return planning


===============================================================================
 14. BEGINNER ROUTE CHECKLIST
===============================================================================

Before plotting:

  [ ] Fuel Scoop fitted

  [ ] Know your FULL-FUEL jump range

  [ ] Fastest Route selected for travel

  [ ] O B A F G K M selected

  [ ] Apply Filter To Route enabled

  [ ] Jet-Cone Boost off unless intended

  [ ] FSD Boost off unless intended


During travel:

  [ ] Watch fuel gauge

  [ ] Scoop regularly

  [ ] Replot if cargo mass changes

  [ ] Do not follow unscoopable stars until nearly empty

  [ ] If hunting discoveries, leave the obvious corridor
