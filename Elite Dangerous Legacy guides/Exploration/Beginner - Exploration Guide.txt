===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 BEGINNER EXPLORATION GUIDE
===============================================================================

This guide is for:

  Elite Dangerous: Legacy / Console
  Horizons 3.8

It is written for beginners and focuses on:

  - What exploration is
  - What modules you actually need
  - What modules are optional
  - What each module does
  - How to scan a system
  - How to map planets
  - What to sell
  - How to avoid losing your data


===============================================================================
 1. WHAT IS EXPLORATION?
===============================================================================

Exploration is the process of travelling through star systems, scanning stars
and planets, mapping valuable worlds, and selling the data to Universal
Cartographics.

The basic loop is:

  1. Jump into a system
  2. Honk the system 
  3. Scan bodies with the FSS
  4. DSS-map valuable planets
  5. Move to the next system
  6. Return safely
  7. Sell the data


===============================================================================
 2. WHAT DO YOU ACTUALLY NEED?
===============================================================================

STRICTLY NECESSARY
------------------

You can technically explore with almost any ship.

You do NOT need a special exploration ship.

The game already gives you access to the basic scanning tools needed to find
and identify bodies.


PRACTICALLY NECESSARY FOR REAL EXPLORATION
-------------------------------------------

FUEL SCOOP

  Purpose:
    Refuels your ship directly from suitable stars.

  Why it matters:
    Without a Fuel Scoop, long-distance exploration becomes extremely limited.

  Beginner verdict:
    ESSENTIAL for serious exploration.


DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER - DSS

  Purpose:
    Maps planets and moons using probes.

  Why it matters:
    Mapping valuable planets can pay much more than only scanning them.

  Beginner verdict:
    ESSENTIAL if you want good exploration payouts.


===============================================================================
 3. BUILT-IN SCANNING TOOLS
===============================================================================

DISCOVERY SCANNER - "HONK"
--------------------------

Purpose:
  Detects the system's astronomical signals.

What you do:
  Enter Analysis Mode and charge the Discovery Scanner.

Use it:
  Every time you enter a new system.

Beginner rule:

  HONK EVERY SYSTEM.


FULL SPECTRUM SYSTEM SCANNER - FSS
----------------------------------

Purpose:
  Identifies planets and moons from a distance.

What it does:
  Lets you scan bodies without flying to each one.

How to use it:
  1. Stay in Supercruise.
  2. Throttle down.
  3. Open the FSS.
  4. Tune the frequency bar.
  5. Find a signal.
  6. Zoom in until the body is resolved.

Beginner rule:

  FSS is how you identify what is actually in the system.

===============================================================================
 5. FUEL SCOOP
===============================================================================

The Fuel Scoop is the most important exploration module after your Frame Shift
Drive.

You can scoop fuel from these star classes:

  O
  B
  A
  F
  G
  K
  M


A common way to remember them is:

  KGB FOAM


You cannot normally scoop fuel from:

  - Brown Dwarfs
  - T Tauri stars
  - Neutron Stars
  - White Dwarfs
  - Black Holes


BEGINNER TIP
------------

Do not wait until your fuel tank is nearly empty before looking for a
scoopable star.

Refuel regularly.


===============================================================================
 6. DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER - DSS
===============================================================================

The DSS is used to map planets and moons.

The difference is:

  HONK
    Finds the system signals.

  FSS
    Identifies the body.

  DSS
    Maps the body.


To use the DSS:

  1. Select the planet.
  2. Fly close enough in Supercruise.
  3. Enter Analysis Mode.
  4. Activate the DSS.
  5. Fire probes at the planet.
  6. Cover enough of the surface to complete the map.


DSS EFFICIENCY
--------------

The DSS gives you a probe efficiency target.

Example:

  Efficiency Target: 6 Probes

If you complete the map using 6 probes or fewer:

  You receive the efficiency bonus.

If you use more:

  You still complete the map,
  but you lose the efficiency bonus.


===============================================================================
 7. SHIELD GENERATOR
===============================================================================

A Shield Generator is optional for exploration.

Reasons to bring one:

  - Protects you from rough planetary landings
  - Protects against minor collisions
  - Gives beginners more room for mistakes


Reasons to leave it behind:

  - Takes mass
  - Uses power
  - Slightly reduces jump range


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  A small shield is a good idea if you plan to land on planets.


===============================================================================
 8. AUTO FIELD-MAINTENANCE UNIT - AFMU
===============================================================================

AFMU means:

  Auto Field-Maintenance Unit


Purpose:

  Repairs damaged internal modules.


Useful for:

  - Very long expeditions
  - Neutron Star travel
  - Accidental heat damage
  - Long trips far from stations


Important:

  An AFMU cannot repair your ship's hull.

  It repairs modules.


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  Optional for short trips.
  Recommended for long-distance exploration.


===============================================================================
 9. HEAT SINK LAUNCHER
===============================================================================

Purpose:

  Dumps heat quickly.


Useful when:

  - You get too close to a star
  - You accidentally drop near a hot object
  - Your ship begins overheating
  - You need to escape a dangerous heat situation


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  Optional, but useful emergency equipment.


===============================================================================
 10. PLANETARY VEHICLE HANGAR AND SRV
===============================================================================

Purpose:

  Lets you land and drive on suitable planetary surfaces.


You need it if you want to:

  - Explore surface locations
  - Visit Guardian sites
  - Collect surface materials
  - Drive around planets
  - Investigate surface points of interest


You do NOT need an SRV just to:

  - Honk systems
  - Use the FSS
  - DSS-map planets
  - Earn normal exploration credits


LEGACY SURFACE NOTE:

  Legacy 3.8 uses the Horizons surface signal / navigation-target system.

  For surface sites and material farming, follow instructions specifically
  written for Legacy / Horizons 3.8.


===============================================================================
 11. REPAIR LIMPET CONTROLLER
===============================================================================

Purpose:

  Repairs hull damage.


Useful for:

  - Extremely long expeditions
  - Players who land often
  - Recovering from accidental hull damage


You also need:

  - Cargo space for limpets


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  Not necessary for normal beginner exploration.


===============================================================================
 12. SUPERCRUISE ASSIST
===============================================================================

Purpose:

  Helps control long Supercruise approaches.


Advantages:

  - Convenient for long flights
  - Useful when travelling to distant planets


Disadvantages:

  - Takes a module slot
  - Not needed for exploration itself


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  Purely optional convenience.


===============================================================================
 13. DOCKING COMPUTER
===============================================================================

Purpose:

  Helps automate docking.


Advantages:

  - Easier for beginners
  - Reduces stress when returning with valuable data


Disadvantages:

  - Uses a module slot


BEGINNER VERDICT:

  Optional.

  Bring one if it makes docking safer for you.


===============================================================================
 14. A SIMPLE BEGINNER EXPLORATION LOADOUT
===============================================================================

A very basic exploration ship should have:

  MUST HAVE:

    Fuel Scoop
    Detailed Surface Scanner


  GOOD TO HAVE:

    Small Shield Generator


  OPTIONAL:

    AFMU
    Heat Sink Launcher
    Planetary Vehicle Hangar
    SRV
    Supercruise Assist
    Docking Computer


You do NOT need to fill every module slot.

Lighter ships generally jump farther.


===============================================================================
 15. WHAT SHOULD YOU SCAN?
===============================================================================

When you enter a system:

  1. Honk
  2. Open FSS
  3. Identify the bodies


Good bodies to look for:

  Earth-like Worlds
  Water Worlds
  Ammonia Worlds
  High Metal Content Worlds
  Metal-Rich Bodies


Also check whether a body is:

  A CANDIDATE FOR TERRAFORMING


Terraformability can greatly increase its value.


===============================================================================
 16. WHAT SHOULD YOU DSS-MAP?
===============================================================================

For good exploration credits, prioritize:

  Earth-like Worlds
  Terraformable Water Worlds
  Terraformable High Metal Content Worlds
  Ammonia Worlds
  Terraformable Rocky Bodies
  Water Worlds


Also worth considering:

  Metal-Rich Bodies
  Class II Gas Giants
  High Metal Content Worlds


Usually not worth a long detour purely for credits:

  Icy Bodies
  Rocky-Ice Bodies
  Ordinary Rocky Bodies
  Most low-value Gas Giants


===============================================================================
 17. FIRST DISCOVERY AND FIRST MAPPED
===============================================================================

If nobody has previously discovered and sold the body:

  You may receive:

    FIRST DISCOVERED BY


If nobody has previously DSS-mapped and sold the body:

  You may receive:

    FIRST MAPPED BY


You must survive and sell the data before the tag is secured.


===============================================================================
 18. YOUR DATA CAN BE LOST
===============================================================================

Unsold exploration data is carried on your ship.

If your ship is destroyed before you sell it:

  THE UNSOLD EXPLORATION DATA IS LOST.


This can include:

  - Exploration credits
  - First Discovery claims
  - First Mapping claims
  - Hours or days of work


BEGINNER RULE:

  The data is not safe until you sell it.


===============================================================================
 19. SELLING EXPLORATION DATA
===============================================================================

Exploration data is sold through:

  UNIVERSAL CARTOGRAPHICS


Normal station:

  100% payout


Fleet Carrier Universal Cartographics:

  75% payout


Fleet Carrier penalty:

  -25%


Example:

  Normal value:          100,000,000 Cr
  Station payout:        100,000,000 Cr
  Fleet Carrier payout:  75,000,000 Cr
  Lost to carrier fee:   25,000,000 Cr


Fleet Carriers are useful for:

  - Safety
  - Convenience
  - Selling data far from civilisation


For maximum credits:

  Sell at a normal station.


LEGACY WARNING:

  Fleet Carrier positions and market information are server-dependent.

  When using a third-party tool, use its Legacy / Console filter if available.


===============================================================================
 20. SIMPLE BEGINNER WORKFLOW
===============================================================================

Every system:

  1. Jump in

  2. Honk

  3. Refuel if needed

  4. Throttle down

  5. Open FSS

  6. Scan interesting bodies

  7. Check the System Map

  8. Look for terraformable planets

  9. Fly to valuable planets

 10. DSS-map them

 11. Try to hit the DSS efficiency target

 12. Jump to the next system


When finished:

 13. Return safely

 14. Dock carefully

 15. Sell at Universal Cartographics
