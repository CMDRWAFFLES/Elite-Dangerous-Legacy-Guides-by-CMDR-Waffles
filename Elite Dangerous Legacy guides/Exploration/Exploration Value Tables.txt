===============================================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
===============================================================================
NORMAL UNIVERSAL CARTOGRAPHICS = 100% PAYOUT FLEET CARRIER CARTOGRAPHICS = 75% PAYOUT FLEET CARRIER PENALTY = -25%
Selling exploration data to Universal Cartographics on a Fleet Carrier has one major disadvantage:
You lose 25% of the normal exploration payout.
A Fleet Carrier's Universal Cartographics service deducts a fixed 25% service fee from the value of the data.

Example:
Normal station payout:     1,000,000 Cr
Fleet Carrier payout:        750,000 Cr
Credits lost:                250,000 Cr

Or

Example:
Exploration data worth:   500,000,000 Cr
Sold at normal station:    500,000,000 Cr
Sold on Fleet Carrier:     375,000,000 Cr
Credits lost:              125,000,000 Cr

All values are approximate Legacy 3.8 payouts.
k = thousand credits
m = million credits
