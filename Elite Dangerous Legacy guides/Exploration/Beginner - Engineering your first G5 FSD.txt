===============================================================================
 3. VERY IMPORTANT: FSS VS DSS VS COORDINATES
===============================================================================

FSS
---

Full Spectrum System Scanner.

The FSS identifies:

  Stars
  Planets
  Moons
  System signals


It does NOT normally give you the exact location of a fixed surface crash site
or settlement.


DSS
---

Detailed Surface Scanner.

In Legacy, DSS mapping can reveal fixed surface POIs such as:

  Dav's Hope

  Jameson Crash Site


When the DSS reveals the site:

  It appears in the Navigation Panel

and you can target it directly.


COORDINATES
-----------

Some older fixed sites are easiest to find the FIRST time by using:

  Latitude
  Longitude


Once certain sites have been scanned:

  They can become permanent Navigation Panel targets for that commander.

===============================================================================
 6. STEP ONE - REACH SCOUT EXPLORATION RANK
===============================================================================

Felicity Farseer's meeting requirement is:

  EXPLORATION RANK:
      SCOUT


Scout is reached at roughly:

  270,000 Cr

of exploration profit.


This is a VERY small amount.


EASIEST BEGINNER METHOD
-----------------------

Do not make a special grind out of this.

While travelling around:

  1. Honk every system.

  2. Use the FSS.

  3. Scan planets and moons.

  4. DSS-map valuable bodies if convenient.

  5. Sell enough data at Universal Cartographics to reach Scout.


Check your Exploration rank in:

  RIGHT PANEL
      ->
  STATUS


Once it says:

  SCOUT

or higher:

  Felicity's meeting requirement is complete.


===============================================================================
 7. IMPORTANT - SAVE SOME EXPLORATION DATA AFTER REACHING SCOUT
===============================================================================

Once you have reached Scout:

  DO NOT automatically sell every new piece of exploration data.


Keep collecting data while travelling to:

  Dav's Hope
  Jameson
  Orrere
  Maia


WHY?
----

After Felicity is unlocked:

  You can sell exploration data at Farseer Inc.


That increases:

  FELICITY FARSEER REPUTATION


Higher reputation means:

  Fewer low-grade engineering rolls
  Faster access to higher grades


If you already have a large amount of exploration data:

  Selling roughly 16 million Cr of exploration data / profit at an Engineer
  base is enough to reach the highest Engineer reputation tier.


You do NOT need to grind 16 million just for this guide.

Any saved exploration data helps.

Engineering rolls themselves also increase her reputation.

===============================================================================
 17. HOW TO FARM DAV'S HOPE
===============================================================================

1. Land near Dav's Hope.

2. Deploy the SRV.

3. Open the SRV Cargo Scoop.

4. Drive around the settlement.

5. Target and collect the loose manufactured materials.

6. Complete the loop.

7. Log out to:

     MAIN MENU

8. Log back in.

9. Materials respawn.

10. Repeat.


For a first FSD:

  Several loops gives you a useful buffer.


Dav's Hope is not necessarily the absolute fastest manufactured-material farm
for an experienced commander.

It IS one of the easiest fixed, predictable methods for a beginner.

===============================================================================
 34. HOW TO ENGINEER THE FSD
===============================================================================

At Farseer Inc:

  1. Open Engineer Workshop.

  2. Select your Frame Shift Drive.

  3. Select:

       INCREASED RANGE

  4. Apply Grade 1 rolls.


As soon as Grade 2 unlocks:

  MOVE TO GRADE 2.


Do NOT waste materials completely filling Grade 1 if Grade 2 is already
available.


Continue:

  G1
    ->
  G2
    ->
  G3
    ->
  G4
    ->
  G5


At Grade 5:

  Continue until the Grade 5 improvement is completed to the level you want.


For an exploration ship:

  Normally complete G5 for maximum range.

===============================================================================
 37. DEEP CHARGE
===============================================================================

MATERIAL COST:

  5 Atypical Disrupted Wake Echoes

  3 Galvanising Alloys

  1 Eccentric Hyperspace Trajectory


Yes:

  MASS MANAGER
  and
  DEEP CHARGE

use the SAME materials.


EFFECT:

  +10% Maximum Fuel Per Jump


IMPORTANT:

That does NOT mean:

  +10% jump range.


It allows the FSD to burn more fuel on a maximum-distance jump.


On smaller FSD classes this can give a slightly longer maximum single jump.


===============================================================================
 38. MASS MANAGER VS DEEP CHARGE - SIMPLE RULE
===============================================================================

+-------------+--------------------------+------------------------------------+
| FSD Class   | Maximum-range choice     | Beginner exploration advice        |
+-------------+--------------------------+------------------------------------+
| Class 2     | Deep Charge              | Deep Charge if chasing max range   |
| Class 3     | Deep Charge              | Deep Charge if chasing max range   |
| Class 4     | Deep Charge by a tiny    | Either; Mass Manager saves fuel    |
|             | amount                   |                                    |
| Class 5+    | Mass Manager             | Mass Manager                       |
+-------------+--------------------------+------------------------------------+


CLASS 4 NOTE
------------

Deep Charge is only very slightly better for maximum single-jump range.

The difference is tiny.

Mass Manager avoids the extra maximum fuel-per-jump consumption.

For a long-distance explorer:

  Mass Manager is perfectly sensible on a Class 4.
