 1. WHAT IS THE NEUTRON HIGHWAY?
===============================================================================

A Neutron Star has large jet cones coming from its poles.

If your ship has a FUEL SCOOP fitted, you can fly through one of those jet
cones in Supercruise and temporarily SUPERCHARGE your Frame Shift Drive.

A Neutron Star supercharge gives:

  NORMAL JUMP RANGE x 4

Example:

  Normal Anaconda jump range:       70 LY
  Neutron-supercharged jump range: 280 LY


The supercharge lasts for ONE hyperspace jump.

After that jump, you must supercharge again at another Neutron Star.

The "Neutron Highway" is simply a route that chains large numbers of Neutron
Stars together:

  Neutron Star
       |
       | Supercharge
       v
  ~280 LY jump
       |
       v
  Next Neutron Star
       |
       | Supercharge
       v
  ~280 LY jump
       |
       v
  Next Neutron Star...


Instead of making hundreds of ordinary jumps, you use a much smaller number
of very long boosted jumps.

 3. HOW MUCH FASTER IS IT?
===============================================================================

IMPORTANT:

The figures below are examples, not guaranteed route counts.

"Normal Minimum" is simply:

  straight-line distance / 70 LY

"All-Neutron Minimum" is the impossible perfect case where every jump could be
a full 280 LY neutron jump.

"Typical Spansh Estimate" is a realistic beginner planning range for a
70-ish LY Anaconda. Your actual route changes with:

  - Exact jump range
  - Route efficiency setting
  - Neutron Star density
  - Refuelling stops
  - Detours
  - Your current position


+---------------------------+-----------+----------------+------------------+----------------------+
| Journey                   | Distance  | Normal Minimum | Neutron Minimum  | Typical Spansh Route |
+---------------------------+-----------+----------------+------------------+----------------------+
| Sol -> Colonia            | 22,000 LY |  315 jumps     |   79 jumps       | ~100-140             |
| Sol -> Sagittarius A*     | 25,900 LY |  370 jumps     |   93 jumps       | ~120-165             |
| Colonia -> Sagittarius A* | 11,367 LY |  163 jumps     |   41 jumps       | ~55-80               |
| Sol -> Beagle Point       | 65,279 LY |  933 jumps     |  234 jumps       | ~280-380             |
+---------------------------+-----------+----------------+------------------+----------------------+


WHAT THAT MEANS
---------------

SOL -> COLONIA

  Ordinary 70 LY travel:
    At least ~315 jumps before routing inefficiency.

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly ~100-140 jumps is a sensible planning expectation.

  Jump reduction:
    Often around 60-70% fewer jumps.


SOL -> SAGITTARIUS A*

  Ordinary 70 LY travel:
    At least ~370 jumps before routing inefficiency.

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly ~120-165 jumps is a sensible planning expectation.

  Jump reduction:
    Often around 55-70% fewer jumps.


COLONIA -> SAGITTARIUS A*

  Ordinary 70 LY travel:
    At least ~163 jumps.

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly ~55-80 jumps.


SOL -> BEAGLE POINT

  Ordinary 70 LY travel:
    At least ~933 jumps.

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly ~280-380 jumps is a useful planning range.


These numbers are deliberately conservative.

Experienced speed-runners can do much better.


===============================================================================
 4. HOW MUCH REAL TIME CAN IT SAVE?
===============================================================================

Neutron travel does NOT make the trip exactly four times faster.

Why?

A normal jump is simple:

  Jump
  -> Scoop
  -> Jump


A neutron jump requires:

  Enter system
  -> Fly into jet
  -> Wait for supercharge
  -> Leave jet
  -> Select next system
  -> Jump
  -> Occasionally refuel
  -> Occasionally repair FSD


Even so, the reduction in total jumps is enormous.


ROUGH BEGINNER EXPECTATIONS
---------------------------

These are travel estimates, NOT promises.

They assume:

  - ~70 LY Anaconda
  - Spansh route
  - Mostly continuous travel
  - Little exploration stopping
  - Safe neutron technique


Sol -> Colonia:

  Ordinary fast travel:
    Roughly 5-7+ hours

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly 2.5-4 hours


Sol -> Sagittarius A*:

  Ordinary fast travel:
    Roughly 6-8+ hours

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly 3-5 hours


Colonia -> Sagittarius A*:

  Neutron Highway:
    Roughly 1.5-3 hours


Do NOT treat these as speed-run times.

A beginner learning neutron scooping should go slower.


HISTORICAL PERSPECTIVE
----------------------

A pre-split 2019 Anaconda speed run completed:

  Sol -> Colonia

in:

  1 hour 38 minutes 11 seconds

using:

  76 total jumps

That was a highly optimised record attempt.

The pilot stated that the 76-jump route was around 27% shorter than the best
Spansh route they had compared against.

For a beginner:

  Do not try to match speed-run times.

Use them only to understand how powerful neutron travel can be.

===============================================================================

Recommended website:

  https://www.spansh.co.uk/

  NEUTRON ROUTER


Spansh is especially useful because the in-game route planner tries to keep

Spansh can deliberately make small detours in order to hit more Neutron Stars.

More neutron jumps usually means:

  FAR FEWER TOTAL JUMPS


LEGACY VALIDITY
---------------

Neutron Star positions are physical galaxy data.

Legacy and the other galaxy branch share the same Stellar Forge galaxy
generation.

Therefore:

  SPANSH NEUTRON STAR POSITIONS ARE USEFUL FOR LEGACY.


Do NOT use Spansh's current server-dependent information as Legacy truth for:

  - Fleet Carrier positions
  - Markets
  - Commodity prices
  - Supply / demand

If a tool offers a Legacy / Console filter for server-dependent information,
use it.


===============================================================================
 6. HOW TO SET UP SPANSH
===============================================================================

Open:

  https://www.spansh.co.uk/


Select:

  NEUTRON ROUTER


Enter:

  SOURCE SYSTEM
    Example:
      Sol

  DESTINATION SYSTEM
    Example:
      Colonia

  RANGE
    Enter your REALISTIC normal jump range.

    For safety, use the range your Anaconda has with fuel onboard.

    Do NOT enter an empty-tank fantasy maximum if your ship cannot normally
    achieve it.


EFFICIENCY
----------

A good starting value is:

  60%


Lower efficiency:

  - Allows larger detours
  - Searches for more Neutron Stars
  - Can reduce total jump count


Higher efficiency:

  - Keeps the route straighter
  - May use fewer Neutron Stars


Beginner recommendation:

  Start at 60%.

If the route looks strange:

  Try 65%
  Try 70%


===============================================================================
 7. HOW TO FOLLOW A SPANSH ROUTE
===============================================================================

Spansh gives you a list of systems.

Your routine is:

  1. Copy the NEXT SYSTEM NAME.

  2. Open the Galaxy Map.

  3. Search for that system.

  4. Select / target it.

  5. Supercharge at your current Neutron Star.

  6. Make the boosted jump.

  7. Repeat.


IMPORTANT:

The simple Neutron Router does NOT manage your fuel for you.

You must watch your own fuel gauge.

===============================================================================

This is a SAFE long-distance concept.

It is not a paper-thin maximum-jump-range record build.


CORE INTERNALS
--------------

FRAME SHIFT DRIVE

  6A Frame Shift Drive

  Engineering:
    Increased Range Grade 5

  Experimental:
    Mass Manager

  THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ENGINEERING ON THE SHIP.


POWER PLANT

  Use a smaller A-rated Power Plant that can safely power your build.

  Do not reduce it so far that you constantly have power problems.

  Recommended engineering if power allows:

    Low Emissions
    Thermal Spread

  Reliability matters more than gaining a tiny amount of extra jump range.


THRUSTERS

  D-rate where practical to reduce mass.

  Keep enough performance that the ship is comfortable and safe to fly.


LIFE SUPPORT

  D-rated

  Engineering:
    Lightweight


POWER DISTRIBUTOR

  Use the lightest distributor that still gives you the flight performance
  you want.

  Being able to BOOST is recommended.


SENSORS

  D-rated

  Engineering:
    Lightweight


FUEL TANK

  Keep the normal 32-tonne tank.

  Do NOT aggressively reduce your fuel capacity for a neutron trip.

 10. OPTIONAL INTERNAL MODULES
===============================================================================

FUEL SCOOP
----------

BEST:
  7A Fuel Scoop

Price:
  ~91.18m Cr


CHEAPER:
  7B Fuel Scoop

Price:
  ~22.80m Cr


For a journey like Sol -> Colonia:

  A 7A scoop is wonderful.

But:

  A 7B works perfectly well if you do not want to spend ~91 million credits.


FUEL SCOOPS HAVE ZERO MASS.

Using a 7A instead of a 7B does NOT reduce jump range.


AFMU
----

AFMU means:

  Auto Field-Maintenance Unit


Take at least:

  1 AFMU


Recommended:

  2 AFMUs


AFMUs have zero mass.

So if you have the internal slots:

  TAKE TWO.


Why two?

  An AFMU cannot repair itself.

But:

  AFMU #1 can repair AFMU #2
  AFMU #2 can repair AFMU #1


GUARDIAN FSD BOOSTER
--------------------

Use:

  5H Guardian Frame Shift Drive Booster

Jump-range increase:

  +10.5 LY

Legacy-era module price:

  ~6.32m Cr


SHIELD
------

Use a lightweight shield.

You do not need a giant combat shield.

The purpose is simply to survive:

  - Bad landings
  - Bumps
  - Accidental impacts


DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER
------------------------

Recommended.

You are travelling through thousands of light-years of exploration space.

If you see:

  Earth-like World
  Water World
  Ammonia World
  Terraformable planet

you may as well map it.


SRV
---

Optional.

Useful if you want to:

  - Land on planets
  - Gather raw materials
  - Visit surface sites

For a pure high-speed Sol -> Colonia run:

  Not necessary.

===============================================================================
 13. HOW TO SUPERCHARGE SAFELY
===============================================================================

NEUTRON STAR ONLY FOR BEGINNERS.


DO THIS
-------

1. Arrive at the Neutron Star.

2. Identify one of the two jet cones.

3. Stay WELL AWAY from the star itself.

4. Fly toward the OUTER END of the jet.

5. Reduce throttle.

6. Enter the cone while pointing generally AWAY from the Neutron Star.

7. The turbulence will push the ship around.

8. Stay inside until you hear / see:

     FRAME SHIFT DRIVE SUPERCHARGED

9. Fly out of the jet.

10. Select the next Spansh system.

11. Jump.


===============================================================================
 14. DO NOT USE WHITE DWARFS FOR THIS
===============================================================================

White Dwarfs can also supercharge the FSD.

But they only give:

  NORMAL RANGE x 1.5


Neutron Star:

  NORMAL RANGE x 4


White Dwarfs also have a much more dangerous relationship between their
exclusion zone and jet cone.


BEGINNER RULE:

  USE NEUTRON STARS.

  SKIP WHITE DWARFS.


===============================================================================
 15. FSD DAMAGE
===============================================================================

Every Neutron Star supercharge damages your Frame Shift Drive slightly.

This damage builds up over repeated boosts.


IMPORTANT NUMBER:

  BELOW 80% FSD HEALTH

the Frame Shift Drive can begin malfunctioning.


Recommended:

  Repair around 85-90%.

Do not wait until the drive starts failing.


===============================================================================
 16. HOW TO REPAIR THE FSD
===============================================================================

Do NOT try to repair while inside a neutron jet.


Safe procedure:

  1. Leave the Neutron Star.

  2. Get well clear.

  3. Drop into normal space somewhere safe.

  4. Open:
       Right Panel -> Modules

  5. Select:
       Frame Shift Drive

  6. Choose:
       Repair

  7. The FSD switches off.

  8. Let the AFMU repair it.

  9. Re-enable the FSD.

 10. Continue your journey.


===============================================================================
 17. FUEL - THE BIG BEGINNER TRAP
===============================================================================

THIS IS IMPORTANT:

  NEUTRON STARS DO NOT REFUEL YOUR SHIP.


The Fuel Scoop is required to supercharge.

But you still need ordinary scoopable stars for FUEL.


Fuel stars:

  O
  B
  A
  F
  G
  K
  M


Remember:

  KGB FOAM


A common mistake is:

  Neutron
  -> Neutron
  -> Neutron
  -> Neutron
  -> OUT OF FUEL


Watch your fuel gauge.


When fuel gets low:

  1. Leave the Spansh neutron chain.

  2. Jump to a nearby scoopable star.

  3. Refill.

  4. Return to the next neutron system on your Spansh route.


===============================================================================
 18. WHY THE 7A FUEL SCOOP IS SO GOOD
===============================================================================

On a normal exploration ship, scoop speed is convenient.

On a Neutron Highway ship, scoop speed directly affects travel time.

You repeatedly:

  - Leave the neutron chain
  - Refuel
  - Return to the route


A very fast scoop means less time sitting beside stars.


If you can afford it:

  7A FUEL SCOOP


If you cannot:

  7B FUEL SCOOP

is still completely usable.


===============================================================================
 19. THE NEUTRON HIGHWAY IS NOT THE BEST WAY TO EXPLORE
===============================================================================

The Neutron Highway is designed for:

  GETTING SOMEWHERE QUICKLY.


It is NOT ideal if your goal is:

  - Discovering lots of systems
  - FSS scanning everything
  - Mapping lots of planets
  - Taking scenic detours


Why?

You skip enormous sections of space with each jump.


Example:

  Normal jump:
    70 LY

  Neutron jump:
    280 LY


If your goal is:

  "I want to reach Colonia"

use the Neutron Highway.


If your goal is:

  "I want to explore everything on the way"

use a normal route and take your time.

 21. PRACTICE FIRST
===============================================================================

Do NOT make your first-ever neutron scoop halfway to Colonia.

Practice near civilisation first.

A well-known Neutron Star relatively close to the Bubble is:

  JACKSON'S LIGHTHOUSE


Practice:

  Entering the outer jet

  Getting supercharged

  Leaving safely

  Making the boosted jump

  Checking FSD health


Once you can do that comfortably:

  Start your long route.

 22. SIMPLE SOL -> COLONIA ROUTINE
===============================================================================

1. Build ~70 LY Anaconda.

2. Fit:
     6A G5 Range FSD + Mass Manager
     Fuel Scoop
     AFMU
     Guardian FSD Booster

3. Open:
     https://www.spansh.co.uk/

4. Select:
     Neutron Router

5. Enter:

     Source:
       Sol

     Destination:
       Colonia

     Range:
       ~70 LY

     Efficiency:
       60%

6. Generate route.

7. Copy first system.

8. Travel to it.

9. Neutron supercharge.

10. Copy next system.

11. Jump.

12. Repeat.

13. Refuel whenever necessary.

14. Check FSD health regularly.

15. Repair around 85-90%.

16. Continue until Colonia.
