===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY / CONSOLE - HORIZONS 3.8
 BEGINNER GUIDE:
 HOW TO GET THE PRE-ENGINEERED 5A FSD V1
===============================================================================

This guide is specifically for:

  Elite Dangerous: Legacy / Console
  Horizons 3.8


MODULE:

  ENGINEERED FSD V1


Sometimes players call it:

  PRE-ENGINEERED FSD
  DOUBLE-ENGINEERED FSD
  5A FSD V1


It is:

  CLASS 5
  RATING A


===============================================================================
 1. WHY SHOULD YOU GET IT?
===============================================================================

For a ship that uses a Class 5 Frame Shift Drive:

  THIS IS THE BEST LEGACY CLASS 5 FSD FOR JUMP RANGE.


It comes with TWO engineering blueprints already applied:

  INCREASED RANGE

and:

  FASTER BOOT SEQUENCE


A normal FSD can only have one normal engineering blueprint.

The V1 therefore gets a special combination that a commander cannot reproduce
by normal engineering.


WHY IT IS GOOD FOR BEGINNERS
----------------------------

You do NOT need to unlock Felicity Farseer first.

You do NOT need to roll:

  G1
  G2
  G3
  G4
  G5


Instead:

  Gather four materials
      ->
  Visit a Human Technology Broker
      ->
  Exchange the materials
      ->
  Receive the FSD


It also normally gives a few LY more jump range than a normal 5A FSD with
Grade 5 Increased Range on the same ship.

Exact gain depends on:

  Ship
  Fuel
  Cargo
  Total mass
  Other modules


===============================================================================
 2. HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?
===============================================================================

CREDIT COST AT THE TECHNOLOGY BROKER:

  0 Cr


You pay with MATERIALS.


ONE FSD V1 costs:

  18 Datamined Wake Exceptions

  26 Tellurium

  26 Electrochemical Arrays

  28 Chemical Processors


TOTAL MATERIAL UNITS:

  98


IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a permanent unlock that lets you buy unlimited copies for credits.


EVERY ADDITIONAL FSD V1 requires the full material cost again.


===============================================================================
 3. CREDIT VALUE COMPARISON
===============================================================================

A normal stock:

  5A FRAME SHIFT DRIVE

costs approximately:

  5,103,953 Cr


The FSD V1 itself costs:

  0 Cr
  +
  Materials


So in direct module-purchase credits:

  You save roughly:

      5.1 MILLION Cr


The real "cost" is the time spent gathering the materials.


===============================================================================
 4. SHOULD A BEGINNER GET THIS OR ENGINEER A NORMAL FSD?
===============================================================================

IF YOUR SHIP USES A CLASS 5 FSD:

  GETTING THE FSD V1 MAKES A LOT OF SENSE.


Reasons:

  - Better range than a normal G5 5A FSD
  - No Engineer unlock required to obtain it
  - No random engineering rolls
  - Only four material types required
  - Can later receive an Experimental Effect
  - Can be moved between ships that use a Class 5 FSD


A normal engineered FSD still matters for ships that use:

  Class 2
  Class 3
  Class 4
  Class 6
  Class 7


The FSD V1 only exists as:

  CLASS 5A


===============================================================================
 5. WHICH SHIPS BENEFIT MOST?
===============================================================================

The FSD V1 makes the MOST sense on ships where jump range matters and the ship
uses a Class 5 FSD.


HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
------------------

  Diamondback Explorer

  Asp Explorer

  Krait Phantom


These are classic exploration ships and benefit enormously from the V1.


VERY USEFUL
-----------

  Krait Mk II

  Python

  Orca


These ships may not always be dedicated explorers, but the extra jump range is
excellent for:

  Travel
  Missions
  Material gathering
  Passenger work
  Bubble transport


SIMPLE RULE
-----------

If your ship's normal FSD slot is:

  CLASS 5

the V1 is normally worth considering.


If the ship needs another FSD class:

  It cannot use this module.

===============================================================================
 10. HOW TO FARM DAV'S HOPE
===============================================================================

1. Land near the settlement.

2. Deploy the SRV.

3. Open the SRV Cargo Scoop.

4. Drive around the settlement.

5. Collect every manufactured material you see.

6. Complete the loop.

7. Log out to:

     MAIN MENU

8. Log back in.

9. The loose materials respawn.

10. Repeat.


DIRECTLY WATCH FOR:

  Electrochemical Arrays

  Chemical Processors

  Galvanising Alloys


Dav's Hope can spawn all three.


Because the drops are random:

  COLLECT EVERYTHING.


If you finish with too much of one manufactured material and too little of
another:

  Use a Manufactured Material Trader.

===============================================================================
 17. IMPORTANT TRADE MATH
===============================================================================

Adaptive Encryptors Capture:

  G5


Datamined Wake Exceptions:

  G5


They are in DIFFERENT encoded families.


Cross-family same-grade exchange:

  6 : 1


Therefore:

  18 Datamined Wake Exceptions

requires:

  108 Adaptive Encryptors Capture


BUT:

The storage cap for a G5 encoded material is:

  100


So you cannot get all 18 DWE from one full 100-stack.


EASY METHOD
-----------

FIRST JAMESON FARM:

  Fill Adaptive Encryptors Capture close to 100.


At Ray Gateway:

  Trade:

      96 Adaptive Encryptors Capture

  for:

      16 Datamined Wake Exceptions


Then farm at least:

  12 more Adaptive Encryptors Capture


Trade those for:

  2 more Datamined Wake Exceptions


TOTAL:

  18 DWE

===============================================================================
 26. DO NOT ACCIDENTALLY DESTROY THE SPECIAL ENGINEERING
===============================================================================

VERY IMPORTANT:

The V1 has TWO special normal engineering effects.


DO NOT APPLY A NORMAL ENGINEERING BLUEPRINT TO IT.


If you overwrite it with something like:

  Normal Increased Range

or:

  Shielded

or:

  Faster Boot


you can remove the special double-engineered combination.


SAFE:

  ADDING AN EXPERIMENTAL EFFECT


NOT SAFE:

  APPLYING ANOTHER NORMAL BLUEPRINT

===============================================================================
 29. DO NOT USE DEEP CHARGE ON THE V1 FOR RANGE
===============================================================================

Deep Charge gives:

  +10% Maximum Fuel Per Jump


On smaller FSD classes it can sometimes produce a tiny single-jump advantage.


BUT:

The FSD V1 is:

  CLASS 5


For Class 5:

  MASS MANAGER gives better range.


It also avoids increasing the maximum fuel-per-jump figure.


BEGINNER RULE:

  5A FSD V1
      ->
  MASS MANAGER


===============================================================================
 30. HOW TO APPLY MASS MANAGER
===============================================================================

You need an Engineer who can work on Frame Shift Drives.


The easiest beginner Engineer is normally:

  FELICITY FARSEER


You do NOT need Felicity to GET the FSD V1.


You only need an Engineer if you want to ADD the Experimental Effect.


At the Engineer:

  1. Fit the FSD V1 to your ship.

  2. Open Frame Shift Drive engineering.

  3. Select an FSD blueprint screen.

  4. DO NOT PRESS APPLY on the normal blueprint.

  5. Choose:

       EXPERIMENTAL EFFECT

  6. Select:

       MASS MANAGER

  7. Apply it.


Mass Manager is safe to add.

It does NOT remove the V1's special double engineering.
