===============================================================================
 ELITE DANGEROUS: LEGACY 3.8 / CONSOLE
 BEGINNER FSS + DSS EXPLORATION SCANNING GUIDE
===============================================================================

This guide is specifically for:

  Elite Dangerous: Legacy / Console
  Horizons 3.8


The normal exploration scanning flow is:

  HONK
    ->
  FSS
    ->
  CHECK SYSTEM MAP
    ->
  DSS VALUABLE BODIES
    ->
  INVESTIGATE SURFACE SITES IF WANTED


===============================================================================
 1. THE THREE MAIN EXPLORATION SCANNERS
===============================================================================

DISCOVERY SCANNER
-----------------

Player nickname:

  HONK

Purpose:

  Detects the system's astronomical signals.

Use it:

  Every time you enter a new system.


FSS
---

Full Spectrum System Scanner.

Purpose:

  Identifies planets, moons and other system signals from a distance.


DSS
---

Detailed Surface Scanner.

Purpose:

  Maps an individual planet, moon or ring using probes.


Simple way to remember:

  HONK:
      "What signals are in this system?"

  FSS:
      "What are those signals?"

  DSS:
      "I want to fully map this body."


===============================================================================
 2. STEP 1 - HONK THE SYSTEM
===============================================================================

When you enter a new system:

  1. Stay in Supercruise.

  2. Enter Analysis Mode.

  3. Charge the Discovery Scanner.

  4. Complete the honk.


This gives you the system's basic signal information.


BEGINNER RULE:

  HONK EVERY SYSTEM.


===============================================================================
 3. STEP 2 - OPEN THE FSS
===============================================================================

After the honk:

  1. Stay in Supercruise.

  2. Throttle down.

  3. Open the Full Spectrum System Scanner.


The FSS lets you identify bodies without flying to each one.


===============================================================================
 4. HOW THE FSS WORKS
===============================================================================

At the bottom of the FSS screen is the:

  FREQUENCY SPECTRUM


Different body types appear in different parts of the spectrum.


Basic method:

  1. Move along the frequency bar.

  2. Find a signal.

  3. Move the targeting circle over it.

  4. Zoom in.

  5. Keep resolving the signal until the body is identified.


Once resolved:

  The body is FSS-scanned.


===============================================================================
 5. FSS SIGNAL TYPES TO WATCH FOR
===============================================================================

For exploration credits, pay special attention to:

  Earth-like Worlds

  Water Worlds

  Ammonia Worlds

  High Metal Content Worlds

  Metal-Rich Bodies


Also watch for:

  Notable Stellar Phenomena

  Unusual signal groups

  Multiple-star systems


===============================================================================
 6. DO YOU HAVE TO FSS EVERYTHING?
===============================================================================

No.


When learning:

  Scanning the whole system is useful practice.


Later:

  You can become selective.


Good reasons to FSS everything:

  - Learning the scanner
  - Hunting First Discoveries
  - Completion
  - Searching for unusual bodies
  - Looking for biological / geological surface sites


Good reasons to be selective:

  - Fast travel
  - Neutron Highway
  - Credit efficiency


===============================================================================
 7. STEP 3 - CHECK THE SYSTEM MAP
===============================================================================

After FSS-scanning interesting bodies:

  Open the System Map.


Check important planets for:

  TERRAFORMABILITY


Look for:

  "This body is a candidate for terraforming."


This matters especially for:

  Water Worlds

  High Metal Content Worlds

  Rocky Bodies


Terraformability can massively increase exploration value.


===============================================================================
 8. WHICH BODIES SHOULD A BEGINNER DSS?
===============================================================================

For good exploration payouts, prioritize:

  Earth-like Worlds

  Terraformable Water Worlds

  Terraformable High Metal Content Worlds

  Ammonia Worlds

  Terraformable Rocky Bodies

  Normal Water Worlds


Also worth considering:

  Metal-Rich Bodies

  Class II Gas Giants

  Normal High Metal Content Worlds


Usually not worth a long detour purely for credits:

  Icy Bodies

  Rocky-Ice Bodies

  Ordinary Rocky Bodies

  Most low-value Gas Giants


===============================================================================
 9. WHAT DO YOU NEED FOR DSS?
===============================================================================

You need:

  DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER


It is an Optional Internal module.


Normal price:

  250,000 Cr


Before leaving on an exploration trip:

  1. Fit the DSS.

  2. Assign it to a Fire Group.


Use it in:

  ANALYSIS MODE


The DSS has effectively unlimited probes.

You do NOT need to buy probe ammunition.


===============================================================================
 10. STEP 4 - FLY TO THE PLANET
===============================================================================

To DSS-map a body:

  1. Target it.

  2. Fly toward it in Supercruise.

  3. Throttle down as you approach.

  4. When close enough, enter Analysis Mode.

  5. Activate the DSS.


===============================================================================
 11. HOW TO MAP THE BODY
===============================================================================

The DSS fires probes at the planet.


Basic method:

  1. Fire probes around the visible side.

  2. Spread them out.

  3. Avoid hitting the same area repeatedly.

  4. Use far-side shots to reach behind the planet.

  5. Reach 90% coverage.


At:

  90% COVERAGE


the game automatically completes the map to:

  100%


You do NOT need to manually cover every tiny gap.


===============================================================================
 12. THE EFFICIENCY TARGET
===============================================================================

The DSS screen displays:

  EFFICIENCY TARGET


Example:

  Efficiency Target:
      6 Probes


If you map the body using no more than that number:

  You receive the efficiency bonus.


Legacy 3.8 bonus:

  x1.25 mapped payout


That is:

  +25%


===============================================================================
 13. WHAT IF YOU MISS THE EFFICIENCY TARGET?
===============================================================================

Finish the map anyway.


You still receive:

  - Normal DSS mapping payout
  - First Mapped bonus if eligible
  - Surface-site information if the body has sites


You only lose:

  The +25% efficiency bonus


===============================================================================
 14. HOW FAR-SIDE PROBES WORK
===============================================================================

This is the most important DSS skill.


If you aim:

  AT THE CENTRE

the probe hits the visible face.


If you aim:

  NEAR THE EDGE

the probe lands near the horizon.


If you aim:

  BEYOND THE EDGE

the planet's gravity bends the probe around the body.


If you aim too far:

  The DSS shows:

      MISS


For a far-side hit:

  Aim beyond the visible edge
  but
  stop before MISS appears.


===============================================================================
 15. SIMPLE BEGINNER PROBE METHOD
===============================================================================

Do not memorize complicated patterns at first.


Try this:

  1. Fire one or two probes at the visible face.

  2. Fire around the edges:
       Top
       Bottom
       Left
       Right

  3. Use far-side shots between the horizon and MISS.

  4. Check the opposite-side view.

  5. Fill the biggest uncovered gaps.


Think:

  EVEN COVERAGE

not:

  PERFECT PATTERN


===============================================================================
 16. USE THE FAR-SIDE VIEW
===============================================================================

The DSS interface lets you switch between:

  FRONT
  and
  BACK


Use this to see:

  - Which areas are already covered
  - Where the biggest gaps are
  - Whether your far-side shots worked


This is especially useful on:

  Large planets

  Gas Giants


===============================================================================
 17. YOU CAN FIRE PROBES QUICKLY
===============================================================================

You do not need to wait for one probe to land before firing the next.


You can:

  Aim
  Fire
  Aim
  Fire
  Aim
  Fire


Then watch the coverage update.


Probe supply is effectively:

  UNLIMITED


===============================================================================
 18. FIRST MAPPED BY
===============================================================================

If nobody has previously mapped the body and successfully sold the data:

  You may earn:

    FIRST MAPPED BY:
        YOUR COMMANDER NAME


Important:

  The tag is not permanently yours until the data is sold.


You must:

  1. Map the body.

  2. Survive.

  3. Sell the data.


===============================================================================
 19. FIRST DISCOVERED VS FIRST MAPPED
===============================================================================

FIRST DISCOVERED
----------------

You were the first commander to discover the body and sell the data.


FIRST MAPPED
------------

You were the first commander to DSS-map the body and sell the data.


You can get:

  First Discovered only

  First Mapped only

  or

  BOTH


===============================================================================
 20. LEGACY SURFACE POIs AFTER DSS MAPPING
===============================================================================

This is very important for console.


Legacy uses the Horizons surface-site system.


After DSS mapping a suitable landable body, exact surface targets can appear in
the Navigation Panel.


Examples:

  Geological Site 1

  Geological Site 2

  Biological Site 1

  Biological Site 2


You can:

  1. Select the numbered site.

  2. Fly directly to it.

  3. Land.

  4. Deploy the SRV.


===============================================================================
 21. WHAT CAN APPEAR AT LEGACY SURFACE SITES?
===============================================================================

GEOLOGICAL SITES
----------------

Examples:

  Geysers

  Lava Spouts


BIOLOGICAL SITES
----------------

Examples:

  Bark Mounds

  Brain Trees

  Sinuous Tubers

Not every landable planet has these sites.

===============================================================================
 23. MAPPING PLANETARY RINGS
===============================================================================

The DSS can also fire probes into planetary rings.


This reveals:

  MINING HOTSPOTS


Examples:

  Platinum

  Painite

  Low Temperature Diamonds


This is mainly useful for mining.

It does not make the planet's normal exploration scan value higher.


===============================================================================
 24. NORMAL DSS ENGINEERING
===============================================================================

The normal Legacy DSS can be engineered with:

  EXPANDED PROBE SCANNING RADIUS


This increases the area covered by each probe.


Benefits:

  - Easier mapping
  - Less probe overlap
  - Easier efficiency bonus


Felicity Farseer can engineer it to:

  Grade 3


Grade 3 gives:

  +30% Probe Radius


Higher Legacy engineers can take it to:

  Grade 5


Grade 5 gives:

  +50% Probe Radius


For beginners:

  A normal unengineered DSS is completely usable.

  Engineering is a useful upgrade, not a requirement.


===============================================================================
 25. COMMON FSS MISTAKES
===============================================================================

MISTAKE:

  Opening FSS while still flying quickly.

FIX:

  Throttle down first.


MISTAKE:

  Not honking first.

FIX:

  Honk the system before using FSS.


MISTAKE:

  Staying on the wrong part of the frequency bar.

FIX:

  Move along the spectrum until the signal becomes clear.


MISTAKE:

  Ignoring the System Map after scanning.

FIX:

  Check valuable worlds for terraformability.


===============================================================================
 26. COMMON DSS MISTAKES
===============================================================================

MISTAKE:

  DSS will not activate.

CHECK:

  [ ] Planet targeted

  [ ] In Supercruise

  [ ] Analysis Mode

  [ ] Close enough

  [ ] DSS assigned to Fire Group


MISTAKE:

  All probes hit the visible front side.

FIX:

  Aim beyond the horizon for far-side shots.


MISTAKE:

  Too much overlap.

FIX:

  Spread probes across new surface.


MISTAKE:

  Used too many probes.

FIX:

  Finish the map.
  You only lose the efficiency bonus.


MISTAKE:

  Trying to manually reach 100%.

FIX:

  90% completes the map automatically.


===============================================================================
 27. FULL BEGINNER SCANNING ROUTINE
===============================================================================

Every new system:


  1. HONK

  2. THROTTLE DOWN

  3. OPEN FSS

  4. IDENTIFY INTERESTING BODIES

  5. OPEN SYSTEM MAP

  6. CHECK FOR TERRAFORMABILITY

  7. TARGET VALUABLE PLANETS

  8. FLY TO THEM

  9. OPEN DSS

 10. CHECK EFFICIENCY TARGET

 11. FIRE EVENLY SPACED PROBES

 12. USE FAR-SIDE SHOTS

 13. REACH 90%

 14. LET THE MAP COMPLETE TO 100%

 15. CHECK NAVIGATION PANEL FOR SURFACE SITES

 16. CONTINUE EXPLORING

 17. SURVIVE

 18. SELL THE DATA


===============================================================================
 28. THE SIX THINGS TO REMEMBER
===============================================================================

1. HONK FINDS THE SYSTEM SIGNALS.

2. FSS IDENTIFIES PLANETS AND MOONS.

3. CHECK THE SYSTEM MAP FOR TERRAFORMABILITY.

4. DSS MAPS INDIVIDUAL BODIES.

5. 90% COVERAGE COMPLETES THE DSS MAP.

6. LEGACY SURFACE SITES APPEAR AS NUMBERED NAVIGATION TARGETS.
