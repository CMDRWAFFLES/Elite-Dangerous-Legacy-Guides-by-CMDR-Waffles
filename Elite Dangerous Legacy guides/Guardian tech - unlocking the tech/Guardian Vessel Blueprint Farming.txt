ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
GUARDIAN VESSEL BLUEPRINT FARMING
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
PURPOSE
===============================================================================

Guardian Vessel Blueprint Fragments are used to unlock the three Guardian
ship-launched fighters:

[ ] XG7 Trident — Plasma Autocannon
[ ] XG8 Javelin — Shard Cannon
[ ] XG9 Lance — Gauss Cannon

Each fighter is a separate broker unlock and requires its own Vessel Blueprint
Fragment.

Vessel farming has two stages:

1. Activate an orbital Guardian Beacon and collect an Ancient Key.

2. Use that Ancient Key at a Vessel Blueprint Guardian Structure.

===============================================================================
SHIP PREPARATION
===============================================================================

BRING:

[ ] A small or medium ship
[ ] At least one laser weapon
[ ] At least two tonnes of cargo capacity
[ ] Collector limpet controller and several limpets, strongly recommended
[ ] Data Link Scanner
[ ] Detailed Surface Scanner
[ ] SRV
[ ] Upper-mounted Point Defence
[ ] Fuel scoop
[ ] Night vision

The laser is required to activate the orbital beacon.

The collector limpet is not mandatory, but manually scooping an Ancient Key
near the beacon can be awkward.

===============================================================================
STAGE 1: OBTAIN AN ANCIENT KEY
===============================================================================

Guardian Beacon system: Synuefe KU-F b44-4
Beacon location:        Orbiting body 4

Enter the system, scan it and target the Guardian Beacon POI near body 4.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEACON ACTIVATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Approach the Guardian Beacon in normal space.
2. Allow the structure to unfold.
3. Locate its three projecting energy pylons.
4. Shoot the glowing section of each pylon with a laser.
5. Continue firing until all three pylons are fully charged.
6. Target the beacon’s central orb.
7. Scan it with the ship’s Data Link Scanner.
8. Wait for the beacon to eject an Ancient Key.
9. Collect the key with a collector limpet or scoop it manually.


The beacon also identifies the linked Guardian Structure containing the Vessel
Blueprint puzzle.

The Ancient Key occupies normal cargo space.

Collect one key for every Vessel Blueprint puzzle you intend to complete
because the key is consumed when deposited.

===============================================================================
STAGE 2: OBTAIN THE VESSEL BLUEPRINT
===============================================================================

System:             Synuefe EU-Q c21-15
Body:               A 1
Site type:          Guardian Structure
Layout:             Squid
Legacy coordinates: approximately +37.8059, −111.6111

This structure is linked to the Guardian Beacon in Synuefe KU-F b44-4.

===============================================================================
SURFACE PROCEDURE
===============================================================================

1. Land near the Guardian Structure.
2. Before deploying, make sure the Ancient Key is in the ship’s cargo hold.
3. Deploy the SRV.
4. Use the right-panel Inventory transfer option to move the Ancient Key into
   the SRV.
5. Drive around and destroy the Sentinels.
6. Raise and charge all six energy pylons.
7. Return to the activation pad in front of the Ancient Data Terminal.
8. Park over the illuminated pad.
9. Jettison the Ancient Key, not an Ancient Relic.
10. Target the orb that rises from the terminal.
11. Scan it with the SRV Data Link Scanner.


The scan awards one Guardian Vessel Blueprint Fragment.

===============================================================================
FARMING MULTIPLE VESSEL BLUEPRINTS
===============================================================================

Each surface puzzle consumes one Ancient Key.

To unlock all three fighters, obtain at least three Ancient Keys and complete
the structure three times.

The Guardian Beacon can be activated repeatedly, while the planetary structure
can be reset through a main-menu relog in Solo.

Keep spare cargo space so several Ancient Keys can be transported together.

Guardian fighters also require considerable Pattern Obelisk Data and Guardian
components at the Technology Broker.

Vessel Blueprint Fragments alone are not enough to complete the fighter
unlocks.
