Guardian technology: efficient Legacy plan

Guardian unlocks divide into three blueprint types:

Blueprint	                Used for	                                                Recommended site
Module Blueprint Fragment	FSD Booster, reinforcement modules, hybrid power modules	Synuefe NL-N c23-4 — B 3
Weapon Blueprint Fragment	Gauss, shard and plasma weapons	                                Synuefe EU-Q c21-10 — A 3
Vessel Blueprint Fragment	Guardian ship-launched fighters	                                Separate vessel site plus an Ancient Key

Each completed Guardian-site puzzle gives one blueprint fragment. The site layout determines which blueprint type it awards; Module and Weapon sites use an Ancient Relic, while Vessel sites require an Ancient Key obtained from an orbital Guardian Beacon.

**********************************************************

Recommended Legacy unlock order
1. Guardian Frame Shift Drive Booster
2. Class 2 Fixed Guardian Gauss Cannon, when preparing for Thargoid combat
3. Guardian Module Reinforcement Package
4. Guardian Shield Reinforcement Package
5. Guardian Shard Cannons or Plasma Chargers for specialized builds
6. Guardian Hybrid Power Distributor and Power Plant as convenient alternatives to engineering
7. Guardian fighters, mostly for enjoyment rather than efficiency

**********************************************************

The FSD Booster is the clear first unlock because one unlock makes all five module sizes available for purchase, providing between +4.00 and +10.50 ly depending on class.

First expedition: Guardian FSD Booster
What you need to unlock it
Requirement	                        Amount
Guardian Module Blueprint Fragment	1
Guardian Power Cell              	21
Guardian Technology Component   	21
Focus Crystals                   	24
HN Shock Mount commodities	        8

This is the Legacy-compatible Technology Broker recipe. Ignore any Live-only Guardian engineering, anti-Guardian-zone modifications or post-Update 14 additions.

Where each item comes from
*Module Blueprint Fragment: complete the Guardian puzzle once.
*Power Cells: destructible panels around the structure.
*Technology Components: destructible panels; these are usually the limiting Guardian material.
*Focus Crystals: ordinary manufactured engineering material—use your existing manufactured stock or a trader.
*HN Shock Mounts: purchasable market commodity. Buy these after returning to the Bubble if convenient.

**********************************************************

Recommended ship setup

A DBX, Asp Explorer, Krait Phantom, Cobra or similar small/medium ship is ideal.

Bring:

*Fuel scoop
*Detailed Surface Scanner
*Two-SRV hangar, strongly recommended
*Two Point Defence utility modules where possible
*Point Defence mounted on the upper surface of the ship
*Small shield
*Good jump range
*Materials for SRV fuel, ammunition and repair synthesis

Your ship’s Point Defence can shoot down Guardian Sentinel missiles while you are driving nearby, provided the weapons have a clear upward firing arc.

You do not need cargo space for the Module Blueprint puzzle. The Ancient Relic is carried by the SRV and used at the site.

**********************************************************

Module-blueprint location

System: Synuefe NL-N c23-4
Body: B 3
POI: Guardian Structure
Legacy structure coordinates: approximately −34.8383, +132.8863

This is a Turtle-layout site that awards Guardian Module Blueprint Fragments.

Map B 3 with the DSS and target the Guardian Structure, not one of the nearby Ancient Ruins. The body contains multiple Guardian POIs, but only the Guardian Structure contains the blueprint puzzle.

**********************************************************

What to do at the site
1. Before starting the timer
2. Land as close as practical, with the top-mounted Point Defence facing the structure.
3. Deploy the SRV.
4. Drive around and destroy Guardian Sentinels as they appear.
5. Shoot every targetable Destructible Panel.
6. Scoop Guardian Power Cells and Guardian Technology Components.
7. Locate all six energy-pylon positions.
8. Shoot a glowing relic tower and scoop one Ancient Relic.

Destructible panels and destroyed Sentinels provide the Guardian manufactured materials needed for broker unlocks.

**********************************************************

Complete the puzzle
1. Approach the main Ancient Data Terminal to raise its nearby pylons.
2. Raise all six pylons by driving close to their triangular bases.
3. Shoot the glowing centre of each pylon until it becomes fully charged and sends a beam upward.
4. Charge all six before the timer expires.
5. Return to the illuminated triangular pad in front of the terminal.
6. Park directly over it.
7. In the SRV Inventory panel, jettison one Ancient Relic.
8. Target the orb that rises from the terminal.
9. Scan it with the SRV Data Link Scanner.

You will receive one Guardian Module Blueprint Fragment. The timer gains additional time as pylons are charged, so locating them before beginning makes the run much easier.

**********************************************************

Farming additional materials

After completing the run:

1. Return to the main menu.
2. Log back into Solo.
3. Panels, Sentinels, relics and the blueprint puzzle reset.
4. Repeat until you have the Guardian materials needed for your intended unlocks.

For the FSD Booster alone, you only need to solve the blueprint puzzle once, but you may need several panel-reset loops to reach 21 Guardian Technology Components.

**********************************************************

Returning to the Bubble

At a normal Guardian Technology Broker:

1. Ensure the eight HN Shock Mounts are aboard your ship.
2. Open Technology Broker services.
3. Unlock the Guardian FSD Booster.
4. Enter Outfitting and purchase the desired class with credits.

The unlock is permanent and covers Classes 1–5. It does not award a free module; it makes the Booster purchasable from stations carrying Guardian technology.

**********************************************************

Efficient broader expedition

While you are already in Guardian space, the efficient approach is to collect:

At least six Module Blueprint Fragments if you expect to unlock most useful Guardian modules
*A large reserve of Guardian Technology Components
*Guardian Power Cells and Power Conduits
*Guardian Sentinel Weapon Parts
*Guardian Wreckage Components
*Pattern Alpha through Epsilon Obelisk Data

However, for the immediate trip, prioritize the FSD Booster requirements rather than trying to fill every Guardian-material bin before you know which modules you actually want.
