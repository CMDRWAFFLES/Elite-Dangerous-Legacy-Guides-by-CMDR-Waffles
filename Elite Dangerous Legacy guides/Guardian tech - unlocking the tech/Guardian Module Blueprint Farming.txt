Guardian Module Blueprint Farming
Elite Dangerous Legacy — Xbox and PlayStation

**********************************************************

Purpose

Guardian Module Blueprint Fragments unlock the following standard Guardian modules at a Guardian Technology Broker:

Guardian Frame Shift Drive Booster
Guardian Module Reinforcement Package
Guardian Shield Reinforcement Package
Guardian Hull Reinforcement Package
Guardian Hybrid Power Plant
Guardian Hybrid Power Distributor

Each separate module unlock requires one Module Blueprint Fragment. Therefore, collect one for only the FSD Booster, three for the FSD Booster plus the two most useful reinforcement modules, or six to unlock every standard Legacy Guardian module.

**********************************************************

Recommended location

System: Synuefe NL-N c23-4
Body: B 3
Site type: Guardian Structure
Layout: Turtle
Legacy coordinates: approximately −34.8383, +132.8863

Map the body with a Detailed Surface Scanner and select the Guardian Structure. Do not accidentally land at one of the Ancient Ruins on the same body.

**********************************************************

Bring
An SRV
Detailed Surface Scanner
Fuel scoop
Small or medium ship
One or two Point Defence modules mounted on the upper surface
SRV repair, refuel and ammunition synthesis materials
Night vision
A two-SRV hangar is recommended but not mandatory

Top-mounted Point Defence can intercept missiles launched at your SRV while your ship is parked near the structure. Park outside the ruins so your ship does not interfere with the SRV turret.

Blueprint procedure
1. Land close enough for Point Defence coverage.
2. Deploy the SRV.
3. Drive around the structure and destroy the Guardian Sentinels.
4. Shoot a glowing relic tower.
5. Scoop the dropped Ancient Relic into the SRV.
6. Locate the Ancient Data Terminal at the head of the Turtle layout.
7. Approach the trigger area so the first pylons rise.
8. Drive to all six pylon positions and make each pylon rise.
9. Shoot the glowing blue section of every pylon until it is fully charged.
10. After all six are charged, return to the triangular activation pad in front of the terminal.
11. Park the SRV over the illuminated pad.
12. Open the SRV Inventory and jettison one Ancient Relic.
13. Target the orb that rises from the terminal.
14. Scan the orb using the SRV Data Link Scanner.

The scan awards one Guardian Module Blueprint Fragment. The timer begins when the first pylon is charged, and each additional charged pylon adds time. The timer disappears once all six are active.

**********************************************************

Farming additional fragments

Return to the main menu and reload in Solo. The puzzle, relics, destructible panels and Sentinels reset.

Repeat the full puzzle once for each additional Module Blueprint Fragment required.

While farming, shoot the targetable destructible panels around the site and scoop:

Guardian Technology Components
Guardian Power Cells
Guardian Power Conduits
Guardian Sentinel Weapon Parts
Guardian Wreckage Components

Guardian Technology Components are particularly valuable and are often the limiting material for Guardian broker unlocks.
