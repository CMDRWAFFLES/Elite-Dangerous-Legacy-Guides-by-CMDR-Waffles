ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
GUARDIAN WEAPON BLUEPRINT FARMING
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
PURPOSE
===============================================================================

Guardian Weapon Blueprint Fragments unlock standard Legacy Guardian weapons,
including variants of:

[ ] Guardian Gauss Cannon
[ ] Guardian Shard Cannon
[ ] Guardian Plasma Charger

Each class and mounting variant listed by the normal Guardian Technology Broker
is a separate unlock and normally requires one Weapon Blueprint Fragment.

Modified Guardian weapons associated with later Live-PC content are not part of
this Legacy guide.

===============================================================================
RECOMMENDED LOCATION
===============================================================================

System:             Synuefe EU-Q c21-10
Body:               A 3
Site type:          Guardian Structure
Layout:             Bear
Legacy coordinates: approximately −23.2718, +126.3758

Map A 3 with the Detailed Surface Scanner and target the Guardian Structure.

The Bear layout has three obvious corner legs, with the fourth corner
comparatively open.

===============================================================================
BRING
===============================================================================

[ ] An SRV
[ ] Detailed Surface Scanner
[ ] Fuel scoop
[ ] Small or medium ship
[ ] Upper-mounted Point Defence
[ ] SRV repair, ammunition and refuel synthesis materials
[ ] Night vision
[ ] Two-SRV hangar recommended

No ship-mounted weapon or cargo rack is required for a Weapon Blueprint puzzle.

===============================================================================
BLUEPRINT PROCEDURE
===============================================================================

1. Land near the edge of the structure.
2. Deploy the SRV.
3. Drive around the outer legs and destroy the Sentinels before starting the
   timer.
4. Shoot one of the glowing relic towers.
5. Scoop one Ancient Relic.
6. Locate the Ancient Data Terminal at the head of the Bear layout.
7. Drive near each of the six pylon positions so they rise.
8. Shoot the glowing section of every pylon until fully charged.
9. Return to the activation pad in front of the terminal.
10. Park over the illuminated triangle.
11. Jettison the Ancient Relic from the SRV Inventory.
12. Target the raised terminal orb.
13. Scan it with the SRV Data Link Scanner.


You receive one Guardian Weapon Blueprint Fragment per completed puzzle.

===============================================================================
FARMING ADDITIONAL FRAGMENTS
===============================================================================

Return to the main menu, reload in Solo and repeat the puzzle.

The Weapon site is generally easier than the Turtle Module site because it has
fewer Sentinel encounters and a comparatively straightforward layout.

One blueprint is required for each separate weapon unlock, so commanders
intending to unlock several sizes or mounting types should repeat the site
multiple times.

Destroy the Sentinels and scoop their drops while farming.

Guardian Sentinel Weapon Parts are needed for some weapon and fighter broker
unlocks.
