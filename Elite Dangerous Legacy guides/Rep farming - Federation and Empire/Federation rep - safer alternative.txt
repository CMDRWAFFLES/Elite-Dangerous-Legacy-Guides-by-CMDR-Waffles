Safer Federation alternative: Ceos ↔ Sothis

Use the Ceos–Sothis courier loop when:

You do not have a large cargo ship.
You dislike interdictions.
Canopus–Exphiay has poor Legacy mission boards.
You want a low-risk grind similar to Ngalinn–Mainani.

Stack Federal courier missions between Ceos and Sothis and select Reputation rewards. It is much safer, but usually slower and more sensitive to local faction states and mission-board quality. Historical Horizons players reported the route becoming less productive even before Odyssey, so I would treat it as the fallback rather than the primary Federal grind.

Important rank rules

Only missions issued by a minor faction carrying the relevant Empire or Federation symbol contribute to that superpower’s naval progression.

Always choose:

REP+++++ > REP++++ > REP+++ > REP++ > REP+

when rank speed matters more than credits or materials.

When your naval-rank bar reaches 100%, look for a mission whose title or description mentions:

Imperial Navy/Naval
Federal Navy/Naval

Completing it awards the next rank. You can continue grinding while sitting at 100%; additional rank progress is stored invisibly and carries into subsequent ranks.

Recommended order
1. Run Ngalinn–Mainani to reach Imperial Duke.
2. Buy and outfit a Cutter for cargo.
2. Run Canopus–Exphiay to Federal Rear Admiral.
3. Take naval promotion missions whenever convenient, but do not stop grinding merely because the displayed bar has reached 100%.
