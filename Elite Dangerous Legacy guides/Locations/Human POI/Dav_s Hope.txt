USE
---
Dav's Hope is mostly useful for easy lower/mid-grade manufactured materials.
For grade-5 efficiency, HGE farming plus material trading is usually better.


1. DSS the body if necessary.
2. Select Dav's Hope from the Horizons Navigation panel POI list.
3. Land and deploy SRV.
4. Drive the loop around the settlement, scooping manufactured materials.
5. Scan the data points if desired for lore.
6. Log to MAIN MENU.
7. Return and repeat the material loop.
