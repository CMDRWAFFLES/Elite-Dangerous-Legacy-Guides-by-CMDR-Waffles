LISTENING POSTS — LEGACY 3.8
============================

WHAT LISTENING POSTS ARE
========================
Listening Posts are scannable space beacons used for environmental-storytelling puzzles.
Many old Horizons chains use multiple posts: each message supplies partial range/body/coordinate clues that can be combined to locate a wreck, base, 
megaship or other hidden site.
