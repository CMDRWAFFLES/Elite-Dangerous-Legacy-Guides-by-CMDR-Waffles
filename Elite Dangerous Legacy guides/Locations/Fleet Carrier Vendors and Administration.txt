WHAT THE TWO TYPES DO
=====================

Fleet Carrier Vendor
--------------------
- Purchase a new Drake-Class Fleet Carrier.
- Base purchase price: 5,000,000,000 CR.
- Vendor systems also have Fleet Carrier Administration.


Fleet Carrier Administration
-----------------------------
Used for a Fleet Carrier you already own.

Used to:
- Install optional Fleet Carrier services.
- Remove optional Fleet Carrier services.
- Change service crews where applicable.
- Buy or remove Shipyard stock packages.
- Buy or remove Outfitting stock packages.

Suspending or reactivating an already-installed service does not
require returning to a Carrier Administration system.
