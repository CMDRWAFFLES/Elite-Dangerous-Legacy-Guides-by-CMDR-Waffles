ROBIGO / LEGACY MONEY-MAKING METHODS
====================================

1. ROBIGO MINES -> SIRIUS ATMOSPHERICS
======================================

What it is
----------
Stack sightseeing passenger missions at Robigo Mines that all visit the
Sirius Atmospherics tourist beacon in Sothis, scan the beacon, then return to
Robigo Mines and turn every mission in.

Recommended ship
----------------
Python.

Why the Python:
- Robigo Mines is an outpost, so the largest usable pad is Medium.
- The Python carries a large number of passenger cabins while still fitting a
  Medium pad.

Useful setup
------------
- Passenger cabins in most optional slots.
- Business cabins are useful in the large cabin slots.
- Economy cabins fill the smaller slots efficiently.
- Aim for at least about 30.2 ly loaded jump range.
- Set route plotting to FASTEST, not Economical.

With enough range, Robigo -> Sothis is normally two jumps each way.

The loop
--------
1. Dock at Robigo Mines in Robigo.
2. Open the Passenger Lounge.
3. Take sightseeing/VIP missions whose destination is Sirius Atmospherics.
4. Jump to Sothis.
5. Head toward Sothis A 5.
6. If necessary, honk the system. The Sirius Atmospherics POI appears in the
   Navigation panel when close enough to A 5.
7. Target Sirius Atmospherics and drop from supercruise.
8. In normal space, target the tourist beacon and point the ship at it.
   The normal ship scanner completes the scan automatically.
9. Wait for the mission-complete messages.
10. Return directly to Robigo Mines.
11. Turn in every passenger mission and repeat.

Reputation
----------
At first, favour REP rewards until you are Allied with the local mission
factions. Better reputation produces the high-value passenger missions.

Useful mission rewards
----------------------
Robigo can also provide valuable engineering materials as mission rewards,
including:
- Biotech Conductors
- Exquisite Focus Crystals
- Modified Embedded Firmware

Historical Legacy-era income
----------------------------
Optimised commanders commonly reported roughly 90-120 million credits per hour
in 2021-2022. Actual Legacy mission boards can be better or worse depending on
the Legacy BGS and your faction reputation.

Important
---------
Do not use current Live mission/BGS information to judge Robigo on console.
The physical Robigo -> Sothis geometry is valid; the mission board is Legacy.
