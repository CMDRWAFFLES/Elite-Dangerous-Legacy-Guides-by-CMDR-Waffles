ELITE DANGEROUS LEGACY CONSOLE
NEUTRON HIGHWAY: THE BUBBLE TO COLONIA
Xbox / PlayStation — Horizons 3.8 / Pre-Update 14

===============================================================================
SCOPE
===============================================================================

This guide covers Legacy neutron-star travel from the Bubble to Colonia:
ship choice, outfitting, route plotting, safe cone entry, fuel management,
repairs, emergencies and permanent Horizons-era repair stops.

Purpose
The purpose of using the neutron highway is to travel very long distances much faster by using neutron stars to temporarily supercharge your Frame Shift Drive.

In Elite Dangerous Legacy, a neutron-star supercharge gives your ship roughly four times its normal jump range for one jump.

For example:

A 40 LY ship can jump about 160 LY.
A 50 LY ship can jump about 200 LY.
A 65 LY ship can jump about 260 LY.

This greatly reduces the number of jumps needed between the Bubble and Colonia. Instead of making hundreds of ordinary jumps, you travel through a chain of neutron stars and make much longer jumps between them.

Why use it?

The neutron highway is most useful when:

Travelling quickly between the Bubble and Colonia
Reaching Sagittarius A* or other distant destinations
Returning from an expedition quickly
Moving an exploration ship across the galaxy
Reducing repetitive jump-and-scoop travel

It is mainly a travel-speed method, not an exploration-profit method. Because you skip across larger distances, you visit fewer systems and therefore collect less exploration data than someone travelling normally and scanning everything.

Not included:
- SCO Frame Shift Drives
- Mandalay or other Live-only ships
- Odyssey mechanics
- Post-Update 14 neutron changes

===============================================================================
HOW THE NEUTRON HIGHWAY WORKS
===============================================================================

A Fuel Scoop lets the ship collect exotic particles from a neutron-star jet
while remaining in supercruise.

LEGACY BOOST VALUES

Neutron star:
- +300% range
- 4 times normal jump range
- Lasts for one hyperspace jump

White dwarf:
- +50% range
- 1.5 times normal jump range
- Much larger exclusion zone relative to the cone

Example:

Normal range: 50 LY
Neutron-supercharged range: about 200 LY

Each supercharge causes roughly 1% Frame Shift Drive integrity damage.

Neutron stars and white dwarfs do not normally refill the fuel tank. Refuel at:

    K G B F O A M

Remember:

    KGB FOAM

===============================================================================
ENGINEERING PRIORITIES
===============================================================================

1. Frame Shift Drive
   - Increased Range
   - Highest available grade
   - Mass Manager is the common exploration Experimental Effect on larger FSDs

2. Guardian Frame Shift Drive Booster
   - Fit the largest practical class

3. Lightweight modules
   - D-rated Life Support
   - D-rated Sensors
   - Lightweight engineering where useful

4. Power Plant
   - Smallest A-rated plant that safely powers the build
   - Armoured with Thermal Spread is a robust exploration choice
   - Avoid making the Power Plant needlessly fragile; it cannot be field-repaired

5. Thrusters
   - D-rated saves mass
   - Do not undersize them so much that planetary landing becomes unsafe

6. Shield
   - A small D-rated shield is strongly recommended for landing and docking

===============================================================================
ROUTE PLOTTING — IN-GAME METHOD
===============================================================================

Open:

    Galaxy Map
      → Route Settings
      → Fastest Routes
      → Use Jet-Cone Boost

The Legacy in-game route plotter is limited to approximately 20,000 LY.

The Bubble-to-Colonia journey is approximately 22,000 LY.

Plot the journey in at least two legs.

Good midpoint choices:
- Sacaqawea Space Port
- Gagarin Gate
- Caravanserai
- Another known system around the middle of the route

STAR FILTERS

Include:
- K, G, B, F, O, A and M stars
- Non-Sequence stars

Where practical:
- Exclude white-dwarf categories
- Keep Use Jet-Cone Boost enabled
- Use Fastest Routes

Do not restrict the route to only scoopable stars while expecting neutron use.

===============================================================================
ROUTE PLOTTING — EXTERNAL NEUTRON ROUTER
===============================================================================

A neutron router such as the Spansh Neutron Plotter can produce a more
neutron-heavy route than the in-game plotter.

For Legacy:
- Enter the exact start system
- Enter Colonia as destination
- Enter the ship's actual Legacy jump range manually
- Use a slightly conservative range if uncertain
- Ignore Live-only ship and SCO options
- Use the generated star-system names only

Neutron-star positions are static galaxy data, so manually entering the Legacy
ship range makes this type of route usable on console.

Console disadvantage:
- More manual Galaxy Map entry
- Fuel stops still need to be planned
- AFMU repairs still need to be managed

The in-game plotter is easier.

The external router is usually faster.

===============================================================================
SAFE NEUTRON SUPERCHARGE PROCEDURE
===============================================================================

STEP 1 — ARRIVE AT ZERO THROTTLE

During the hyperspace jump, hold throttle-down so the ship enters the neutron
system at minimum supercruise speed.

Do not arrive at full throttle.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 2 — USE THE OUTER END OF THE CONE

Choose the wide outer third of either jet.

Avoid:
- The narrow cone close to the star
- The exclusion-zone boundary
- A path aimed toward the star
- Crossing the cone at a steep angle

The safest direction is:

    ENTER NEAR THE OUTER END
    TRAVEL GENERALLY AWAY FROM THE STAR

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 — APPROACH AT A SHALLOW ANGLE

Aim roughly along the cone rather than straight across it.

Use low or moderate throttle.

A good setup has:
- The star behind and to one side
- The nose pointed toward open space
- The ship entering near the wide end

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 — ENTER AND REDUCE THROTTLE

Inside the cone:
- The cockpit shakes
- Controls become unstable
- The FSD warning appears
- The Fuel Scoop begins collecting particles

Reduce throttle to minimum once safely inside.

Make small corrections.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 5 — WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION

Do not leave after the first safety warning.

Wait until the ship announces:

    FRAME SHIFT DRIVE SUPERCHARGED

The warning means charging has started.

The supercharged message means the boost is complete.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 6 — EXIT AWAY FROM THE STAR

Increase throttle and fly toward open space.

Leave the turbulence before aligning for the next jump.

Never drop from supercruise inside the cone.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 7 — MAKE THE BOOSTED JUMP

If following an in-game route:
- Select the next route target
- Align
- Jump

If routing manually:
- Select a system within boosted range
- Plot the jump
- Confirm the route accepts it
- Jump

The boost is consumed by that hyperspace jump.

===============================================================================
FSD DAMAGE AND REPAIR
===============================================================================

Each supercharge causes roughly 1% FSD damage.

Recommended repair point:

    ABOUT 90%

Practical warning:

    AVOID GOING BELOW 80%

Below approximately 80%, the FSD can begin to malfunction.

Malfunctions can:
- Cancel charging
- Delay a jump
- Require another attempt
- Become dangerous near a jet

HOW TO REPAIR THE FSD

1. Leave the cone.
2. Move away from the star.
3. Drop to normal space.
4. Stop the ship.
5. Open the right-hand Modules panel.
6. Enable the AFMU.
7. Select the Frame Shift Drive.
8. Choose REPAIR.
9. Wait until the desired integrity is restored.
10. Stop repair.
11. Re-enable the FSD.
12. Disable the AFMU if normally left off.
13. Return to supercruise.

DO NOT REPAIR THE FSD WHILE IN SUPERCRUISE.

Repairing the FSD switches it off and can cause an emergency drop.

===============================================================================
FUEL MANAGEMENT
===============================================================================

Neutron stars do not normally refuel the ship.

Every boosted jump still consumes ordinary hyperspace fuel.

SAFE RULE:

Do not continue a neutron chain with only one normal jump remaining.

Recommended:
- Refuel before dropping below two normal jumps
- Keep enough reserve to divert to KGBFOAM
- Check the fuel line before every boosted jump

If low:
1. Stop following the neutron route.
2. Filter the Galaxy Map to KGBFOAM.
3. Plot to a reachable fuel star.
4. Refuel fully.
5. Replot toward the next neutron waypoint.

Some neutron systems have scoopable companion stars.

Never assume that every neutron system does.

===============================================================================
HULL, CANOPY AND POWER PLANT REPAIR
===============================================================================

HULL

Repair with:
- Repair Limpets
- Station or surface-port repair

The AFMU cannot repair hull.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CANOPY

A damaged but unbroken canopy can be repaired with an AFMU.

A completely breached canopy requires a port.

After a breach:
- Life Support begins counting down
- Fly to the nearest repair facility
- Synthesize oxygen before the timer becomes critically low

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

POWER PLANT

Cannot be repaired by:
- AFMU
- Repair Limpets

It requires:
- Station repair
- Surface-port repair
- A suitable carrier service if independently available in Legacy

Do not rely on player carriers for a planned route.

===============================================================================
THE COLONIA CONNECTION HIGHWAY
===============================================================================

The Colonia Connection Highway is a chain of permanent Horizons-era ports
between the Bubble and Colonia.

Use them to:
- Repair hull
- Repair Power Plant
- Restore modules
- Refill AFMU ammunition
- Restock limpets
- Sell exploration data
- Stop safely between sessions

It is not the same as the neutron highway.

The neutron highway is a travel technique.

The Colonia Connection Highway is a line of inhabited service stops.

===============================================================================
EMERGENCIES
===============================================================================

CAUGHT IN THE CONE BUT STILL IN SUPERCRUISE

1. Do not drop.
2. Point away from the star.
3. Use small control inputs.
4. Fly toward the nearest outer edge.
5. Exit the cone before trying to align for hyperspace.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EMERGENCY-DROPPED INSIDE THE CONE

1. Stay calm.
2. Use a Heat Sink if overheating.
3. Select SUPERCRUISE rather than a distant hyperspace target.
4. Charge the FSD.
5. Point toward open space.
6. Enter supercruise as soon as alignment permits.
7. Escape the cone.
8. Move away from the star.
9. Inspect hull, canopy, FSD and Power Plant.
10. Repair everything possible.

Normal-space exposure inside a neutron jet causes severe module and hull damage.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FSD MALFUNCTION

1. Move away from the star.
2. Drop to safe normal space.
3. Repair the FSD with the AFMU.
4. Restore it above 90%.
5. Continue only after confirming normal operation.

If the FSD reaches 0%:
- Try Reboot/Repair
- Use the AFMU once the module is restored above 0%
- Seek player rescue if necessary

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOW FUEL

If a scoopable star is reachable:
- Stop the neutron route
- Plot to KGBFOAM
- Use an FSD Injection if required
- Refuel completely

If none is reachable:
- Drop to normal space
- Switch off nonessential modules
- Stop wasting fuel in supercruise
- Log out if necessary
- Seek a Legacy-compatible fuel rescue

Do not immediately self-destruct.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LOW HULL

Above 80%:
- Continue cautiously

50–80%:
- Use repair limpets
- Consider a Highway port

Below 50%:
- Stop unnecessary neutron use
- Avoid planetary landing
- Repair immediately

Critical:
- Travel only to the nearest safe repair facility

===============================================================================
COMMON MISTAKES
===============================================================================

1. Pointing toward the star inside the cone
   - Enter at the outer end while moving away

2. Treating a white dwarf like a neutron star
   - White dwarfs are riskier and only give 1.5× in Legacy

3. Leaving after the first warning
   - Wait for FRAME SHIFT DRIVE SUPERCHARGED

4. Forgetting the Fuel Scoop
   - It is required for supercharging

5. Forgetting neutrons do not refuel the tank
   - Divert to KGBFOAM regularly

6. Letting FSD integrity fall below 80%
   - Repair around 90%

7. Repairing FSD in supercruise
   - Drop to normal space first

8. Assuming the AFMU repairs hull
   - Use Repair Limpets or a port

9. Assuming the AFMU repairs Power Plant
   - Use a port

10. Plotting Bubble to Colonia in one in-game route
    - Legacy route limit is about 20,000 LY; use multiple legs

11. Using empty-tank range in a route planner
    - Use current laden range

12. Continuing while tired
    - Stop at a port or safe normal space
