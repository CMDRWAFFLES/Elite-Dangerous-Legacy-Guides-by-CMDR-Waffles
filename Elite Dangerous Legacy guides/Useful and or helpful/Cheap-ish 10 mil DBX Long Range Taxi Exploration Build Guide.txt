===============================================================================
BUILD PURPOSE
===============================================================================

This Diamondback Explorer is a lightweight, long-range utility ship designed
primarily for travelling rather than fighting.

Its strongest roles are:

- General Bubble taxi
- Engineer-unlock travel
- Data courier missions
- Federal and Imperial courier rank loops
- Guardian-site trips
- Engineering-material expeditions
- Rare-good collection in small quantities
- Surface-site visits with an SRV
- Short and medium exploration journeys
- Long-distance travel after adding an AFMU 
- Neutron-assisted travel after adding an AFMU (See Legacy Neutron Highway Bubble to Colonia Guide for more information)
- Visiting permit systems, Technology Brokers and Material Traders

Useful info for newer CMDRS:
Unengineered 36 LY jumprange 
Engineered 63 LY jumprange 
Engineered + Guardian FSD 72 LY jumprange

The build combines:

- A Grade 5 Increased Range 5A Frame Shift Drive 
- Mass Manager Experimental Effect
- Class 4 Guardian FSD Booster
- 32-tonne fuel tank
- Class 4A Fuel Scoop
- Small shield
- Eight tonnes of cargo space
- One SRV
- One Heat Sink Launcher
- No weapons

This makes it fast, economical and convenient, but not combat-capable.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BULKHEADS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LIGHTWEIGHT ALLOY

Purpose:
- Keeps the ship as light as possible
- Maximises jump range
- Costs nothing to replace because it is the standard hull armour

Strength:
- Best choice for a dedicated taxi or explorer

Weakness:
- Minimal protection in combat or after shield failure

Recommended engineering:
- Heavy Duty
- Deep Plating

Heavy Duty engineering on Lightweight Alloy adds useful hull strength without
the normal mass penalty because the base armour has zero mass.

This is optional. The ship should still avoid combat.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POWER PLANT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3A POWER PLANT

The DBX has a Class 4 Power Plant slot, but this build uses an undersized 3A
plant.

Purpose:
- Saves mass
- Provides enough power for the lightweight travel equipment
- A-rating gives good heat efficiency

Why it suits the build:
- The ship has no weapons
- Most optional equipment has modest power requirements
- AFMUs and repair equipment can be disabled until needed



-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THRUSTERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4D THRUSTERS

Purpose:
- Low mass
- Adequate normal flight and planetary landing performance
- Preserves jump range

Strength:
- Appropriate for a travel ship

Weakness:
- Not designed for aggressive combat manoeuvring
- Care is required on high-gravity planets

Recommended engineering options:

Taxi and escape focus:
- Dirty Drive Tuning
- Drag Drives

Cooler exploration focus:
- Clean Drive Tuning
- Drive Distributors or Stripped Down

Dirty Drives are useful for escaping interdictions, but increase thermal load.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FRAME SHIFT DRIVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5A FRAME SHIFT DRIVE

Engineering encoded in the shared build:
- Grade 5 Increased Range
- Mass Manager Experimental Effect

This is the heart of the build.

Purpose:
- Provides the ship's long normal jump range
- Reduces travel time between engineers, material sites and distant systems
- Combines with the Guardian FSD Booster

The build uses the normal engineerable Legacy 5A FSD.

It does not rely on:
- SCO
- Live-only FSD technology
- Odyssey mechanics

Recommended use:
- Keep this module at 100% integrity before major journeys
- Add an AFMU before repeated neutron-star boosting
- Never sell it accidentally when changing ships

3D LIFE SUPPORT

- Provides normal cockpit life support

Strength:
- Standard exploration and taxi choice

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POWER DISTRIBUTOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3D POWER DISTRIBUTOR

The ship has a Class 4 slot, but this build uses an undersized 3D distributor.

Purpose:
- Saves mass
- Retains enough engine capacitor capacity to boost
- Suitable because the ship has no weapons

Strength:
- Efficient mass-saving choice

Weakness:

- Poor weapon and systems endurance

Recommended engineering:

Travel and escape:

- Cluster Capacitors

- Charge Enhanced

The main requirement is preserving the ability to boost.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLASS 4 SLOT — 4A FUEL SCOOP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
- Refuels from K, G, B, F, O, A and M stars
- Allows indefinite travel without buying fuel
- Required for neutron-star FSD supercharging

Strength:
- Largest Fuel Scoop the DBX can fit

Limitation:
- The DBX cannot fit the very large scoops available to an Asp Explorer, Krait
  Phantom or Anaconda
- Refuelling can therefore feel slower on repeated long journeys

Practical method:
- Begin scooping immediately after arrival
- Arc around the star while aligning with the next jump
- Leave before excessive heat accumulates

The Fuel Scoop should remain fitted for nearly every role in this guide.

===============================================================================
HOW TO FLY IT
===============================================================================

NORMAL TRAVEL

1. Plot using Fastest Routes.
2. Keep scoopable stars enabled.
3. Scoop while turning toward the next destination.
4. Avoid carrying unnecessary cargo.
5. Use Supercruise Assist only as a convenience, not a substitute for attention.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INTERDICTION ESCAPE

1. Submit intentionally.
2. Put four pips to ENG and two to SYS.
3. Boost.
4. Keep boosting as the distributor permits.
5. Charge the FSD.
6. High-wake if mass-locked.

Do not deploy hardpoints because none are fitted.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PLANETARY LANDING

1. Check gravity before descent.
2. Keep the shield active.
3. Approach slowly.
4. Avoid steep descents on high-gravity worlds.
5. Land close enough to the target to reduce SRV driving.
6. Keep enough fuel to return to orbit and reach a scoopable star.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NEUTRON USE

Only after fitting an AFMU:

1. Enter the outer part of the neutron cone in supercruise.
2. Travel generally away from the star.
3. Wait for FRAME SHIFT DRIVE SUPERCHARGED.
4. Exit the cone.
5. Make the boosted jump.
6. Repair the FSD around 90% integrity.
7. Avoid allowing it to fall below 80%.

FINAL ASSESSMENT
===============================================================================

OVERALL ROLE:

    LONG-RANGE LEGACY CONSOLE UTILITY TAXI

EXCELLENT FOR:
- Engineer unlocking
- Courier missions
- Federal and Imperial courier loops
- Guardian technology trips
- Material-farming travel
- Small rare-good collections
- Surface POIs
- General Bubble movement
- Short and medium exploration
- Long exploration after adding DSS and AFMU

POOR FOR:
- Combat
- Bulk cargo
- Passenger work
- Mining
- AX combat
- Repeated neutron travel without an AFMU

The build's greatest strength is that it removes travel friction.

It is inexpensive compared with larger exploration ships, lands almost
anywhere, reaches distant systems quickly, carries an SRV and enough cargo for
small objectives, and can be adapted with the empty slots without rebuilding
the entire ship.

The two most important additions are:

    1I DETAILED SURFACE SCANNER
    2A AUTO FIELD-MAINTENANCE UNIT

With those installed, it becomes a genuinely strong exploration and
engineering-progression ship rather than only a fast taxi.
